# The First Copper

<!-- STEP id="copper" -->
## A New Metal in the Toolkit

Until now, every tool in this chapter has been made of stone, bone or wood. The people of Mehrgarh were the first in the subcontinent to make copper objects, and copper is worked from a rock called an ore.

**With that step, they entered the metal age.**

<!-- STEP id="chalco" -->
## Copper and Stone Together

Copper did not replace stone. People used the two side by side, and the period has a name built from those two materials. **Chalcolithic** joins *chalco*, copper, with *lithic*, stone. By about 4000 BCE the people of Mehrgarh were known as Chalcolithic people.

<!-- STEP id="signpost" -->
## What Grew Out of This

Bronze is a harder metal, made by mixing copper with tin. The book says that this first step laid the basis for the Bronze Age Sindhu-Sarasvati civilisation, around 3500 BCE. We reach that civilisation in the next lesson.

The book dates this civilisation from about 3500 BCE on this page and from 3300 BCE on its timeline. The cities came about 2600 BCE.
