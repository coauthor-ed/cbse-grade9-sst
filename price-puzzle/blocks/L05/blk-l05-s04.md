# Point E on the Graph

<!-- STEP -->
## Where the curves cross

Draw the market demand curve DmDm' and the market supply curve SmSm' on one graph. They cross at one point, E. From E, read across to the price, ₹100, and down to the quantity, 12 kg. These are the equilibrium price and the equilibrium quantity.

<!-- STEP -->
## Gaps above and below E

At ₹150, above E, the supply curve is to the right of the demand curve. The gap between them, from 6 kg to 18 kg, is the surplus. At ₹50, below E, the demand curve is to the right. The gap from 6 kg to 18 kg is now the shortage.

<!-- STEP -->
## The price moves towards E

A surplus pushes the price down. A shortage pushes the price up. **Either way, the price moves towards E, the only point where the two quantities are equal.** If nothing else changes, it stays there.
