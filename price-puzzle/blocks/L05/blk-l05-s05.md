# Equilibrium Keeps Moving

<!-- STEP -->
## Real markets keep changing

On a graph, equilibrium is one point. Real markets change all the time. New technology, wages, interest rates, wars, pandemics, weather and natural disasters all change demand or supply. So a real market is always moving towards a new equilibrium, and it never settles at the old one for long.

<!-- STEP -->
## Face masks in 2020

During the COVID-19 pandemic in 2020, the demand for face masks rose very fast. Makers could not produce more masks at once, so supply could not rise fast enough. The price of masks rose a lot.

<!-- STEP -->
## Supply catches up

Over time, makers produced many more masks, and the price fell. When the pandemic was over, demand fell further. **Mask prices went back to about what they were before the pandemic.**
