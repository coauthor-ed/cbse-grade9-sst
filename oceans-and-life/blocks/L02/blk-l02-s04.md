## The parts of a wave

The high peak of a wave is the **crest** and the low valley is the **trough**. **Wave height** is the distance from the trough up to the crest.

In this wave the crest is 1.5 m above calm sea level and the trough is 1.5 m below it. So the wave height is 3 m, and not 1.5 m. Tap a name to light it.

## Length and time

**Wavelength** is the horizontal distance between two crests in a row, or two troughs. Here A and B are one wavelength apart.

The **wave period** is the time the crest at A takes to reach B: here 4 seconds. **Wave frequency** is the number of crests passing A each second. One crest in 4 seconds is 0.25 each second.
