# Two Coasts, One Puzzle

<!-- STEP -->
## The same question, two answers

The panel shows two coasts with a slider that runs up and down them.

On the coast of Norway, the harbours stay open all winter and the sea in them never freezes. On the coast of Labrador, further south than the top of Norway, the sea freezes solid and icebergs drift down past the shore.

Both coasts are in the far north. Yet one is open water and the other is ice.

<!-- STEP -->
## What the tide cannot do

The tide is the same on both coasts. It rises and falls twice a day, it sloshes up and down the same stretch of water, and it goes nowhere.

**The tide moves water up and down in one place. What carries warmth right across the ocean?**
