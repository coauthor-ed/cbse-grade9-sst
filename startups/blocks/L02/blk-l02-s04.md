## Buses and scholarships

The women begin work early in the morning. Lijjat runs buses that pick them up and drop them back home, which makes the early start easier. Lijjat also gives scholarships to the school-going children of its members, to encourage them to study further.

## Women with their own income

Lijjat has helped thousands of women become **financially independent**: they earn their own money. It has made communities stronger. It has also grown into a large business.
