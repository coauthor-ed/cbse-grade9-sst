## Aarav's College Fee

Aarav has saved **₹50,000**. His college fee of ₹50,000 is due in **three months**, and he will need every rupee of it on that day.

He can keep the money in shares, a mutual fund, a fixed deposit or savings account, or a five-year bond.

**Predict:** Where should Aarav's ₹50,000 wait for the next three months?
