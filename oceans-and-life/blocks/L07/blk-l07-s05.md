## Minerals and energy

The non-living side divides into minerals and energy. Minerals include sand, gravel and deep-sea minerals. Energy divides again, into conventional and non-conventional.

<!-- STEP -->
## Old sources

**Conventional** energy is the kind used for a long time: oil and natural gas from under the sea bed.

<!-- STEP -->
## New sources

**Non-conventional** energy is newer. It includes biofuels, gas hydrates, offshore wind and ocean energy. Gas hydrates are natural gas held in an ice-like solid in the sea floor. The line from the living side shows that sea plants can give biofuels.

<!-- STEP -->
## Ocean energy

Ocean energy divides once more. Ocean mechanical energy uses moving water: tides, waves and currents. Ocean Thermal Energy Conversion (OTEC) uses the difference between warm surface water and cold deep water.

These resources do not all behave alike. Oil, gas and minerals do not come back once they are used, while tides and waves keep coming. Fish come back only if enough are left to breed.
