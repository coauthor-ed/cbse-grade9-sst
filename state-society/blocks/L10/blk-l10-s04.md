## Across the subcontinent

Before the second millennium CE, several important centres of education arose across the Indian subcontinent. The map marks nineteen of the prominent ones, from Śhāradā Pīṭha in the north to Anurādhāpura in the south.

Tap **North**, **East**, **West** or **South** to read the names in that region.

<!-- STEP -->
## Two dates

Two centres have dates we can place on the timeline. **Nālandā** university was established in 427 CE, and **Vallabhī** university in 480 CE.

The date strip shows how close together they are, though one lies in the east and the other in the west.

<!-- STEP -->
## Who came, and what grew

These institutions of higher learning attracted students from both India and other regions.

The map shows only some centres. In Lesson 5 you saw land grants create agrahārams, and some of these, such as **Aihole** and **Badami**, became centres of learning.
