# Voting From Home

<!-- STEP -->
## A Ballot Paper at Home

These photographs show a home visit by a polling team in Bhopal in 2023. The voter is holding a pink ballot paper beside a cardboard screen marked "Voting Compartment". No voting machine is used here.

<!-- STEP id="compartment" -->
## A Private Place to Mark It

In the second photograph, a voter is marking a ballot behind the same kind of screen. The screen does the same work at home as it does at a polling station: **nobody can see how the voter votes.**
