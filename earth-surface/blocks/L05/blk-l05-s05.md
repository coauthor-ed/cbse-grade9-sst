## The River Slows at the Sea

Where a river enters a sea, ocean or lake, its water slows down. It can no longer carry its load, so sand and mud settle at the mouth.

Drag **Years** to build the land.

<!-- STEP -->
## A Fan of Channels

The deposits block the way, so the river splits into smaller channels called **distributaries**, with **islands** and **bars** between them. In time they form a fan-shaped or triangular area of land, a **delta**.

<!-- STEP -->
## Life on a Delta

Fertile delta soil suits crops such as rice and jute. The mix of fresh and salt water supports fishing, and the channels serve trade and transport. Many people live on deltas, and floods can harm them.

A delta grows only where the river brings sediment faster than waves and tides remove it.
