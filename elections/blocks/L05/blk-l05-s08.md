# Who Tells the Voter About the Candidates?

## A Vote Within Reach

Look at what you have seen in this lesson. Braille beside the buttons, a screen at home, a ballot sent electronically, a companion and a wheelchair all bring the vote closer to the voter.

**Every one of these measures helps a voter to cast a vote.**
