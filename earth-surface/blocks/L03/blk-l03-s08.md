# A Volcano With No Fire

<!-- STEP -->
## Mud, not lava

Not every volcano gives out lava. The mud volcano at Baratang Island in the Andaman and Nicobar Islands is a rare natural wonder, and the book calls it India's only mud volcano.

Here mud bubbles out of the ground, with no fire and no lava.

<!-- STEP -->
## What pushes the mud up?

Natural underground gases and pressure push water and clay up through cracks. The mud spills out and dries in a low mound.

A mud volcano has no link to magma, so it works in a different way from a fiery volcano.
