## Why a wave breaks

Near the shore the water gets shallow. The bottom of the wave drags on the sea bed and slows down, but the top keeps going. The top runs ahead, leans over and topples. That is a breaking wave.

<!-- STEP -->
## Here the water really does move

Once a wave breaks, its water rushes up the beach and then slides back. This is why waves look as if they carry water: at the very edge of the sea, they do. Beyond the breakers, the water only goes round.
