# Compare Two Parties

<!-- STEP -->
## A Task for Your Group

Form a group of 4 to 6 students. Compare the policy preferences of two political parties in the last Lok Sabha elections, using credible sources like party manifestos and newspapers.

Compare six areas: education, national security, social welfare, environment, economy and health. Present the points in a poster, using bullet points, symbols and colour coding.

<!-- STEP -->
## Four Columns

Each row of your table has four columns:

- policy area
- the party's viewpoint
- any policy or law that aligns with that viewpoint
- your source

This picture fills in one row for two made-up parties, to show the shape of the work.

<!-- STEP -->
## Your Own Sheet

The other five rows are yours, for two real parties you choose. Write down only what your sources say, and name each source in the last column.

**The task asks you to compare two parties, so keep to what they say and leave your own choice out of the table.**
