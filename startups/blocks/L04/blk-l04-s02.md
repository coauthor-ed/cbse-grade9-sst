## Four areas of a business

The questions in a business plan can be grouped into four main **functional areas**. They are management and operations, finance and accounting, human resources, and marketing and sales. The book calls them the four key pillars (the main parts that hold a business up) that every successful business runs on. Each area has its own work and helps the business run smoothly.

<!-- STEP -->
## What each area looks after

Management and operations looks after day-to-day matters, such as production, quality and supplies. Finance and accounting manages money wisely, through budgets and reports. Human resources manages people: hiring staff, their benefits and settling quarrels at work. Marketing and sales spreads awareness and drives sales, through advertising and offers.

## Four jobs, four areas

Now look at the bakery's four jobs again. Baking bread is operations. Paying the rent is finance. Hiring a helper is human resources. Telling people about the mango cake is marketing and sales. **A good recipe helps with only one of the four areas.** The areas work together, so the business uses its money, people and time well.
