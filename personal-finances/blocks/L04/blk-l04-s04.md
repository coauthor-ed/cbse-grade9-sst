# Fixed Deposits

<!-- STEP -->
## A rate fixed in advance

In a **fixed deposit**, a person puts a **lump sum** (a whole amount at one time) with a bank or post office. It stays there for a fixed period, such as one, three or five years.

The rate of interest is fixed on the first day and does not change for the whole period. Fixed deposits give assured returns and suit people who prefer safety to high returns.

<!-- STEP -->
## Maturity period

The time from the day the deposit is made to the day the money is paid back is the **maturity period**. Money taken out before that day usually earns a lower rate.

The government also backs long-term schemes such as the **Public Provident Fund** (PPF), the **National Savings Certificate** (NSC) and the **Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana**.
