# From high to low

<!-- STEP -->
## Out of the high, into the low

The wind blows from the 1012 area towards the 984 area. You might expect wind to blow towards high pressure, but air leaves the place where it presses hardest and moves to where it presses least.

<!-- STEP -->
## Lines called isobars

The lines on this map are **isobars**: each joins places of equal pressure, marked in millibars. Going from H to L the numbers fall in steps: 1012, 1008, 1004, 1000, 996, 992, 988, 984.

<!-- STEP -->
## The Earth bends the wind

The arrows are not straight, because the Earth's rotation turns moving air. North of the equator, wind spirals clockwise out of a high and anticlockwise into a low. South of the equator the turning is the opposite way.
