# Kin and Land Together

## A new tie is added

The second answer is right. The kin lines stay where they are, and a ring of land is added around them.

Between about **1000 BCE and 600 BCE**, people were connected by kinship and also "by a sense of belonging to a land". A larger clan would leave out this second tie.

<!-- STEP -->
## Why land began to matter

Control over **land**, **farming** and **trade routes** became more and more important. So more complex systems of administration developed.

Some scholars see this change as a consolidation of identity for the Vedic janas, which means the janas became firmer and more settled as units.

<!-- STEP -->
## Bigger units

From about 600 BCE, bigger political units appear in the texts of the time. These are the **mahājanapadas**, and the sources usually speak of **sixteen**.
