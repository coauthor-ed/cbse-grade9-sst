## Entrepreneurship

Running the stall took planning, organising and learning by doing. This is **entrepreneurship**: the process through which a person, or a group of people, turns an idea into a business. Every business starts with an idea, a need, or a wish to make something new.

<!-- STEP -->
## Ten elements of entrepreneurship

The book's figure shows ten elements of entrepreneurship: market, activities, performance, strategy, team, AI, analysis, goal, opportunities and innovation. AI (artificial intelligence) means computer systems that can do tasks such as studying information; this explanation is ours, not the book's.

<!-- STEP -->
## Four elements at your stall

At your stall, for example, you can see four of them. Your classmates are the team. The parents and children who visit are the market. Making a profit in the challenge is the goal. A new recipe, such as nimbu pani with mint, is innovation.
