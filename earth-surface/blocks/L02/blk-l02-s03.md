## Seven major plates

The Earth's outer shell is broken into plates. The major ones are the Pacific, Eurasian, African, North American, South American, Indo-Australian and Antarctic plates. Many smaller plates, such as the Nazca and Arabian plates, lie between them.

Tap a plate to see what it carries and which way it moves.

<!-- STEP -->
## A plate is not a continent

A plate's edge is often not the edge of the land. The African plate carries Africa and a wide part of the ocean floor too.

There are three main types of plates.

- **Continental plates** carry continents.
- **Oceanic plates** carry ocean floors.
- **Mixed plates** carry both.

## India's plate

India lies on the Indo-Australian plate. The plate moves north, into Asia, at about 5 cm a year.
