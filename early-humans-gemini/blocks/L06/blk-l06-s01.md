# A Village From 9,000 Years Ago

<!-- STEP id="grid" -->
## Mehrgarh

A grid of rooms built of sun-dried brick stands beside the Bolan river, in present-day Pakistan. This is Mehrgarh, and people were living here by about 7000 BCE, which is about 9,000 years ago. The book calls it the oldest Neolithic site in the subcontinent and its earliest agricultural village.

<!-- STEP id="bins" -->
## Rooms and Bins

Look closer and the grid divides into two kinds of space. There are small rooms for living, and long bins split into compartments, which the book calls granaries. All of it is built of handmade brick.

<!-- STEP id="valley" -->
## The Bolan Valley

The Bolan runs down out of the Baluchistan hills toward the plain. People lived here for a very long time, and when houses fell they rebuilt them on the same spot.
