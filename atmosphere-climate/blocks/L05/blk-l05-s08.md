# Challenge: Run the Monsoon

<!-- STEP -->
## A board of stations, and one month dial

The board is a strip of the Indian coastline with stations along it - wet west coast, dry inland, wet east coast. There is a **month dial** and a **strength dial**.

Both are yours to set. The month dial is not a clock and it does not tick: a month is something you choose, and you may change it as often as you like.

<!-- STEP -->
## Nothing is paid out until the wind arrives

Each station carries a moisture budget. The budget fills when the wind reaches that station **from the sea** - and the wind is not a setting. The wind is the *result* of the land-ocean temperature difference you set.

So what you are really doing is not placing rain. You are placing a temperature difference and letting the wind work out where it blows.

A station the wind is blowing away from gets nothing, however strong the season is.

<!-- STEP -->
## What actually wins

Read that second point again, because it is the whole game.

**A strong monsoon does not pay every station.** A learner who has memorised "monsoon means rain" reaches the end of the board with a full budget and three dry stations, and cannot close it.

To close the board you have to set the land hotter or colder than the sea, work out which way that sends the wind, and check which stations sit on the receiving side of it - in the same month.

## Take your time

No timer, no moves to protect, unlimited retries, and the same board each time. **If you won by turning the strength dial up, you have not used the month dial yet.**
