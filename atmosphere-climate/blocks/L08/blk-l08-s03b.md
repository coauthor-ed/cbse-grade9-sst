# The Ozone Hole Is a Different Problem

<!-- STEP -->
## Two problems, two kinds of ray

People often mix these two up. Greenhouse gases hold **heat** rising from the ground. The ozone layer, high in the stratosphere, takes in the Sun's **ultraviolet** rays. Tap each one to see it.

<!-- STEP -->
## What the ozone hole is

The **ozone hole** is a thinning of ozone over Antarctica, caused by CFCs, gases once used in fridges and sprays. It lets in more ultraviolet.

It is not the main cause of warming. In 1987, at Montreal, the world agreed to stop CFCs.
