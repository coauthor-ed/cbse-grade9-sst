## Where warm meets cold

The regions where warm and cold currents meet are known as **fishing grounds**. One lies off Newfoundland in Canada, where the cold Labrador Current meets the warm Gulf Stream. Slide the two currents together and see what happens.

<!-- STEP -->
## Why the fish come

These waters are rich in nutrients, the food that sea life needs. Scientists explain that the two waters mix and bring nutrients up from the deep. Tiny drifting living things called **plankton** grow on them, and fish come to eat the plankton.

## The same meeting off Japan

Off Japan, the cold Oyashio meets the warm Kuroshio in the same way. Places like these support a large variety of fish, which makes them important fishing grounds.
