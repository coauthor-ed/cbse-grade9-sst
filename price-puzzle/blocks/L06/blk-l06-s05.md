# Goods for Everyone

<!-- STEP -->
## Public goods

Roads, bridges, public parks and streetlights are for everyone to use. So are national defence, sanitation and drainage. Goods like these are called public goods. Private companies usually do not provide them, because they earn no direct profit from them.

<!-- STEP -->
## A park for the neighbourhood

Suppose your neighbourhood needs a park. If each family pays ₹5,000, it can be built. But many families think: "If others pay, the park will be built anyway, and I can use it without paying." So not enough money is collected.

<!-- STEP -->
## Why the government builds it

The park is never built, even though everyone needs it. **This is why goods that help everyone are often provided or paid for by the government.** It makes sure that everyone, rich or poor, can use them.
