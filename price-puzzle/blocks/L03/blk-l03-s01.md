# What Is Supply?

<!-- STEP -->
## Sellers decide too

Buyers decide how much to buy. Sellers decide how much to bring. Seller A has a mango stall in the same market where Srivalli, Alex and Israt buy. Each morning he decides how many kilograms of mangoes to put out for sale.

<!-- STEP -->
## Willing and able

**Supply** is the quantity of a good that sellers are willing and able to offer for sale at a particular price. Willing means the price is worth it to the seller. Able means the seller really has the goods. A seller with no mangoes left cannot supply any, whatever the price.

<!-- STEP -->
## Early and mid-season

At the start of the mango season, only a few mangoes are ripe. Supply is low, so mangoes are costly. By the middle of the season, many more mangoes reach the market. Supply rises, and prices fall.
