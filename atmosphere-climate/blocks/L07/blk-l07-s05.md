# Three Pairs of Stations

<!-- STEP -->
## Near the sea, far from the sea

Thiruvananthapuram stays between 26.2 and 28.7 °C. A climate this steady is called **equable**. It lies near the equator and beside the sea, which warms and cools slowly.

Nagpur, far from the sea, swings by 14.8 °C.

<!-- STEP -->
## One sea, two seasons

Kolkata and Chennai are both near the Bay of Bengal, but their wettest months differ. Kolkata's are July and August, with the south-west monsoon. Chennai's are October and November, with the north-east monsoon.

<!-- STEP -->
## Wettest and driest

Most monsoon rain falls on the southern slopes of the Himalaya, and Leh lies behind them, so it gets only 8.5 cm.

Leh swings by 25.7 °C, the widest in the table, and Jodhpur's 19.0 °C comes next. These two have the most extreme climates here.
