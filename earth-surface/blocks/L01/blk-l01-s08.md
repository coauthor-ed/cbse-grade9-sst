# A Broken Shell

<!-- STEP -->
## From the top to the centre

You can now read the Earth from top to bottom. A rigid shell rests on a hot, soft layer, and slow currents in the mantle push and pull it. The only fluid layer is the outer core, far below.

<!-- STEP -->
## How many pieces?

The rigid shell is broken into pieces, and the pieces carry the land and the oceans.

**How many large plates are there, and what does each one carry?** We look at that in the next lesson.
