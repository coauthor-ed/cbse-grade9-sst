# Grey Powder

<!-- STEP -->
## What caused this?

A volcano is an opening in the crust through which molten rock, gas and ash come out. Molten rock underground is **magma**.

Here a volcano is erupting. Hot gas throws rock and ash high into the air.

<!-- STEP -->
## What is the grey powder?

The ash cools and falls back to the ground. Coarse pieces land near the volcano, and fine ash drifts further. The material thrown out by a volcano is called **tephra**.

The grey powder covers roofs, trees and roads.

<!-- STEP -->
## What does it tell us?

The ash came from inside the Earth. **An eruption shows that the Earth's internal forces can bring material from deep below up to the surface.**
