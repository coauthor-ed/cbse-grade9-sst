# Winter and summer winds

<!-- STEP -->
## January: from the land

In January the wind blows from the land to the sea, and it is cold and dry. You might expect a sea wind on a coast, but India's land cools faster, so pressure is high over the land and low over the seas.

<!-- STEP -->
## June: it turns around

In summer the land heats up faster than the oceans. Low pressure forms over the subcontinent, while the cooler Indian Ocean has high pressure. Moist winds now blow from the ocean to the land, bringing rain.

<!-- STEP -->
## Most of our rain

**This summer wind brings most of India's rainfall for the whole year.** Mumbai gets about 175 of its 183 cm in these months. It comes from the same cause as the sea breeze: land and sea heating unequally.
