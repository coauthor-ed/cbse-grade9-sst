## It stays about where it started

The coconut ends up about where it started. It goes up on each crest and down in each trough, and the wave passes on.

If you guessed the beach, that is a fair guess, because water at a beach does seem to come in. We will see why very soon.

<!-- STEP -->
## Round in a small circle

Watch the marked drops of water. Each one goes round a small circle: forward on the high part of the wave and back in the low part. Deeper down the circles are smaller.

<!-- STEP -->
## The energy goes on

Each drop passes its movement to the next and comes back to its place. So the energy travels on across the ocean, and the water does not travel with it.
