# Making the Tool Again

Go back to the wear marks on the edge of the stone. They do not come with an explanation, so how can anyone say what made them?

Archaeologists make a copy of the tool in the same way, by striking flakes off a stone. Then they use the copy to cut meat, scrape a hide or chop wood. Last, they compare the damage on its edge with the damage on the old tool.

**A matching pattern of wear is good evidence of how a tool was used, but it is not proof.**
