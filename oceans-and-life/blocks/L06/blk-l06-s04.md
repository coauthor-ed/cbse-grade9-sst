## Detection

After 2004, India built a tsunami warning system that works in four stages. First comes **Detection**: a network of seismic stations detects undersea earthquakes in real time. Put the four cards in order, or go on and see each one.

<!-- STEP -->
## Confirmation

Not every undersea earthquake makes a tsunami, so the next stage checks. In **Confirmation**, Ocean Bottom Pressure Recorders (OBPRs) on the sea floor detect the passage of tsunami waves.

<!-- STEP -->
## Tracking

In **Tracking**, coastal tide gauges and radars monitor changes in sea level. This follows the arrival and movement of the tsunami waves.

<!-- STEP -->
## Warning Dissemination

*Dissemination* means spreading. In this last stage, alerts go by satellite to government agencies, and directly to officials and the public through SMS and other channels.
