## A path that comes and goes

The temple at Dwarka stands on its small hillock, and a stone pathway leads out to it. Visitors can walk along the pathway only at low tide.

<!-- STEP -->
## High tide and low tide

Move the clock. The sea rises for about 6 hours and then falls for about 6 hours. This rhythmic rise and fall is the **tide**.

When the water reaches its highest level and covers much of the shore, it is **high tide**, or flood tide. When it falls to its lowest level and moves away from the shore, it is **low tide**, or ebb tide.

<!-- STEP -->
## Twice a day, a little later each day

In most parts of the world this happens twice a day. High tide to the next high tide takes about 12 hours 25 minutes, so the tides come about 50 minutes later each day.
