## High tide on a fishing shore

During high tide many fish move closer to the shore in search of food and shelter. So fishers casting nets from the shore and from boats get a better catch.

<!-- STEP -->
## Low tide on the same shore

At low tide the sea moves away from the shore and leaves wet sand bare. People walk out to the new edge of the water to cast their nets and to gather what they can find there.
