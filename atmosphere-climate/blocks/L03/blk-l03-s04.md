# Water you cannot see

<!-- STEP -->
## Water in the air

When water evaporates from land and water bodies, it becomes water vapour, a gas you cannot see. **Humidity** means the presence of water vapour in the air. The more vapour, the more humid the air.

<!-- STEP -->
## Warm air can hold more

Warm air can hold more water vapour than cold air before it is full. You can see this in the two boxes of air.

**As air gets warmer, its capacity to hold water vapour increases.** So warm air over the sea can take up a lot of vapour.

<!-- STEP -->
## Dry heat, sticky heat

Jodhpur in May is very hot, but its air holds little vapour, so sweat dries quickly.

Chennai in May is hot too, and its air is close to full. Clothes take longer to dry and sweat evaporates slowly, which feels very uncomfortable.
