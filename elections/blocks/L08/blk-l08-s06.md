# How the Vote Has Changed

<!-- STEP -->
## Pebbles

In ancient Greece, pebbles were used for voting. That is where the word **psephology**, the study of elections, gets its start.

<!-- STEP -->
## Paper

Indian voters used to mark a ballot paper in private, fold it and drop it into a ballot box. Paper ballots are still used in some cases, such as voting from home.

<!-- STEP -->
## The EVM

Today most voters use an **EVM**, an electronic voting machine. The voter presses the button beside a candidate's name and symbol, and the machine beeps.

<!-- STEP -->
## Braille

An EVM has Braille numbers beside the buttons, so a voter who cannot see can find the right button without help.

<!-- STEP -->
## The VVPAT

After the press, the **VVPAT** shows a slip behind a small window for a few seconds. It then drops into a sealed box.

**Braille made the vote easier to reach, and the VVPAT made it easier to check.**
