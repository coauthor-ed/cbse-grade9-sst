## Five Agents

**Agents of gradation** are natural forces that wear down, carry and deposit material on the Earth's surface. There are five main ones: running water, glaciers, wind, waves and groundwater.

Together they lower high areas and fill up low areas, so a mountain's rock can end up on a plain or on the sea floor.

<!-- STEP -->
## What Each Agent Does

Tap each agent.

- **Running water** erodes rock and soil to form valleys and plains.
- **Glaciers** scrape and carry huge amounts of material, and carve U-shaped valleys.
- **Wind** shapes deserts by eroding and depositing sand.
- **Waves** shape coasts into cliffs, beaches and bays.
- **Groundwater** dissolves rocks such as limestone, making caves and sinkholes.
