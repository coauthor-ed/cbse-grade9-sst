## Land Shaped History

Landforms have also shaped human history. Tap each kind of land on the map.

- **Rivers and plains:** the Ganga, Brahmaputra and Indus, like the Nile, gave rise to farming societies and early cities.
- **Mountains:** the Himalaya shielded India from invasions, while passes like the Khyber allowed cultural exchange.

<!-- STEP -->
## Deserts and Coasts

- **Deserts:** the Thar limited large settlements but encouraged trade routes, such as the Silk Route.
- **Coasts:** harbours supported trade and travel to distant lands, and helped kingdoms in south India to flourish.

Wars, settlements, trade and culture have all been influenced by the land's physical features.
