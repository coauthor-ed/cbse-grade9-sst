## A dead mouse and two coins

This story is from the **Jataka Tales**. A rich merchant once told his king, "Even a dead mouse can be the start of a fortune, if someone works hard and uses their brain." A poor young man heard this and decided to test it. He sold a dead mouse to a shopkeeper for two coins, because the shopkeeper's cat was hungry.

<!-- STEP -->
## One trade after another

With the two coins, the young man bought fruits and water. He gave them to a tired flower picker for flowers, and sold the flowers in the city. After a storm he cleared fallen branches and sold them as firewood. He traded grass with horse dealers, opened a refreshment shop, and later did business with foreign merchants.

<!-- STEP -->
## What he did with his wealth

With intelligence, dedication and hard work, the young man became very wealthy. He did not forget that it all began with the merchant's words, so he gave the merchant half his fortune to thank him. He also used his wealth to help communities in the city, not only himself.
