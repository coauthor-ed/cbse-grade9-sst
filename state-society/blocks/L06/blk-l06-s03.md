# What the Pot Was For

<!-- STEP -->
## The ballot pot

This system is called the **kudavolai**, or the 'ballot pot' system. It had four parts:

- the names of eligible candidates, written on palm leaves and placed inside a large pot
- a public gathering
- a young child, who drew the leaves one by one
- the community watching, often at a temple

<!-- STEP -->
## A fair draw

A draw may well be quick, but speed is not the reason our source gives. **To ensure fairness, the draw took place in full view of the community.**

One caution: the inscription does not tell us how names reached the leaves.

<!-- STEP -->
## From the pot to the committees

The elected members were then divided into specialised committees, called **variyams**, and each was given important village work:

- the tank committee, which managed irrigation
- a committee that administered justice
- a committee that collected taxes
