# Ice-free at sixty-five degrees north

## Two coasts of one ocean

Two coasts face each other across the North Atlantic. Norway, on the right, lies at about 65 degrees north, and its sea stays largely free of ice. Labrador, on the left, lies further south, and icebergs float off its coast.

**Predict:** Why is the sea off Norway free of ice, when the sea off Labrador, further south, carries ice?
