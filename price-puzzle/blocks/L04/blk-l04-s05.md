# Better Technology

<!-- STEP -->
## Drip irrigation

A farmer used to water his field by hand, which took a lot of work. Then he put in drip irrigation, which brings water to each plant through thin pipes. His field now uses 40 per cent less water, and gives 30 per cent more crop.

<!-- STEP -->
## A lower cost for each kilogram

The farmer spends less on water and work, and he grows more. So each kilogram costs him less to produce. He is now willing to offer more at every price, and his supply curve moves to the right.

<!-- STEP -->
## Many farmers, one market

When many farmers use better technology, the market supply rises. Cold storage helps too: mangoes kept cool in storage and in trucks can travel to distant markets, so those markets get a bigger supply.
