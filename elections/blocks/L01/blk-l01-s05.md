## A Word in Two Pieces

The scientific study of elections has a hard name: **psephology**. Nobody is born knowing a word like this, and it becomes easier once you take it apart.

## Why a Pebble?

The word comes from Greek. *psēphos* means pebble, and *logy* means systematic study.

**In ancient Greece, pebbles were used for voting.** That is how the study of elections came to be named after a small stone.
