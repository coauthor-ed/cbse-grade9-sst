# When the Climate Warmed

<!-- STEP id="thaw" -->
## Warmer Land

Around 12,000 years ago, Earth's climate became warmer. This was a long change, not one year. Forests and grasslands spread onto land that had been covered by ice sheets.

<!-- STEP id="landscape" -->
## More Kinds of Food

The new landscapes offered a wider variety of food: small game animals, fish and edible wild grains. The people of this time are called **Mesolithic** hunter-gatherers. *Meso* means middle, so this is the Middle Stone Age.

<!-- STEP id="harvest" -->
## One Kind of Blade, Three Tools

The small blades could be set into many tools, so a new food source needed only a new handle. The same stock of microliths could arm an arrow for small game, a harpoon (a barbed spear) for fish and a sickle (a curved cutting blade) for wild grass. The book says that fishing, in the sea and in rivers, was the mainstay: the main source of food.

<!-- STEP id="boom" -->
## The First Population Explosion

With more food available, the number of people grew quickly. The book calls this the first population explosion in human history.

Art also flourished at this time, and caves and rock shelters were frequently occupied. One group of those shelters still has its paintings on the walls.
