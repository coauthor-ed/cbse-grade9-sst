# One Verse, Three Questions

<!-- STEP -->
## What does the source tell us?

Here is the verse in full: "I am a poet; my father is a physician, my mother is a grinder of corn; having various occupation, desiring riches we remain (in the world) like cattle (in the stalls)."

Historians ask three questions of a source. The first is about **evidence**. This verse tells us of three occupations in one family.

<!-- STEP -->
## What can we understand from it?

The second question is about **interpretation**. Three occupations in one family suggest that a son need not follow his father's work, at least in this family.

<!-- STEP -->
## What might it not tell us?

The third question is about the **limits of the source**. One verse cannot tell us how common such families were, or how people ranked these occupations.
