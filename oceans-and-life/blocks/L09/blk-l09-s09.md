## Before we move on

- Oceans are vital for global trade, travel, communication and climate regulation.
- Submarine relief means the physical features of the ocean, such as the shelf, ridges, trenches and seamounts. They influence marine life, resources and undersea exploration.
- Waves, tides and currents affect marine ecosystems, the global climate, fishing and navigation.
- Maritime zones include territorial waters, EEZs and the high seas.
- Sustainable use of marine resources needs cooperation, regulation and conservation, to protect sea life and support future generations.
- Maritime rules and regulations exist to resolve disputes over marine boundaries.
- International laws such as UNCLOS help define maritime zones and settle disputes among countries.
