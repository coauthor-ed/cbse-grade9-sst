## What a startup is

A **startup** is a young, early-stage company started by entrepreneurs to offer a new product or service. India's startup world is built on new ideas, and also on **perseverance**: going on working hard even when things are difficult.

<!-- STEP -->
## When a startup fails

Every entrepreneur should know that a business can fail. Some startups grow fast, while others struggle or even close down. There is nothing wrong with failure if an honest effort has been made. Each failed attempt teaches lessons that help entrepreneurs build stronger ideas and better businesses in future.

## Swami Vivekananda's words

Swami Vivekananda famously said: **"Arise, awake and stop not till the goal is reached."** (Get up, wake up, and do not stop until you reach your goal.) India's startup world has grown through this kind of steady hard work and perseverance.
