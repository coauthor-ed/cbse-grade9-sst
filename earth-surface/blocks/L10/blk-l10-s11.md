# Your Turn

## Protect Your Land

These have no single right answer. Talk them over in class or at home.

Make a plan to protect the land near you from erosion.

- Where does water or wind carry soil away?
- What could hold that soil in place?

<!-- STEP -->
## Your Region

Which disasters might you face where you live? Discuss a plan to reduce the harm.

- Which landforms are close to you?
- Who needs to be warned first, and how?

<!-- STEP -->
## A Past Disaster

Write about a disaster that hit your region.

- How did it change work, school, travel and daily life?
- Ask older people what they remember.

## More to Try
