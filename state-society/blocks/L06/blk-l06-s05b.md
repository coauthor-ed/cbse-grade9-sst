## Run a Village Draw

Now you will run a kudavolai yourself, in two parts.

- **First, put the steps of the draw in order.** Tap them from first to last.
- **Then draw.** Tap the pot to draw a leaf, and put each name on a committee: the tank committee, the justice committee or the tax committee.

You cannot pick a name. You take whatever name the pot gives you, as the village did.

There is no clock.
