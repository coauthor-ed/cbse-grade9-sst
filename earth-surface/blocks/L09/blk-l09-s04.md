## A Hollow Under Flat Ground

Water can dissolve a large **cavity** under ground that looks perfectly flat. Press **More years**. The cavity grows, and the rock roof above it gets thinner.

<!-- STEP -->
## The Roof Falls In

In the end the thin roof cannot hold up the ground above it, and it collapses. The bowl-shaped hole left at the surface is a **sinkhole**, also called a **doline**.

Many sinkholes form this way. Others form slowly as water dissolves the rock from the top down.

## A Land of Hollows

Limestone land can hold many sinkholes side by side, as the block in the figure shows.
