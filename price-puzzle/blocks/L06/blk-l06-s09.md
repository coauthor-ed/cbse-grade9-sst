# What Drives the Market

<!-- STEP -->
## Ask these questions

Markets are not machines with one fixed price. They keep changing.

The next time a price changes, for books, vegetables or sports equipment, ask: Is supply changing? Is demand changing? Is the market moving towards equilibrium? Is the government helping or hurting?

<!-- STEP -->
## Before we move on: demand and supply

- Demand is how much buyers are willing and able to buy at each price. When the price falls, the quantity demanded rises.
- Supply is how much sellers are willing and able to offer at each price. When the price rises, the quantity supplied rises.

<!-- STEP -->
## Before we move on: equilibrium and government

- At equilibrium, the quantity demanded equals the quantity supplied. When conditions change, the market moves to a new equilibrium.
- The government acts when essentials cost too much, public goods are missing, or one seller controls a market. **Too many rules can also harm the market.**
