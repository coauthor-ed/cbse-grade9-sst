## Things to think about

Talk about these with a friend. There is no answer key.

- How do waves, tides and currents influence climate, marine life and people's work around the world?
- How do different features of the ocean floor affect fishing, transport and undersea exploration?

## Things to do with your friends or class

- **Poster:** draw a poster that asks people to protect marine resources. Add a slogan, such as Save Our Seas, and explain your message in two or three sentences.
- **Map:** on a map of India, mark at least five coastal states that might need tsunami warnings.
- **Role-play:** in a group, act out what happens when a cyclone warning is issued. Show what scientists, officials and coastal villagers each do.
- **Model:** make a model of an ocean with ship routes, protected zones and safety measures. Explain how it follows maritime laws and protects the ocean.
