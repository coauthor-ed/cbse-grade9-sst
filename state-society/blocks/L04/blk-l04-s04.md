# The Council of Ministers

<!-- STEP -->
## A small body of elders

At the centre of the administration was the **mantri-pariṣhad**, the council of ministers. It was a small body of elder statesmen, and its main role was to advise and support the king.

The seven limbs do not name the council separately; its members were among the ministers and high officials, the **amātya**.

<!-- STEP -->
## Four posts

The council generally included four officers. Tap each one to see who he is.

- the treasurer
- the chief tax collector
- the chief legal advisor
- the commander-in-chief of the army

<!-- STEP -->
## When the emperor was away

An Aśhokan inscription refers to decisions taken by the council during the emperor's absence.

**So in exceptional circumstances, the council could decide independently, in the public interest.**
