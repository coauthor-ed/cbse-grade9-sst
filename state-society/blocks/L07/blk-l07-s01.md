# What Does Dharma Mean?

<!-- STEP -->
## A word many people think they know

Ask people what **dharma** means and many will say "religion". The word is often heard in talk about religion, yet it means much more.

Now read this old saying: "the dharma of iron is to sink and of wood is to float."

<!-- STEP -->
## Iron and wood in water

Press **Drop the iron** and then **Drop the wood**. Each one does what the saying says it does.

<!-- STEP -->
## A puzzle in the saying

Iron and wood do not follow any religion, so the word must mean something else in this saying. What meaning could fit iron, wood and people too?
