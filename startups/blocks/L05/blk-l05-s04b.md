## The no-loss game

You sell cakes at ABC Bakery for three months. Each month has fixed costs, such as rent, and each cake uses ingredients and a box, which are variable costs. These are made-up numbers. Find the smallest number of cakes you must sell in the month so that the bakery makes no loss. This no-loss number is an extra idea, beyond the book.

<!-- STEP -->
## Working it out

Each cake leaves its price minus its variable cost. Those amounts must add up to the fixed costs. In the first month a cake sells for ₹500 and uses ₹300 of ingredients and a box, so each cake leaves ₹200 towards ₹10,000 of fixed costs. The bars show revenue and expenses as you change the number.
