# Direct and Indirect Elections

<!-- STEP -->
## Direct Elections

**Direct elections** are elections in which citizens vote directly to choose their representatives or leaders. In India, these are elected directly every five years:

- members of the Lok Sabha, at the central level
- members of the Vidhan Sabha (Legislative Assembly), at the state level; Vidhan Sabhas make laws for their States and some Union Territories
- members of local bodies, like Panchayats and municipal corporations

<!-- STEP -->
## Indirect Elections

**Indirect elections** are elections in which citizens vote for representatives, who then choose or elect the leaders. These are elected indirectly:

- the President
- the Vice President
- members of the Rajya Sabha
