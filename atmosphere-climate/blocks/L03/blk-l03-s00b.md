# Five things we measure

## The elements of weather

Weather and climate are both described by the same five **elements**: temperature, precipitation, humidity, wind and atmospheric pressure.

Each one has its own instrument at a weather station. Tap a name to see it.

<!-- STEP -->
## Why they matter

A weather report gives you all five, as in this example. They shape daily life: what you wear, when farmers sow, whether you carry an umbrella.

Note down the weather report from a local newspaper for two weeks, and observe how the weather changes.

We start with temperature. Why is one place hot and another cold?
