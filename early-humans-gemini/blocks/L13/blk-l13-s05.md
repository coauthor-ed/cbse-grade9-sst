# A Cloth That Named a Road

<!-- STEP id="cocoon" -->
## Silk Is Very Old

Silk comes from the cocoon of the silkworm: one long, fine thread that can be unwound. The book says silk was known to the Chinese from the Neolithic period, 4000-3000 BCE, and it became an important craft during the Bronze Age.

<!-- STEP id="demand" -->
## Silk Goes West

Around the 2nd century BCE, during the Han dynasty, silk became a major item of external trade. External trade means trade with people outside the country.

<!-- STEP id="route" -->
## The Silk Route

Silk mattered so much that the entire route on which it was traded came to be known as the Silk Route. The route ran for thousands of kilometres, past deserts and mountains, from China towards West Asia.

<!-- STEP id="india" -->
## India on the Route

You studied this route in Grade 7. Southern branches reached northern India, so goods and people could travel between India and China.

**A very old craft gave its name to a trade route.**
