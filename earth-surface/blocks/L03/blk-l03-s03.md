# Do the Belts Follow the Plates?

<!-- STEP -->
## Lay the plates over the dots

Now compare two maps: the plate boundaries and the earthquakes. Tap **Earthquakes** and **Plates** to switch each one on and off.

The belts of earthquakes follow the plate boundaries.

<!-- STEP -->
## Test the volcanoes

The triangles are active volcanoes. Tap **100 km**, **300 km** and **500 km** to count how many lie that close to a plate boundary.

The count is worked out from real positions, so you can check the claim for yourself.

<!-- STEP -->
## Most, but not all

Most volcanoes lie close to a plate boundary, but not all of them. Hawaii lies in the middle of the Pacific plate, far from any edge.

**Most earthquakes and volcanoes occur along plate boundaries, because that is where plates meet, part and slide.**
