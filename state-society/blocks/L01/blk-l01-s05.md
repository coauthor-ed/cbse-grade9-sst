# One Spelling, Three Words

## Three words that look alike

Three words look almost the same, and it is normal to mix them up at first. You will meet all three in this course.

- **Brāhman** is a concept: the Ultimate Universal Being.
- **Brāhmaṇas** are texts: the prose part of each Veda.
- **Brāhmaṇas** or **Brahmins** are a **varṇa** category, a group in society. Lesson 8 explains varṇa.

<!-- STEP -->
## Which one is meant?

Read the sentence, then tap the card it means. Ask what the sentence is about: an idea, a text, or a group of people.

The rest of the sentence tells you which word is meant.
