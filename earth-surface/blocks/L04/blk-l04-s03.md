## The Stream Is the Different One

The stream is the odd one out, because the pebbles travel. The other three all break rock where it stands.

In all three types of weathering the rock breaks down, and the broken pieces stay where they are.

**Erosion** is the wearing away and carrying of soil and rock from one place to another, by water, wind, ice or waves.

## Follow One Grain

A grain sits on a weathered rock. Drag it down the slope and into the stream.

While it stays on the rock, only weathering has happened to it. As soon as it travels, erosion has begun.

**Weathering breaks rock down. Erosion moves the broken material.**
