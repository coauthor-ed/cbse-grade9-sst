# Up and Down

<!-- STEP -->
## Forces from inside push up

Everything in the last three lessons came from inside the Earth. **Internal forces** move plates, fold rock, break faults and bring up lava, and these build the surface up. Press **Raise**.

<!-- STEP -->
## Forces on the surface wear down

**External forces** work on the surface all the time. Water, ice, wind and waves wear rock down and carry it away. Press **Wear down**.

<!-- STEP -->
## Both work at once

No landform stays as it is, because both kinds of force are always at work.
