# Thinking About Tomorrow

<!-- STEP -->
## Today's choices, tomorrow's supply

Our choices today affect what is left for the future. Buying too many cheap clothes that are soon thrown away, catching too many fish and taking out too much groundwater can all harm supply later. For example, if too many fish are caught this year, fewer fish are left to breed, so fewer can be caught next year.

<!-- STEP -->
## A new equilibrium later

Less supply next year moves the supply curve to the left. It now meets the demand curve at a higher price and a smaller quantity. Using resources so that they last for the future is called sustainability. **Short-term gain today can make goods scarcer and costlier tomorrow.**
