# The Crop That Earns More

<!-- STEP -->
## What the farmer does

He grows more chickpeas. The same field, water and work now earn him more from chickpeas than from wheat. So next season, the supply of chickpeas rises and the supply of wheat falls.

<!-- STEP -->
## Related goods

**The supply of one good depends on how much the seller can earn from the other goods he could make instead.** This is not only true for crops. A factory that can make school bags or suitcases makes more of the one that earns more.
