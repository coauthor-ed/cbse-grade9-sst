# Lower Price, More Mangoes

<!-- STEP -->
## What Srivalli did

She bought more. At ₹150 per kg she took 1 kg, at ₹100 she took 2 kg, and at ₹50 she took 3 kg. Nothing else changed: the same buyer, the same mangoes and the same money in her purse. Only the price moved.

## The Law of Demand

When the price of a good rises, people buy less of it. When the price falls, they buy more. Price and quantity move in opposite directions, so economists call this an inverse relationship.

**This is the Law of Demand: the higher the price, the smaller the quantity demanded.**

<!-- STEP -->
## Why a lower price means more

At a lower price, each kilogram takes less of Srivalli's money, so she can afford more. A mango that was not worth ₹150 to her may well be worth ₹50, so she adds it to her basket.
