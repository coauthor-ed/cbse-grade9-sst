## Management and operations

**Management** means planning, organising and guiding the use of resources to reach the goals of the business. One goal could be a bigger share of the customers in the area, won by keeping customers satisfied. **Operations** means producing goods or services and delivering them well and on time.

## Operations in the bakery

At ABC Bakery, operations means choosing the right equipment, such as ovens and mixers, and arranging the ingredients. It means checking the quality of what comes out of the oven. And it means planning each day's baking, so that fresh, good bread and cakes are ready on time, with no delay and no waste.
