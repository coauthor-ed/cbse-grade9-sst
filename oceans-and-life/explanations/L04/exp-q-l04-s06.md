# Reading the two currents near India

On this map, the arrows near India are both **red**. Maps usually show warm currents in red and cold currents in blue, so both are **warm currents**. A warm current is one that flows from a warmer region.

The arrow in the Arabian Sea curves east and then south-east towards the southern tip of India, and the arrow in the Bay of Bengal runs north-east. The currents near India change direction with the monsoon winds, so the map shows one pattern.
