# Borrowing

<!-- STEP -->
## A loan

Sometimes people need money they do not have yet. They may borrow it from a bank, from relatives or from others. A **loan** gives the money now, and it is paid back later with interest.

<!-- STEP -->
## Helpful and harmful borrowing

Borrowing helps when it is used for a productive purpose. An **education loan** pays for a course, and the course can raise what the student earns for many years.

Borrowing to buy things you do not really need can lead to a **debt** burden. **Debt** is money owed to somebody.

<!-- STEP -->
## Borrowing responsibly

Responsible borrowing means taking a loan only when it is needed, and making sure it can be repaid on time.
