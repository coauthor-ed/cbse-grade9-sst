## Slowing the Water

Fast water on a bare slope carries soil away. These measures slow it so that it soaks in.

- A **contour trench** is dug along a contour line, a line at one height across the hill.
- A **bund** is an earthen bank along a contour line.

Pick **Bare**, **Trench** or **Bund**, then press **Rain**.

<!-- STEP -->
## Steps and Small Dams

- **Terraces** are level or gently sloping steps cut into a hillside.
- A **check dam** is built across a small stream. It slows the water and lets the soil settle behind it.

Try **Terrace** and **Check dam** with **Rain**.

## An Old Idea in India

These methods are old. The Sindhu-Sarasvatī civilisation used dams, canals, contouring, bunding and terracing to manage water. Sanskrit texts such as the Vedas, Kṛiṣhiparāśhara, Kauṭilya's Arthaśhāstra and Vṛikṣhāyurveda describe these practices.

In Nagaland, the **Zabo** system uses earthen bunds on hillslopes to save both soil and water.
