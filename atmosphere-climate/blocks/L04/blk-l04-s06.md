# Gentle or strong?

<!-- STEP -->
## Four speeds of wind

Wind can be gentle or strong. You can read it from what it does to things around you. Tap each speed.

- **Calm**, 0-1 km/hr: smoke rises straight up.
- **Light breeze**, 6-11 km/hr: you feel it on your face and leaves rustle.
- **Strong breeze**, 39-49 km/hr: large branches sway and umbrellas are hard to use.
- **Storm**, 103-117 km/hr: widespread damage, and very rarely experienced.

<!-- STEP -->
## Find the rest

This table has gaps, for example between 11 and 39 km/hr. Find out the other categories of wind and their effects, and share them in class.

Think of a time when strong wind made it hard for you to walk, or caused some other problem.

## Named for where they come from

**Winds are named after the direction they blow from.** A westerly blows from the west, towards the east. So a name tells you where the air has been.
