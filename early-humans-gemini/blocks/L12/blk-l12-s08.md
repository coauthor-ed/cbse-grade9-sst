# Jade That Rings

Bone and bronze are not the only things that survive. The book says that jade objects and figurines were either ritual objects or prestige objects, which means things that show high rank. These drawings show three objects like those in the book.

<!-- STEP id="jade-pendant" -->
## Jade From Far Away

Jade was not available locally, and the book says it was obtained from outside China. So each piece had travelled a long way. The book says jade probably played an important role in social transactions, which means gifts and exchanges between people.

<!-- STEP id="bronze-vessel" -->
## Bronze and Marble

Artisans also mastered bronze, and they made weapons, tools and elaborate ritual vessels like this one. Marble was carved into birds and animals, and it was also used as foundations for wooden pillars.

<!-- STEP id="jade-chime" -->
## A Stone That Rings

Artisans carved jade into shapes such as fish and tied them with a cord. When struck, the piece gave a clear and lasting sound. The book says this shows the importance of music in Chinese culture.

<!-- STEP id="evidence" -->
## What One Object Can Tell Us

**A jade piece is evidence of long-distance exchange and of fine craft.** It probably also marked the rank of its owner.
