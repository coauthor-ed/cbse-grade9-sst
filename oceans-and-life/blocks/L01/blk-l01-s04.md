## Continental shelf

The shelf is the submerged, shallow edge of a continent. It is rich in marine life and in resources like oil and gas. The oil field of Mumbai High stands on the shelf off Mumbai.

<!-- STEP -->
## Continental slope

The slope is the steep drop that marks the true edge of a continent. The shelf is still part of the continent, even though the sea lies over it.

<!-- STEP -->
## Continental rise

The rise is the gentler ground at the foot of the slope. It leads down to the deep floor.
