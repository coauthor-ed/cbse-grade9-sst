## A slick on the water

Ships must also meet safety standards, and they are prohibited from polluting the seas. This tanker has oily waste on board and a current flows towards a fishing coast. Choose what happens to the waste.

<!-- STEP -->
## What each choice leaves

Waste put over the side spreads on the water, and the current carries it towards the coast and the nets. Waste kept on board can be handed over at a port facility, so the water stays clean. The sea and the current are the same in both runs.

## MARPOL

**MARPOL**, the International Convention for the Prevention of Pollution from Ships, is an important international agreement aimed at reducing pollution from ships. Being far from land does not free a ship from it. The rules allow only small, treated discharges far from land. A single ship's waste does not stay in one place, so the rule has to be shared.
