## Hold the Slope

The game builds a slope one row at a time, from the top down, and gives you a tray of measures each turn.

Spend one on the row that needs it - a contour trench, a bund, a terrace, a check dam - then press **rain** and see what survives.

Rows above a planted row hold. Rows with nothing on them wash out. One measure is in the tray that will not help at all.
