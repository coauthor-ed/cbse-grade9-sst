# Slab Game

## Three incomes

The game gives you three taxable incomes, one at a time: **₹6,00,000**, **₹9,00,000** and **₹17,50,000**.

Split each income into its slabs. The game charges each part at its own slab's rate, and the parts add up to the tax. You win when all three totals are exact. You can move any part again before you check.
