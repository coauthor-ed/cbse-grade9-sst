## Four things move a current

Four things work on ocean currents at the same time:

- winds, which drag the surface water along
- differences in temperature and in **salinity**, which means how salty the water is
- the shape of the continents and ocean basins, which turns the water aside
- the Earth's rotation

## The turning Earth bends the path

The Earth's rotation gives rise to the **Coriolis force**. It deflects any free moving object from its straight path, to the right in the Northern Hemisphere and to the left in the Southern Hemisphere. Launch the water north of the Equator and watch which way it bends.
