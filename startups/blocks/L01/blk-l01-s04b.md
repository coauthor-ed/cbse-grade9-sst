## The festival price game

Now you run the stall for three rounds: the morning, a hot afternoon and the last hour. In every round a glass costs ₹6 to make. A board shows how many visitors will buy at ₹5, ₹10, ₹15 and ₹20. These are made-up numbers.

Pick the price that leaves the most money after costs.

<!-- STEP -->
## Working it out

For each price, take ₹6 away from the price to find what one glass leaves, then multiply by the number of buyers. At ₹5 every glass loses ₹1.

The best price changes from round to round, because the crowd changes.
