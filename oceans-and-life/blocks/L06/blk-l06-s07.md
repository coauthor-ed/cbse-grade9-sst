## From the centre to the people

An alert must not stop at the warning centre. ITEWC sends alerts to the Government of India, which then informs state governments and emergency response teams.

<!-- STEP -->
## Four ways to reach people

Four channels carry the message: satellite-based communication networks, SMS, emails and coastal display boards. Using several channels at once means that one failure need not stop the alert.

<!-- STEP -->
## Knowing what to do

Public awareness and training are as important as the technology. Officials and coastal communities learn how to interpret warnings, so that people know how to respond when an alert comes.
