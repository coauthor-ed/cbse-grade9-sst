# Six Bowls

Six small bowls hold six kinds of grain. They come from six regions on two continents, Asia and Africa.

<!-- STEP id="wheat" -->
## Wheat and Barley, West Asia

The pale, plump grains are wheat and barley, the crops of West Asia.

<!-- STEP id="yangtze" -->
## Rice, South China

The long, slender, brownish grains are rice from the Yangtze valley in south China.

<!-- STEP id="yellow" -->
## Millets, North China

The small, round, yellow grains are millets from north China.

<!-- STEP id="ganga" -->
## Rice, Ganga Plains

The slender, reddish grains are rice again, this time from the Ganga plains in India.

<!-- STEP id="india" -->
## Millets, India

The dark grains in the next bowl are millets grown in India.

<!-- STEP id="pearl" -->
## Pearl Millet, West Africa

The pale grains in the last bowl are pearl millet from West Africa.

Each region had its own crop, its own land and its own weather.

**Did farming begin once and spread to the others, or did it begin more than once?**
