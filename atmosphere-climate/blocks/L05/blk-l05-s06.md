# The east coast's own rain

<!-- STEP -->
## Dry for most of India

From October to February, cold and dry winds blow from land to sea. They generally do not bring rain to most parts of India.

<!-- STEP -->
## The Bay of Bengal adds water

These winds then pass over the Bay of Bengal and pick up moisture. They bring rain to the eastern coast of India, especially Tamil Nadu, Andhra Pradesh and parts of Karnataka.

<!-- STEP -->
## Chennai's wettest months

Chennai shows this in its rainfall: 30.6 cm in October and 35.0 cm in November, more than in any summer month. **The winter monsoon is important for the rainfall of south-eastern India.**
