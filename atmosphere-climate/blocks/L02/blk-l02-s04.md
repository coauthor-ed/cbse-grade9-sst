# Falls, Rises, Falls, Rises

## Mark each part of the line

Many people think the air gets colder all the way up to space. The line shows something else.

Follow the line upward through each layer, and mark it **Falls** or **Rises**.

<!-- STEP -->
## Two fall and two rise

**Temperature decreases with altitude only in the troposphere and the mesosphere.**

- **Troposphere:** the ground warms this air from below, so higher air is cooler.
- **Stratosphere:** ozone takes in ultraviolet rays, which warms this air.
- **Mesosphere:** there is little here to take in the Sun's energy, so temperature falls again.
- **Thermosphere:** gas molecules take in X-rays and short-wave ultraviolet, so temperature rises fast.
