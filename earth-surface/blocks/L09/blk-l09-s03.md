## On the Roof and on the Floor

New rock grows in both places. When a drop reaches the open air of the cave, it loses some of its carbon dioxide. The water can then no longer hold all its dissolved rock, so a tiny ring of a mineral called calcite is left behind.

Press **More years**. Rock grows down from the roof where each drop hangs, and up from the floor where it lands.

<!-- STEP -->
## Two Names

The icicle-shaped form hanging from the ceiling is a **stalactite**. The form rising from the floor is a **stalagmite**.

Neither one is rock that broke off. Both were built one drop at a time from dissolved rock.

<!-- STEP -->
## When They Meet

Press **More years** again. In time a stalactite and a stalagmite join to make a **pillar** from floor to roof.

The opening where the cave reaches the outside is the **cave mouth**.
