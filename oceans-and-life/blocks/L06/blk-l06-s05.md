## From the sea floor to the coast

This drawing shows ten parts of the system. Tap a name to light that part. Part 10 is the **earthquake**. Part 9, the **seismic waves**, travel out through the rock. On land stand part 8, a **seismometer station**, and part 7, a **GPS station**. Part 6, a **tide gauges station**, is at the shore.

<!-- STEP -->
## Two satellites

Part 5 is a **GPS satellite**, which works with GPS stations on the ground. Part 4 is a **communication satellite**, which carries information between the stations and the centre.

<!-- STEP -->
## The centre and the people

Part 1 is the **warning centre**. Part 2, **modelling/simulation**, uses computers to work out how a tsunami may travel. Part 3, **capacity development**, means training people, and it stands on both coasts because it happens everywhere.
