# Reading a Decorated Box

<!-- STEP id="intro" -->
## A Box From Sumer

The book prints a photograph of a decorated box from Sumer. It shows scenes from Sumerian life, made of small pieces of shell and coloured stone. The book asks you to note the wheeled cart, and the rich cloth and ornaments.

This picture is a drawing made for this course, not a photograph. The real box has its scenes on two sides, and this drawing puts them together in three rows. So we will claim only what the real box also shows.

<!-- STEP id="top" -->
## Top Row: A Banquet

People sit on chairs and drink from cups. This is a feast. Who sits and who serves tells us that some people here ranked above others.

<!-- STEP id="middle" -->
## Middle Row: Animals Brought In

Men lead sheep, goats and cattle. On the real box these animals and other goods are being brought in, probably for the feast. So the farmland and herds supported the people at the table.

**Food and goods moved towards those at the top.**

<!-- STEP id="bottom" -->
## Bottom Row: Carts and Soldiers

Here are soldiers and four-wheeled carts. The drawing shows horses, but the real box shows animals of the donkey family. The book says the Sumerians invented the wheeled cart.

<!-- STEP id="ornament" -->
## Cloth and Ornaments

Look at the clothes and ornaments. Rich dress is another sign of rank. The city itself matches this: kings lived in grand palaces, and most people lived in small brick houses.

Most people farmed, and others were metalworkers, potters, weavers, merchants and traders.

**The box shows a society of ranks: rulers, workers and soldiers, each in a separate place.**
