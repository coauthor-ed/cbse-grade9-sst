## Telling people about the bakery

People will not buy from a bakery they have never heard of. **Promotion**, or marketing, spreads the word. ABC Bakery can post on social media, put up posters and give special offers. Businesses also use TV and print advertisements.

<!-- STEP -->
## Packaging

The box a cake goes home in matters too. Good packaging makes the cakes look more attractive. It can also keep them fresh for longer, so they can be sold and eaten over more days. This time is called the shelf-life of a product.

<!-- STEP -->
## Customers who come back

An offer can bring a customer in once. Good customer service brings them back again and again. Staff who are polite and helpful, and cakes that taste the same every time, keep customers coming to ABC Bakery.
