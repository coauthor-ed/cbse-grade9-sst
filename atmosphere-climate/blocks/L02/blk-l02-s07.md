# Where the Air Thins Out

<!-- STEP -->
## The exosphere

The uppermost layer is the **exosphere**. Its air is very thin.

Light gases like **helium** and **hydrogen** float into space from this layer, because gravity is too weak up here to hold them.

<!-- STEP -->
## No sharp top

The air has no lid. The thin air of the exosphere fades away into space, with no line where it stops.

All five layers play a part in the Earth's weather and climate.
