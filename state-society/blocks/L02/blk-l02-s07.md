# South of the Ganga Plain

<!-- STEP -->
## The Deccan and the far south

In the Deccan, the **Satavāhana Empire** followed the Mauryan Empire and lasted nearly 500 years, from the 2nd century BCE to the 3rd century CE.

Further south were the Cholas, the Pandyas, the Keralaputras (the Cheras) and the Satiyaputras. Tap a name to find it. All these polities are also mentioned in the Aśhokan inscriptions.

<!-- STEP -->
## Three emblems

Three kingdoms of the south each had a royal emblem.

- **Tiger**: the Cholas
- **Bow**: the Cheras
- **Fish**: the Pandyas

Their rulers were known as the three crowned kings, or **Vendar of Tamilakam**. Much of what we know comes from **Sangam literature**, the earliest Tamil literary tradition, dated between 300 BCE and 300 CE.

<!-- STEP -->
## Early, not later

Sangam poems describe brave and ambitious kings, including a Chera ruler who earned the title **adhirāja** after defeating several crowned kings. They also mention Vedic yajñas and links with epic traditions.

**These early kingdoms are not the later imperial Chola, Chera and Pandya kingdoms, which emerged many centuries afterwards.**
