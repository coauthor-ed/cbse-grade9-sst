# Ishani Votes for the First Time

<!-- STEP -->
## The Day Before

Ishani and her mother are in the local market, the day before the State assembly election. Wall writing and posters are everywhere, and campaigners are handing out pamphlets and raising slogans.

Campaigning must stop 48 hours before polling ends, so the campaigners should not have been there.

<!-- STEP -->
## Morning at the Polling Station

Ishani has turned 18 and registered through the ECI's online portal. The next morning she goes to the polling station with her mother and her disabled elder brother. The station is well guarded by police personnel.

<!-- STEP -->
## Help at the Gate

A wheelchair is made available for her brother, and volunteers guide the family to the polling booth. Inside the booth, only three people are performing all the duties.

<!-- STEP -->
## The Forgotten Cards

Ishani has forgotten her voter ID card and her Aadhaar card. She still votes, because her identity is verified with an alternative document, such as a passport or a driving licence.

<!-- STEP -->
## The VVPAT Slip

After casting her vote, Ishani sees the **VVPAT** slip. It shows behind a small window for a few seconds, then drops into a sealed box.

The slip stays with the machine, and Ishani does not take it. It lets her check that the vote went to the right candidate.

<!-- STEP -->
## Two More Voters

While leaving, Ishani wonders how the police vote, since they are on duty all day. Police on election duty and army personnel can vote by postal ballot.

At home she tells her story to her grandmother, aged 89, who has cast her vote from home.
