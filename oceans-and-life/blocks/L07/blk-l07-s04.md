## Two big branches

Marine resources fall into two groups, **living resources** and **non-living resources**. The tree shows how each group divides. We start with the living side, which has four branches.

<!-- STEP -->
## Fish, by depth

Fish are sorted by where they live. **Coastal or neritic fish** live within the continental shelf, at depths of 0 to 200 metres. **Oceanic fish** live beyond the shelf, at depths of more than 200 metres.

The long names under them, such as demersal and benthic, tell which part of the water each kind lives in. You need not learn them by heart.

<!-- STEP -->
## Three more living branches

- **Bioactive natural products** are substances from sea creatures and plants that act on the body. Some are used in medicines.
- **Marine bio-organisms** are the sea's other living things, such as algae, plankton and mussels.
- **Submerged aquatic vegetation (SAV)** means plants that grow under water, such as seagrass, which shelters young fish.
