## The Fall Moves Upstream

The waterfall moves slowly upstream. The hard rock resists, but the softer rock below it wears away much faster.

<!-- STEP -->
## A Gap Opens

Falling water and spray cut back the soft rock, and a **gap** opens behind the fall. The hard rock now sticks out with nothing under it.

<!-- STEP -->
## The Lip Breaks

At the foot, water and tumbling stones dig a deep basin called a **plunge pool**. In time the unsupported hard rock breaks and falls, so the waterfall now stands a little further upstream.

## What Waterfalls Give Us

Waterfalls bring tourists, which helps local earnings, as at Dudhsagar on the Goa-Karnataka border. Some are used to make **hydroelectric power**, as near Jog Falls in Karnataka, because falling water can turn turbines. They also offer trekking and photography, and many have cultural or religious meaning.
