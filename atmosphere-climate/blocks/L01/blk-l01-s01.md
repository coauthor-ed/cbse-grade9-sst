## A blanket of air

What do you see when you look up? You might see floating clouds and sunshine, and feel a breeze.

All of these come from a blanket of air around the Earth, called the **atmosphere**. Gravity pulls this air down around the planet and holds it there.

<!-- STEP -->
## Think about it

Can you imagine what would happen if there were no atmosphere?

Think of one thing you would lose, then discuss your thoughts with your friends and teachers. The picture shows the Earth with its air taken away.

<!-- STEP -->
## What we would lose

- **Air to breathe:** every living being depends on this mixture of gases.
- **Warmth:** the air traps some of the Sun's energy and stops it escaping into space.
- **A shield:** the air blocks harmful radiation from the Sun, including ultraviolet.
- **Weather:** no clouds, no rain and no wind.
