# What the Gases Do

<!-- STEP -->
## Nitrogen helps plants grow

Nitrogen is the largest share of the air, yet plants cannot take it straight from the air.

You learnt in Grade 8 that bacteria in the soil and in root nodules change it into a form plants can use.

<!-- STEP -->
## Oxygen and carbon dioxide

People and animals breathe in **oxygen** and breathe out **carbon dioxide**.

Green plants take in carbon dioxide to make their food. Carbon dioxide also holds some of the Earth's heat in the air.

<!-- STEP -->
## Water vapour and ozone

**Water vapour** gives us clouds and rain. **Ozone** is one of the smallest shares, yet it filters the Sun's ultraviolet radiation high above us.
