## Build the River's Three Landforms

The game hands you a stretch of channel and a tray of the three structures a running river can build: a **waterfall**, a **meander**, and a **delta**.

Each one goes on a reach, and the reach is the whole difficulty. The tray will not tell you where a structure belongs, and it will happily accept one that is wrong.

Steepness is the test. The first round gives you the reach; the second round turns it around and gives you the structure and asks for the reach.
