## The Bowl at the Top

At the head of a glacier, ice carves a bowl-shaped hollow into the mountain. This is a **cirque**.

<!-- STEP -->
## A Sharp Ridge

Cirques and valleys cut into a mountain from different sides. The rock left between them is a sharp ridge called an **arete**.

<!-- STEP -->
## A Low Way Through

Where two hollows cut into a ridge from opposite sides, a low gap can be left. This is a **pass**, and people cross mountains through such gaps.

Tap **Cirque**, **Arete** and **Pass** to find each one.
