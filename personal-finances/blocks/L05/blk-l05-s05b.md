# Five People, Five Dates

## One place each

Five people bring you their money and the date they need it:

- Aarav: college fee in three months
- Ritu: school fees in six months
- a farmer: emergency cash, any week
- a teacher: a house repair in five years
- a parent: a daughter's college in fifteen years

Choose a fixed deposit, a bond, a mutual fund or shares for each. The plan is then tested on three possible paths of the market.

You win when every person has the full amount on their date in every path, and the two long goals grow faster than prices.
