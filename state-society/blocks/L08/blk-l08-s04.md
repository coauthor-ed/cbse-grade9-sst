## A new social structure

Gradually, a distinct social structure called **jāti** emerged. It came about through various factors:

- intermarriage among the varṇas
- migrating communities becoming endogamous
- territorial differences

## What endogamy means

**Endogamy** is the practice of marrying within one's own social group, community, clan or tribe. It is a social custom in which people are expected to choose marriage partners from within the same group.

<!-- STEP -->
## Four and many

You might think that more jātis would mean more varṇas. It did not: the number of varṇas was fixed at four, and there was no restriction on the number of jātis.

As new social groups and occupations developed, the number of jātis continued to grow.
