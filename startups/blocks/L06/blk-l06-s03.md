## Business ethics and trust

The Chettiars were known for strong **business ethics**: honest and fair ways of doing business. They were also known for **trust-based lending**: they lent money to people they trusted, and the borrowers trusted them in return. Trust between lender and borrower let lending work even without a formal bank.

<!-- STEP -->
## Careful accounts

The Chettiars also had excellent accounting and record-keeping skills. They kept careful written records of their money, so they knew what had been lent, what had been paid back and what the business had earned.

<!-- STEP -->
## Profits put back to work

The Chettiars reinvested their profits: they put the money back into trade, and into temples, education and community welfare. Even today, Chettiar entrepreneurship inspires modern India. **It shows how risk-taking, discipline and long-term vision can build businesses that last, rooted in tradition.**
