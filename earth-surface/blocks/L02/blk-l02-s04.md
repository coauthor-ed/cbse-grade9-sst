# A Few Centimetres a Year

<!-- STEP -->
## As slow as your nails

Plates usually move a few centimetres a year, about as fast as your fingernails grow. You cannot feel this movement, and you cannot see it in one lifetime.

We will use 5 cm a year. Tap **1 year**.

<!-- STEP -->
## Slow adds up

Now tap **100 years**. In a century a plate moves only 5 m, about the length of a car.

Then tap **1 crore years**. The plate has moved 500 km.

<!-- STEP -->
## Long enough to move a continent

At 5 cm a year, a plate moves 1000 km in 2 crore years. The Earth has had far longer than that.

**Very slow movement, kept up for crores of years, can carry a continent across an ocean.**
