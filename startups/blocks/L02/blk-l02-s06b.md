## The trade chain game

Now you follow the young man's trades from the Jataka tale. You start with a dead mouse and no coins. In each of six rounds you hold something, and three people walk past, each with a need. Tap the person whose need your goods meet.

<!-- STEP -->
## How the game works

A good trade gives you coins, or new goods for the next round. A wrong tap costs nothing, but the person tells you what they really need. The trades follow the tale; the people's words and the coin amounts are made up. If you finish all six rounds, you end with 120 coins.
