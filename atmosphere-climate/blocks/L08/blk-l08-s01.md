# A Natural Blanket of Heat

<!-- STEP -->
## Sunlight in, heat out

Sunlight warms the ground, and the warm ground sends heat back up. Some gases hold part of that heat and send some back down.

This is the **greenhouse effect**. The gases include carbon dioxide, methane, nitrous oxide and water vapour.

<!-- STEP -->
## Earth without it

The greenhouse effect is not bad in itself. Without these gases, Earth's average temperature would be about **-18 °C**, not about **15 °C**.
