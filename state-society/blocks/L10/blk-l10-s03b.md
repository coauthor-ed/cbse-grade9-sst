## Your School and a Gurukula

Compare your school life with the life of a student in a gurukula. Think about daily routine, subjects studied, teachers, discipline and living arrangements.

What advantages and challenges might each system have for students? Here are some points to weigh.

- **Living with the teacher.** A student gets close guidance all day. That same student lives away from home and depends on one teacher.
- **Many teachers.** In your school, each subject may have its own teacher, so you meet many people who know their subject well. Each teacher, though, may know you less closely.
- **Discipline.** A strict routine can build self-control. Some students may also learn best when they are free to question and to choose.

There is no single right answer. Talk about it with a friend and say which part of each system you would keep.
