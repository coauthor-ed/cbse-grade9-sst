# An Ideal in a Verse

<!-- STEP -->
## What a text says

The tradition of respect for women also appears in texts composed after the Vedic period. The **Manu-smṛiti** (3.56) says:

> "Where women, verily, are honoured, there gods rejoice; where, however, they are not honoured, there all sacred rites prove fruitless."

<!-- STEP -->
## Two kinds of source

A verse like this tells us what its author held to be right. A land grant tells us what one person did in one place. Historians use both, and they ask what each kind can show.

So the verse shows an ideal, and it cannot tell us how women lived in every place and time.

<!-- STEP -->
## What the evidence shows overall

**Over time, the position and roles of women fluctuated, even declined, as social and political conditions changed.**

Even so, there are many examples of women continuing to contribute to household management, agriculture, crafts and religious practices.
