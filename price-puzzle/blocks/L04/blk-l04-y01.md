# Your Turn: What Moves Supply?

Discuss these with a partner or in class. There are no right or wrong answers.

1. Pick one good or service: rice, cotton shirts, mobile phones or haircuts. What happens to its supply if the cost of an input rises? If a new, cheaper input is found?
2. What happens to the supply of fish if a lake is overfished, or to the supply of rice if the groundwater runs out?
3. Think of a flood, a drought or a storm in the news. Which goods became hard to find after it, and why?
