## Before a Landslide

The National Disaster Management Authority made this landslide poster. The board shows its own words.

Most of the work happens before the rain: growing trees, keeping drains clean, and watching for warning signs such as cracks on rocks. Also listen to the radio or watch TV for alerts, and do not build near steep slopes or drainage paths.

<!-- STEP -->
## During a Landslide

Stay calm, ignore rumours and stay with your companions.

Trees cracking or boulders knocking together are warning signs. If you notice them, **move away from the slide's path and from the valley below it, quickly.**

Rock and mud travel down that path into that valley, so neither is a safe place to stand. Then inform the nearest Tehsil or District HQ.

## After a Landslide

The danger has not ended. **Keep away from the slide's path and from the valley below it.**

Do not touch or walk over loose material, wires or poles. Check for injured and trapped people. Do not move an injured person without first aid, unless the person is in immediate danger. Do not drink water straight from rivers, springs or wells.
