# Your Turn: Plan ₹1,500

## An investment plan

Suppose you got ₹3,000 as birthday gifts and decided to save ₹1,500.

With your teacher, work out what you might have earned in a year. Suppose you had put ₹500 in a fixed deposit, ₹800 in shares and ₹200 in gold. Find the returns in newspapers, magazines or official websites.

Then try two other combinations. Write a short plan that says why each option suits you or does not, using risk, return, time horizon and liquidity.
