# Mutual Funds

<!-- STEP -->
## One pool, many savers

A **mutual fund** pools money from many investors. A professional **fund manager** invests the pool in a basket of shares, bonds or other assets.

Each investor earns or loses in proportion to what they put in. Put in one tenth of the pool, and you get one tenth of any gain or carry one tenth of any loss.

<!-- STEP -->
## Spreading the risk

Mutual funds spread the money across many investments. This is called **diversification**. When one company does badly, only a small part of the basket falls.

They suit people who do not have the time or knowledge to choose investments themselves.

<!-- STEP -->
## Subject to market risks

Mutual fund advertisements say: "Mutual fund investments are subject to market risks." Many shares can fall at the same time. Then the whole basket loses value, and the loss is the investor's.
