## Startup India and Atmanirbhar Bharat

The government knows that entrepreneurship matters, so it has started many schemes, such as **Startup India**. Startup India supports the vision of Atmanirbhar Bharat: making India self-reliant through innovation and local solutions. Atmanirbhar Bharat is a national policy framework, launched by the government in 2020. It aims to boost manufacturing inside India and reduce dependence on imports.

## Viksit Bharat 2047

**Viksit Bharat 2047** is the government's vision to make India a fully developed nation by 2047, when India marks 100 years of independence. On the way, entrepreneurship will create jobs, drive innovation and empower communities. The next generation of entrepreneurs may be sitting in your classroom today.
