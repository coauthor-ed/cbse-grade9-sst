## Why the profit falls

**Interest is an expense.** The profit and loss account lists interest on a loan among the expenses. ABC Bakery's March account shows ₹10,000 of it.

**Same revenue, more expenses.** Profit = Total Revenue - Total Expenses, so when the expenses rise and the revenue stays the same, the profit before tax falls.

**A loan is not revenue.** Revenue is money earned from selling goods and services. Borrowed money is not earned, so it is not revenue.
