# Buyers and Sellers Meet

<!-- STEP -->
## Two sides of one market

Every market brings buyers and sellers together. Buyers think about how much they are willing to pay. Sellers think about how much they are willing to accept. So the price is set by both sides together: by demand and by supply.

## One table for the mango market

Put the mango market's demand and supply side by side. At each price, the table shows how much buyers want and how much sellers offer. Your textbook uses different numbers here; the idea is the same.
