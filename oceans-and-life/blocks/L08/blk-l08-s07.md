## Beyond the EEZ

Beyond the EEZs of all countries lie the **high seas**, also called international waters. They cover about 64 per cent of the world's oceans. These waters do not belong to any single country, so they are considered part of the **global commons**, which means something all countries share.

<!-- STEP -->
## Six equal rights

All countries have equal rights on the high seas:

- navigation
- fishing
- flying over them
- laying undersea cables
- building lawful structures, such as artificial lands
- exploring for minerals

<!-- STEP -->
## Why it is hard out here

Since no single country controls the high seas, problems often arise: overfishing, illegal fishing and conflicts over resources. These affect millions of people who depend on the sea, and keeping ships safe is harder too.

It is easy to think that there are no rules out here, but that is wrong. Rules do exist, and the next lesson asks whose they are.
