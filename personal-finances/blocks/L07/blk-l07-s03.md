# Tax Slabs

<!-- STEP -->
## Step 1: taxable income

India works out income tax with **tax slabs**: taxable income is divided into ranges, and each range has its own rate. The slabs are proposed by the Ministry of Finance in the Union Budget every year.

Step 1 for Priya: ₹14,00,000 - ₹75,000 standard deduction = **₹13,25,000** taxable income.

<!-- STEP -->
## Step 2: slab by slab

The first ₹4,00,000 is not taxed. The next ₹4,00,000 is taxed at 5%: ₹20,000. The next ₹4,00,000 at 10%: ₹40,000. The remaining ₹1,25,000 at 15%: ₹18,750.

Priya's total tax is **₹78,750**, not 15% of everything.

<!-- STEP -->
## Progressive taxation

As income rises, the tax rises too, and higher incomes pay higher rates on their top part. This is **progressive taxation**. People who can afford more give a larger share of their earnings for public services.
