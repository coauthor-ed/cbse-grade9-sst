# Challenge: Read the Boundary

Each round shows a small map of one place where two plates meet. Three labels are loose, and they all have to go in the right place.

<!-- STEP -->
## What you are sorting with

- **Which kind of boundary it is** - the three ways plates meet.
- **What the crust is doing** - being made, being destroyed, or neither.
- **What a person standing there would say they felt** - fold mountains, a trench and volcanoes, or shaking ground.

<!-- STEP -->
## The places are real

One round is a ridge running down the middle of an ocean. One is an ocean plate going under the edge of a continent. One is two continents nose to nose. One is a fault crossing a coastline.

The last round shows a place with **no name on it**. You will have to work out which one it is before any label can go down.

<!-- STEP -->
## All three, or it does not count

A round is won only when all three labels agree with what the plate rules say. Drop two and miss one and nothing registers - there is no partial credit and no beep, just a boundary that has not been read yet.

The win condition is the lesson's own rule, applied once. **No timer. Take as many goes as it takes.**
