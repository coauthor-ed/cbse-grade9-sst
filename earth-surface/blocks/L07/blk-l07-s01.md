## Ice That Moves

A glacier looks as still as rock, yet it moves. Snow piles up high in the mountains, presses into ice, and the ice slowly flows downhill under its own weight.

Press **Ten years pass** and watch the stake on the ice.

## Ice That Scrapes

Rocks frozen into the base of a glacier scrape the land as it moves, and the ice carries huge amounts of broken material.

Where the ice ends, it melts into a river. The Gangotri glacier feeds the Bhagirathi in this way. **Glaciers are crucial sources of fresh water for people downstream.**
