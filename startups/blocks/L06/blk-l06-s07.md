## Quality of life, jobs and taxes

The book draws six ways in which businesses add to society and the economy. They improve the quality of life by bringing medicines, transport and technology. They create jobs, which give households an income. They also pay taxes to the government, and taxes fund roads, schools and hospitals.

## Goods, innovations and social responsibility

Businesses produce goods and services, which keeps economic activity going. They make innovations that drive economic growth and offer solutions to problems. **Corporate social responsibility** is when a company helps society beyond its own business. Through it, businesses support education, the environment and healthcare.
