# Early India on One Line

## Three thousand years

This line runs from 2000 BCE to 1000 CE. Historians see four broad phases along it. The timeline starts the Vedic period at 2000 BCE, though the date of the Ṛigveda is still debated, as you read in Lesson 1.

- **Kin-based polities** in Sapta-Sindhu, around 2000 BCE
- **Janapadas**, from about 1000 BCE, and **mahājanapadas**, from about 600 BCE
- **Empires** and early southern polities, from about 300 BCE
- **Regional empires**, from about 700 CE

<!-- STEP -->
## How we know each phase

For the earliest phase we have the Ṛigveda, the first of our literary sources. Texts then add to what archaeologists find in the ground.

Later phases have far more: pottery and iron from digs, and inscriptions on rock and copper. Our picture gets fuller as we move to the right.
