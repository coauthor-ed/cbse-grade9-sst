# A List of Goods, in Clay

<!-- STEP id="whole" -->
## One of the Oldest Pieces of Writing

This baked clay tablet from Sumer is covered in small marks. It is one of the oldest pieces of writing in the world. What would you expect the first writers to write down? A poem, a prayer and a law are all fair guesses.

<!-- STEP id="closeup" -->
## Most Early Tablets Are Lists

Historians can read these marks today. The oldest tablets are mostly not poems, prayers or laws.

<!-- STEP id="transcript" -->
## The Tablet, Line by Line

Each line gives a number and a thing, such as jars, fish or sheep. It works like the bill from a kirana shop. Whoever made these marks was keeping count of things, not telling a story.

**It is a list of goods, and how many of each.**
