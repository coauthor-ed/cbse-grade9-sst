# India's Four Seasons

<!-- STEP -->
## What makes a season

Weather can change within a day. A **season** is a common pattern over weeks or months: cool or hot, windy or calm, cloudy or bright, wet or dry.

India's climate is broadly **tropical monsoon**, and the Indian Meteorological Department (IMD) names four seasons.

<!-- STEP -->
## Cold, then hot

- **Winter**, December to early April. December and January are coldest, around 10-15 °C in the north-west and 20-25 °C in the south-east.
- **Summer or pre-monsoon**, April to June, or up to July in the north-west. April is hottest in the west and south, and May in the north.

<!-- STEP -->
## Wet, then clear

- **Monsoon**, June to September, when humid south-west winds sweep across the country.
- **Post-monsoon**, October to December. October and November are usually cloudless in the north-west.

The Himalayan states are more temperate and have two more seasons, autumn and spring.
