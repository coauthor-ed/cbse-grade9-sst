## A desk clerk in Shimla

Mohan Singh Oberoi was born in Punjab in 1898. His father died when Mohan was still a baby, so life was hard from the start. After finishing his education with difficulty, he moved to Shimla and became a hotel desk clerk, earning ₹50 a month. He did not stop at his own duties: he learned every part of hotel work, from guest service to billing and administration.

## A big risk in 1934

In 1934 Oberoi took a bold step. He mortgaged his wife's jewellery and put in all his savings to buy a small hotel. A **mortgage** is a special type of loan used to buy a house or land, with something valuable, called collateral, kept by the lender until the loan is paid back. He knew the risks, but he believed in his plan.

<!-- STEP -->
## From one hotel to a hotel group

Through hard work, Oberoi built one of India's largest hotel chains. He opened luxury hotels, founded a hotel management school and took his business to other countries. In 2001 the Government of India gave him the Padma Bhushan. He died in 2002, when the Oberoi Group, named after him, was one of the world's most respected hotel brands.
