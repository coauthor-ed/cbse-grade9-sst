Notes:

- `l02s01_b05.jpg` is referenced twice inside blk-l02-s01 on purpose: as the
  base `image` (beat 0) and as the `trend` beat's `reveal.images` entry, so
  the row is guaranteed on screen in both the opening beat and the final
  comparison beat.
- The skull row appears in three separate files with different jobs: un-
  labelled and individual (`s01`), unlabelled whole-row predict stimulus
  (`s04`), and labelled question stimulus (`s07`). Only `s07`'s version
  carries names - the earlier ones must not, or the hook and the prediction
  give themselves away.
- All four skull prompts and both row prompts require invented cast
  likenesses, not any identifiable real specimen.
