# Which Action Fits?

<!-- STEP -->
## Six cases

A medicine is too costly for poor families. Workers are paid too little for a day's work. One company controls all the mobile phone services in a town. A neighbourhood needs a park.

In a pandemic, shopkeepers hide sanitisers. Mango prices fall when the season's crop is large.

<!-- STEP -->
## Five actions

The government can set a highest price. It can set a lowest price, such as a minimum wage. It can set up a regulator to keep a monopoly in check, or provide the good itself. Or it can leave the market alone, because nothing has gone wrong in the market.

<!-- STEP -->
## How to play

For each case, ask what goes wrong in the market. **Then choose the action that fixes that problem.**
