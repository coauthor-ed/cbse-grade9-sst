# Warm air rises, cold air sinks

<!-- STEP -->
## Hot ground: low pressure

Where temperatures are high, air heats up and rises, and this creates a **low-pressure area**. Rising air cools, so low pressure goes with cloudy skies and wet weather.

<!-- STEP -->
## Cold ground: high pressure

Where temperatures are lower, the air is cold and heavy, so it sinks. This creates a **high-pressure area**, which goes with clear and sunny skies.

<!-- STEP -->
## Air moves across

Air always moves from a high-pressure area to a low-pressure area. **This movement of air is called wind.** So a difference in heating has made the air move.
