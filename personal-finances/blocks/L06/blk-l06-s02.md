# How Insurance Works

<!-- STEP -->
## The premiums come in

**Insurance** is a legal contract. A person pays a regular amount, called a **premium**, to an insurance company. In return, the company pays for the losses the policy covers.

Here 1,000 families each pay a premium of ₹500 a month. In a year that is ₹60,00,000.

<!-- STEP -->
## A few large bills

This year, 25 families need hospital treatment, with bills of ₹80,000 each. The company pays all 25 bills, ₹20,00,000 in total, so no family empties its savings.

The rest of the premiums pay the company's costs and are kept for years with more claims. A premium is not saved for the family; it buys **cover**.

<!-- STEP -->
## Risk sharing

Insurance works on **risk sharing**: many people pay into a common pool, and those who meet a loss are paid from it. One family alone would take years to recover from a ₹80,000 bill.

So every family gained, including the 975 that never claimed: each one was protected all year.
