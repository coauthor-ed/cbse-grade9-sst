## The law of the flag

Panama's law applies, because Panama is where this ship is registered. On the high seas, the laws of the **flag state** apply. The flag state is the country in which a ship is registered, and the ship flies its flag.

<!-- STEP -->
## Not a sea without law

No single country owns this water, but that does not leave it without law. On the high seas, the flag state's law is what applies to the ship.

<!-- STEP -->
## A law far from home

The law applies out here, but applying it is harder. Panama may be thousands of kilometres away, and no single country controls this water. Out on the high seas there are no clear ways to enforce the rules, so problems such as overfishing and illegal fishing arise.
