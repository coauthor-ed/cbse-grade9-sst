# What Was a King Told to Do?

<!-- STEP -->
## From chief to king

The rājā of Lesson 1 was a clan chief. From the 6th century BCE, rulers took titles such as **rājā**, **mahārāja** or **samrāṭ**, which varied from region to region.

Whatever the title, what was the job? The oath of coronation in the **Yajurveda** gives three pieces of advice to a new king.

<!-- STEP -->
## Judge fairly

First, the king is advised to judge the strong and the weak **impartially and fairly**. A powerful man and a poor man stand before him, and he must not favour either.

<!-- STEP -->
## Protect the country

Second, he is to protect the country **from all calamities**. That covers any disaster that may reach the people, and not only an enemy attack.

<!-- STEP -->
## Do good

Third, he is to **do good to the people**. A well dug for a village is one example of such good.

So the oath describes a king by what he owes to his people.
