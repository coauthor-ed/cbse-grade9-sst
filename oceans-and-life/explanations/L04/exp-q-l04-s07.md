# Norway and Labrador

**The warm side.** The Gulf Stream starts in the Gulf of Mexico and flows towards Norway, so sea ice does not form easily there, even at 60 to 70 degrees north.

**The cold side.** The Labrador Current flows along the east coast of Canada and carries freezing water, so sea ice and icebergs form there even at a lower latitude.

**Together.** Norway is further north but has less ice, so latitude alone does not decide how cold a sea is. A current tends to carry the warmth or cold of the place it came from.
