# Several Walls, Later Joined

The remains give a clue. In many places short stretches of wall stand apart from each other, as in this drawing. The book says the Great Wall was built over a period of two thousand years.

<!-- STEP id="regional" -->
## Separate Walls, From 680 BCE

The book says several walls were built from 680 BCE onwards, by the Zhou and other dynasties. They were protection against violent raids by nomadic tribes. Nomadic people move from place to place with their herds and have no fixed village.

<!-- STEP id="unification" -->
## Joined Together

The book says these walls were later joined to make an effective defence. Historians connect much of this joining with the Qin, after they united China in 221 BCE. One country now had one northern frontier to guard.

<!-- STEP id="han" -->
## Pushed West

Later rulers, especially the Han, pushed the line far to the west, along the edge of the desert. They built this part in rammed earth, and it helped protect the trade routes that ran beside it.

<!-- STEP id="repair" -->
## Two Thousand Years of Work

Expansion and repair went on until the 17th century CE. The stone wall in the pictures comes mostly from this late period.

**The Great Wall was not one project. Many rulers built it over two thousand years.**
