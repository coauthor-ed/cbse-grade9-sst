# Demand and Supply Together

<!-- STEP -->
## Two curves on one graph

Put the mango market's two curves on one graph. The market demand curve, DmDm', slopes down: buyers want less at a higher price. The market supply curve, SmSm', slopes up: sellers offer more at a higher price.

<!-- STEP -->
## Where they cross

The two curves cross at one point. At that price, the amount buyers want is exactly the amount sellers offer. In the next lesson, we find this price and see why the market moves towards it.
