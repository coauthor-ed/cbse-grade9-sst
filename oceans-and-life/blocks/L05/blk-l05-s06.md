## Watching both coasts, all the time

India has several Cyclone Warning Services and Marine Weather Services that monitor storms along both the eastern and western coasts. A warning system only works if it runs continuously. Predicting cyclones early has greatly reduced the loss of human and animal lives.

<!-- STEP -->
## Shelters and stronger warnings

The National Cyclone Risk Mitigation Project (NCRMP) builds cyclone shelters, strengthens warning systems and improves emergency response in cyclone-prone States and Union Territories. It also gives disaster management authorities a Decision Support System (DSS).

## When wind pushes the sea on to land

A cyclone's strong winds pile sea water up against the coast, and this rise is a **storm surge**. The Indian National Centre for Ocean Information Services (INCOIS), with the India Meteorological Department (IMD), set up the Storm Surge Early Warning System (SSEWS).
