## One Hill, Many Landforms

All the landforms of underground water can sit in one hill: sinkholes on top, caves and an underground river inside, and a cave mouth at the foot.

## Fresh Water, With Care

Caves and underground rivers are sources of fresh water, so people depend on them.

Spring water from a cave looks clean, but it is not always safe. Water moves quickly through wide cracks, so it is not filtered the way it is in sand, and dirt from the surface reaches it easily. It should be tested before drinking.

## Visitors and Scientists

Caves bring tourists and adventurers. In Meghalaya, visitors walk through Mawsmai cave near Sohra, and Krem Liat Prah is among the longest caves in India. The Borra Caves are in Andhra Pradesh.

Stalactites and stalagmites also draw geologists, who study them.

## Places of Meaning

In some places a cave has cultural or religious meaning, and people visit it for that reason.

So one hill can give water, work, knowledge and meaning, all at once.
