# The Tool That Shrank

Two stones lie on one centimetre scale, with no label and no date.

<!-- STEP id="handaxe" -->
## The Big One

The first stone is about 15 cm long, worked on both faces until it tapers to a point. Tools of this shape come from sites in India, alongside animal fossils more than a million years old.

<!-- STEP id="blade" -->
## The Small One

The second stone is about 2 cm long, the size of a thumbnail, and sharp along one edge. It also comes from the subcontinent, but from a much later layer.

<!-- STEP id="puzzle" -->
## More Than a Million Years of Shrinking

Put the two side by side and compare them:

- More than a million years of toolmaking lie between them.
- The tools became smaller, not bigger.

**If people were getting better at making tools, why did the tools get smaller?**
