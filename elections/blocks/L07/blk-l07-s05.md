# Let's Map

## A Task from Your Textbook

Choose any three States, including yours. Find the symbols of State parties belonging to those States and plot them on a map of India. List their major agenda, as mentioned in their manifesto in the latest Lok Sabha, Vidhan Sabha or local body elections.

<!-- STEP -->
## Working on the Map

Tap a State to place a pin. This map names no party and shows no symbol, as that work is yours.

In your notebook, write three things for each State you pin: a State party, its symbol, and one line of its agenda. A State party here means one that met any of the five State routes.

**Note the source of every line, such as the ECI website or a party's manifesto.**
