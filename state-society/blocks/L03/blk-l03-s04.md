# What a King Had to Do

<!-- STEP -->
## Protect

The texts make the king responsible first for protection. This work has two sides.

- from **external threats**, such as an enemy at the border
- from **internal disorder**, such as violence inside the kingdom

<!-- STEP -->
## Administer justice

The king was also expected to administer justice. The Śhānti Parva names cases such as **abduction**, **robbery**, **theft** and **adultery**.

Tap a case to see it.

<!-- STEP -->
## Serious offences

Punishments for serious offences were severe. These offences included the killing of a cow, acts of treachery, and the consumption of intoxicating liquor. The penalties were harsh, and sometimes they included capital punishment, which means death.
