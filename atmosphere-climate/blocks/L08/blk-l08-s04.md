# Punjab Floods, 2025

<!-- STEP -->
## What happened

In 2025, Punjab had floods after heavy monsoon rains, as the Satluj, Beas and Ravi rose one after another. The floods damaged villages, farmland, houses, roads and bridges. Some of these causes were natural and some were human-made.

<!-- STEP -->
## Natural causes

The monsoon rains were very heavy, and western disturbances brought more moisture. It also rained in Himachal Pradesh and Jammu & Kashmir.

The Satluj, Beas, Ravi and Ghaggar were already flowing high before the heavy rains began. Water from the hills and local rain made them overflow.

<!-- STEP -->
## Human-made causes

1. Weak and old embankments, or **dhūsī bāndh**, could not stop the water.
2. Houses and farms close to the rivers left less space for floodwater.
3. Silt and mud reduced what rivers and dams could hold.
4. In some areas, warnings came late or were not clear.

Together these increased the damage.
