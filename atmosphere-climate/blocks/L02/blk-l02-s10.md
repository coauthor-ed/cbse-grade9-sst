# Challenge: Draw the Profile

<!-- STEP -->
## Read the column, segment by segment

A marker starts at the ground and climbs. Wherever it stops, you have to say three things about it: which way the **temperature** is running there, which **layer** you are in, and what happens in that layer.

For each segment, set the direction - **falling** or **rising** - then attach the layer's name and its one fact.

<!-- STEP -->
## It will not accept a profile that only falls

Try marking the whole column as falling. It refuses, and it keeps refusing, because two of the four segments are rising and the game can see that.

There is no clock here and no score to protect. Take as many attempts as you want, in any order.

**A learner who has memorised five names cannot get through this. You have to know which way the line goes.**
