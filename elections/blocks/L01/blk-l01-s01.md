# Can She Stay?

<!-- STEP -->
## Chosen by Her Class

In Grade 8, Gurmat's class elected her as class representative, or CR. A CR speaks for the class, and the class had chosen her by a vote.

She did the job well, and her classmates were happy with her.

<!-- STEP -->
## The Year Ends

The class has now moved up to Grade 9. Some of her classmates say there is no need for a new election, because she was good and can simply carry on.

**Can Gurmat continue as CR in Grade 9 without facing a fresh election?**

<!-- STEP -->
## The Same Question for a Country

A government also has a fixed **term**, which is the time it is allowed to hold office. So we can ask the same kind of question about a country:

- Can a government continue beyond its term without asking the people again?
- Can people really make a choice if only one political party is contesting the election?

Keep your answer to all three questions in mind.
