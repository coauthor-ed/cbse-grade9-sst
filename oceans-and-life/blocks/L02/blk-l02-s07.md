## Storm waves

During storms, strong winds can make huge waves that may cause serious damage along a coast. These waves are made by wind, like everyday waves, only much larger.

<!-- STEP -->
## Tsunamis

A **tsunami** is a giant wave with another cause. Most tsunamis are caused by underwater earthquakes. People sometimes say "tidal wave", but that name is wrong: neither tides nor wind make a tsunami.

<!-- STEP -->
## Run-up height

The greatest height a tsunami reaches above sea level on the shore is its **run-up height**. It can be up to 30 m, or even more in extreme cases. That is about as tall as a building of ten floors.
