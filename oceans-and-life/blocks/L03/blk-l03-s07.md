## Electricity from the tide

In some places the rise and fall of the tide is used to make electricity. A wall called a **barrage** holds back the high water. When the tide falls, the water runs out through a turbine, and the turbine turns a generator.

## Three sites in India

India has a vast coastline of 11,098.81 km. Tidal energy is not yet widely used here on a large scale, but certain areas have been identified as suitable. The Gulf of Kutch, the Gulf of Khambhat and the Sundarbans are among the major sites.
