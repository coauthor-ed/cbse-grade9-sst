# Same Money, Three Needs

<!-- STEP -->
## Three people, ₹50,000 each

Three people each hold ₹50,000. A student must pay a fee in three months. A parent is saving for a daughter's college in fifteen years. A farmer may need cash for seeds or medicine in any week.

Their time horizons are three months, fifteen years and any day.

<!-- STEP -->
## Match each one

Move each person to a place that fits. The student needs safety and money ready on the day. The parent has fifteen years, so can accept ups and downs for a higher return. The farmer needs cash on any morning, so liquidity comes first.

<!-- STEP -->
## Is higher risk always best?

No. Even the parent keeps a small amount in a savings account for emergencies. Ask three questions: what is the money for, when is it needed, and can the family bear a loss?
