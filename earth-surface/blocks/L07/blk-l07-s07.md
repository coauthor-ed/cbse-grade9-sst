## A Sudden Flood in Winter

In February 2021, a flood struck Chamoli district in Uttarakhand. Many people and livestock lost their lives. Buildings, roads, bridges and hydel projects were badly damaged, and villages were cut off.

Why did it come so suddenly, in winter?

<!-- STEP -->
## Rock and Ice Fell

On 7 February, a huge mass of rock and glacier ice broke off a high ridge above the Rishiganga valley and fell.

<!-- STEP -->
## A Flood Without a Lake

The ice, rock and water rushed down the valley and reached the hydel projects. You might expect a burst glacial lake, as in some mountain floods. Studies of this flood found no lake burst. **Not every mountain flood has the same cause.**
