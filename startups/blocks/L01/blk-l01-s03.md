## Stall A: a long queue

Stall A collects 200 × ₹5 = ₹1,000. But its 200 glasses cost 200 × ₹6 = ₹1,200 to make. The stall spends ₹200 more than it collects, so it ends the day with a **loss** of ₹200.

<!-- STEP -->
## Stall B: a profit

Stall B collects 80 × ₹15 = ₹1,200 and spends 80 × ₹6 = ₹480, so ₹720 is left. **Profit** is the money earned in trade or business after paying the costs of producing and selling goods and services. Stall B made a profit of ₹720.

## One glass at a time

At Stall A each glass sells for ₹5 but costs ₹6, so each glass loses ₹1. Change the number of glasses on the picture: 50 glasses lose ₹50, and 200 glasses lose ₹200.

**Selling more glasses helps only when each glass sells for more than it costs.**
