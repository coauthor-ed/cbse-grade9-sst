# Sixty

<!-- STEP id="firsts" -->
## More Sumerian Firsts

The Sumerians invented the wheeled cart and the sailboat. They also used calculation to plan buildings and measure fields, and measuring a field soon raises a question: how do you share it out equally?

<!-- STEP id="base60" -->
## Counting in Sixties

We count in tens, but the Sumerian number system was based on 60. We do not know for certain why they chose it. One clear advantage is that 60 can be shared equally in many more ways than 10.

**Try the numbers: 10 shares equally only by 2 and 5, but 60 shares by 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 10, 12 and more.**

<!-- STEP id="legacy" -->
## Still on Your Wall

Look at any clock. The book tells us that the 60-minute hour, the 60-second minute and the 360-degree circle all began with the Sumerians.

**You use their sixties every time you read the time.**
