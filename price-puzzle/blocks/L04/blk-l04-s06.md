# What Sellers Expect

<!-- STEP -->
## Producing for the future

What producers expect about future demand changes how much they make. If they expect buyers to want more soon, they produce more, and supply rises. If they expect buyers to want less, they produce less, and supply falls.

<!-- STEP -->
## Holding stock back

Sellers who already have goods in store also look ahead. Suppose potato wholesalers expect the price to rise in the peak season. Then they may hold some potatoes back now, and sell them later at the higher price. So the supply of potatoes today falls.

<!-- STEP -->
## Costs, weather and disasters

Other changes move supply too. If seeds, fertiliser or fuel cost more, supply falls. If a cheaper input is found, supply rises. If water or another resource runs out, or a flood or drought ruins a crop, supply falls.
