# One MP, Very Different Numbers

<!-- STEP -->
## Two Seats, One MP Each

Look at these two seats. The hill seat has villages far apart and **5 lakh** people. The city seat has crowded streets and **25 lakh** people.

Each seat sends **one** Member of Parliament (MP) to the Lok Sabha. Each small figure beside an MP stands for 1 lakh people.

<!-- STEP -->
## Move the Line

The line between the seats is a **boundary**. Drag it across the city, and the people on each side change.

Every person who moves across the line leaves the city seat and joins the hill seat. Both seats still send one MP each, and the two numbers still add up to 30 lakh.

<!-- STEP -->
## One Parliament

Boundaries are not fixed for ever.

**Both MPs sit in the same House, and each has one vote there.**
