## The baking game

You decide how many cakes ABC Bakery bakes each morning, from Monday to Friday. In this example, each cake costs ₹150 to bake and sells for ₹250. A cake not sold by evening is thrown away. On a normal day about 20 people buy a cake. These are made-up numbers.

Each morning a note tells you what is happening that day. Bake 10, 20, 30 or 40 cakes.

<!-- STEP -->
## Working it out

Each cake you sell leaves ₹100, before rent and other running costs. Each cake you throw away loses its ₹150. So bake as many cakes as you expect to sell that day, and no more.
