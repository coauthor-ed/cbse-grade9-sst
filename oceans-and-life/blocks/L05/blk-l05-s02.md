# Two boats at sea

## Will the warning reach them?

A cyclone warning goes out for the coast of Keralam. Two fishing boats are at sea off Alappuzha, one 30 nautical miles from the shore and one 80 nautical miles. Each has a mobile phone and a GEMINI, a handheld device that receives information from a satellite.

**Predict:** Which boats will get the warning on their GEMINI today?
