# Start-Age Game

## Three targets

Each round puts ₹1,000 a year into a scheme at 7%, stopping at 65. The targets are ₹50,000, then ₹1,00,000, then ₹1,50,000.

Find the **latest** age to start that still reaches the target. Do not just divide the target by ₹1,000: that counts the deposits and leaves out the interest. You can try again as many times as you like.
