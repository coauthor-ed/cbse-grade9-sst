# Stored Food, but No King

Mehrgarh stored food for a very long time. A store of food could give someone power over others, because whoever guards it can say who eats. We do not know if anyone at Mehrgarh ever used it in this way.

<!-- STEP id="nopalace" -->
## What Has Not Been Found

No palace has been reported here, and no royal tomb. The remains are houses, bins and graves.

<!-- STEP id="stillvillage" -->
## Copper Came, and It Stayed a Village

Copper was being worked here by about 4000 BCE. Mehrgarh remained what it had been: a farming village, not a city.

<!-- STEP id="question" -->
## What Is Still Unknown

So stored food and metal were both here, and no city grew on this spot. Why did cities rise later, and in other places? One site cannot answer that.
