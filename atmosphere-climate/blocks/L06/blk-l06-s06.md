# Six Seasons of the Old Calendar

<!-- STEP -->
## The six Ṛtus

Traditionally, India counts six seasons, or **Ṛtus**, each about two months long. They come from an astronomical division of the 12 months into six parts.

They may vary across the country. Tap a Ṛtu to read its months in both calendars.

## Think about it: rāgas of the seasons

Hindustani classical music links certain rāgas with certain seasons. Ask your elders and teachers:

- Which rāga goes with the rains?
- Is it sung only in that season?
- What in the music recalls the season?
