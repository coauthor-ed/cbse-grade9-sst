# Climate Change

<!-- STEP -->
## What it means

**Climate change** means long-term changes in temperature, rainfall and wind, caused mainly by human activities: burning fossil fuels, cutting forests and industrial pollution.

These add greenhouse gases, so more heat is held in.

<!-- STEP -->
## What it does

We are seeing more frequent floods and droughts, melting glaciers, rising sea levels and loss of biodiversity.

It also has a great impact on almost all sections of the population, including women and children.

<!-- STEP -->
## What we can do

Tackling it takes all of us together: reducing carbon footprints, using renewable energy, protecting forests and adopting sustainable lifestyles.

A **carbon footprint** is the total greenhouse gases released as a result of human activities, such as energy use, transport or making goods.

**Every small step counts.**
