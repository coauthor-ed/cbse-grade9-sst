# Risk and Return Go Together

<!-- STEP -->
## Lower risk, lower return

The picture places the four options by risk and return. Fixed deposits are low-risk and give steady but lower returns. Bonds sit close to them; government bonds are safer than company bonds.

Mutual funds may give higher returns. Shares can give the highest returns over many years, but they also carry the highest risk.

<!-- STEP -->
## The trade-off

In general, lower-risk investments give lower returns, and higher-risk investments **may** give higher returns. A higher return is possible only if you accept the chance of a loss.

So people choose by their goals, the risk they are willing to take, and when they will need the money.
