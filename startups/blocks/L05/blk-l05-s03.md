## Fixed costs

Some expenses stay the same in the short term, however much the bakery bakes or sells. These are **fixed costs**, also called overhead costs. Rent is one: ABC Bakery pays ₹40,000 a month whether it bakes 100 loaves or 2,000. The cost of equipment, such as ovens, mixers and refrigerators, is also a fixed cost.

<!-- STEP -->
## Variable costs

Other expenses go up and down with the amount the bakery makes or sells. These are **variable costs**. Raw materials, such as flour and sugar, and packaging are variable costs. If the bakery bakes twice as many loaves, it needs twice as much flour and twice as many packets.

## Both kinds together

Suppose each loaf uses ₹20 of flour, sugar and packaging; this is a made-up number. For 1,000 loaves the variable cost is ₹20,000, and with the rent the total is ₹60,000.
