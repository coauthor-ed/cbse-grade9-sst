# Two monsoons

<!-- STEP -->
## Two halves of a year

The **south-west monsoon**, or summer monsoon, blows from sea to land between June and September. It crosses the Indian Ocean, the Arabian Sea and the Bay of Bengal.

The **north-east monsoon**, or winter monsoon, blows from land to sea from October to February.

<!-- STEP -->
## How they got their names

Winds are named after the direction they blow from. So the south-west monsoon comes from the south-west, and the north-east monsoon comes from the north-east.
