# One Wheel Does Not Move the Carriage

<!-- STEP -->
## A carriage with one wheel

This bullock carriage is loaded and ready to go. It has only one wheel on its axle, so it leans over and cannot move.

<!-- STEP -->
## Add the wheel

**Tap the spare wheel** to fit it to the axle. With a pair of wheels, the same carriage can roll.

<!-- STEP -->
## What the line means

The **Arthaśhāstra** is one of the earliest and most systematic treatises on statecraft. It says that "one wheel does not move the carriage".

This line captures a belief: good government needs cooperation and a well-organised administration. Literary and inscribed sources of the period show that **the king did not rule alone**.
