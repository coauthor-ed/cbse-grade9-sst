# Three Kinds of Insurance

<!-- STEP -->
## Health insurance

**Health insurance** covers medical costs: hospital stays, medicines, tests and treatment. The family can focus on getting well instead of on the bills.

In India, the government scheme **Ayushman Bharat Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana** gives health insurance to eligible low-income households.

<!-- STEP -->
## Life insurance

**Life insurance** pays the family if the person who earns for it dies. The money helps the family meet daily expenses, keep the children in school and repay loans.

<!-- STEP -->
## General insurance

**General insurance** covers vehicles, homes, businesses and other property. Motor insurance pays for repairs after a vehicle accident. Property insurance covers fire, theft, flood and earthquake.

Crop insurance covers harvests lost to natural disasters, pests or a fall in prices. Cattle insurance covers the death or disability of farm animals.
