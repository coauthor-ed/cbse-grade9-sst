## A unit taken from the Earth

Sea distances are measured in **nautical miles**. Go once round the Earth through both poles and you pass through 360 degrees. Each degree has 60 minutes, so the full circle has 360 x 60 = 21,600 minutes.

One nautical mile corresponds to one minute of latitude.

<!-- STEP -->
## Exactly 1,852 metres

The Earth is about 40,000 km round, and 40,000 km shared among 21,600 minutes is about 1.85 km. The unit is now defined as exactly 1,852 metres, about 6,076 feet.

This suits sailors, because the latitude scale on the side of a chart also works as a distance ruler.

## One distance, two numbers

Move the marker along the ruler. 12 nautical miles is 22.2 km, and 24 nautical miles is 44.4 km.

The EEZ limit of 200 nautical miles is 370.4 km. The number in kilometres is always larger, because a nautical mile is longer than a kilometre.
