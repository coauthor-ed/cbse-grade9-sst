# A Pot and a Child's Hand

<!-- STEP -->
## What the village is doing

A large pot stands on a stone floor, and each palm leaf inside it has a name written on it. A crowd stands around the pot, and a young child stands beside it.

Press the hand, and the child draws out one leaf. Press again for another name.

<!-- STEP -->
## Where this comes from

This scene comes from a real record. The **Uttaramerur inscription** of the Chola king **Parantaka I**, from the 10th century, is in the Vaikuṇṭha Perumal Temple in Kanchipuram district of Tamil Nadu.

These Tamil inscriptions tell us how a village chose people to run its affairs. A village could have asked its richest man to name them, so why would it use a pot and a child?
