# What Was Asked of a Candidate

<!-- STEP -->
## The Ur and its assembly

Look at the lowest level: the **Ur** and its assembly, keeping its tank, its roads and its records.

The Uttaramerur inscription has one more detail. It specifies that candidates for the village assembly should have "honest earnings" and be "pure" of mind.

<!-- STEP -->
## Why ask this?

A village could have asked only about wealth or skill. Why would an inscription about elections ask about a person's earnings and mind?

The answer lies in an idea that runs through many Indian texts and records: **dharma**. The next lesson explains what this word means.
