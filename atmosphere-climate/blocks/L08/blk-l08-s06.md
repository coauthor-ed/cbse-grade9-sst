## Tick what fits you

This is for you alone, so there are no right answers. In each of the four groups, tick the line that describes you best.

<!-- STEP -->
## Add it up

Low impact is 1 point, medium is 2, high is 3 and very high is 4. Your total lies between 4 and 13.

The score helps you notice your habits. It does not measure you as a person, and some choices depend on where you live.
