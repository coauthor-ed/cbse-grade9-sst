# Saving What Is Left, or Saving First

<!-- STEP -->
## Saving what is left

Meera follows her plan for six months. At the end of each month she drops what is left into a jar: ₹200, ₹0, ₹150, ₹300, ₹100 and ₹250. No month goes badly wrong, but the jar holds only **₹1,000**. The bicycle costs ₹3,000, so she is ₹2,000 short.

<!-- STEP -->
## Saving first

Now Meera turns the plan around.

₹3,000 in six months is ₹500 a month, so she keeps ₹500 aside on the day her money comes and spends the other ₹1,000. After six months the jar holds ₹3,000.

**Save first, then spend: expenses = income - savings.**

<!-- STEP -->
## A budget

A plan like Meera's is a **budget**: a written plan, made in advance, that balances income and expenses. Her budget for one month reads: ₹1,500 comes in, ₹500 goes to savings, and ₹1,000 goes to expenses. Making such a plan is called **budgeting**.
