# What is the sky doing today?

## Today's sky

"Is it going to rain today?" "Will it be bright and sunny?" You ask these before you go out to play.

**Weather** means the conditions of the atmosphere from hour to hour and day to day. This made-up July week in Delhi shows how quickly it can change.

<!-- STEP -->
## Thirty years of Julys

Now put together the Julys of many years and take the average. Delhi's average July comes to 30.0 °C, with 19.3 cm of rain.

**Climate is the average weather of a place over a long time, usually thirty years or more.**

<!-- STEP -->
## One wet week

You might think one very wet week makes Delhi a wet place. It does not, because that week is a tiny part of thirty years and the average hardly moves.
