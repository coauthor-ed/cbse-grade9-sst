## Read It Side by Side

Country C has the highest standard of living in this chart, and no voting rights. Country A has voting rights and a choice of parties, with an average standard of living.

So in this chart, you cannot predict a country's political rights from its standard of living.

## Two Questions for You

- What is the difference between having a voting right in a country with a multi-party system, and in one with a single-party system? Think back to the question of a contest with only one party.
- In which of these countries would you like to stay, and why?

The second question has no fixed answer. Say what you would gain, and what you would give up.
