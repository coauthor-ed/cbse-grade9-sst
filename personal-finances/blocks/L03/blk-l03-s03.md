# Paid In and Added

<!-- STEP -->
## Two parts in every bar

The money in an account comes from two places: what the saver **paid in**, and what the scheme **added**. Each bar on the picture shows the two parts in two colours.

At 65, Bhavisha paid in ₹40,000 and the scheme added **₹1,59,635**, about four times her own money.

<!-- STEP -->
## Interest earns interest

In the first year, 7% of Bhavisha's ₹1,000 is ₹70. The ₹70 stays in the account, so the next year's 7% is worked out on more than ₹1,000.

This is **compounding**: interest left in the account earns interest of its own. The money first put in is the **principal**.

<!-- STEP -->
## Slow first, fast later

After ten years Bhavisha has only about ₹13,816. In her last year, 7% of her large balance adds about **₹13,000** of interest in one year.

Move the year on the picture and watch one year's added part grow.
