# More Than Holding an Election

<!-- STEP -->
## The Whole Process Counts

No, holding an election is not enough. For elections to be meaningful, the entire process must be conducted in a fair and transparent manner. If fairness is not ensured, public confidence in electoral institutions may gradually weaken.

<!-- STEP -->
## Three Situations

Tap each situation. In all three, an election may be held, yet its democratic value is reduced.

- Candidates are influenced by personal favours.
- Votes are not counted accurately.
- Some participants face difficulties in exercising their right to vote, due to accessibility issues.

The process must be **impartial**, **trustworthy** and **inclusive**.

<!-- STEP -->
## What the ECI Uses

Against misinformation, fake news and intimidation, the ECI uses:

- the Representation of the People Acts of 1950 and 1951
- the **Model Code of Conduct**, a set of rules for parties and candidates during an election
- EVMs
- the **VVPAT**, a printed slip that lets the voter check the vote
- voters' awareness campaigns, and other measures
