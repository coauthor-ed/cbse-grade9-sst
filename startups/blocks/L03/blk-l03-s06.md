## Challenges and risks

**Challenges and risks** are threats that disrupt a business or cause losses. For a bakery they include many other bakeries nearby, flour and butter getting costlier, customers changing what they like, and money coming in unevenly from week to week. Every business venture faces risks like these.

<!-- STEP -->
## Perishable inventory

**Inventory** is the goods, materials and other things a business holds to make products or to sell them. A bakery's inventory includes flour, butter and the cakes on its shelves. Bread and pastries are perishable: they spoil in a day or two. Bake too many and the rest is thrown away. Bake too few and customers leave without buying.

## Questions the plan asks

So the plan asks questions such as these. How can we manage perishable inventory well? How can we keep the quality of our products the same every day? What do we do when the number of customers goes up and down? How can we compete with other bakeries nearby?
