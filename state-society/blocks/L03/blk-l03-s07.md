# One King and a Large Land

<!-- STEP -->
## How far can one man go?

Set the king's duties on a large kingdom. He must judge, protect and do good everywhere, and yet he can stand in only one place.

The small ring shows the ground one rider can cover in a day. The rest of the kingdom lies outside it.

<!-- STEP -->
## Not one man alone

The Arthaśhāstra has a short line about this: "one wheel does not move the carriage".

Lesson 4 asks what else a king needed in order to govern.
