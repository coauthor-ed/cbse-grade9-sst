## Close to India

Look at the two arrows near India. In the Arabian Sea, a red arrow curves east and then south-east, towards the southern tip of India. In the Bay of Bengal, a red arrow runs north-east. Both are red, so both are warm currents.

## Take a note

The currents near India change direction with the monsoon winds, so this map shows one pattern, not the whole year. Write down what you see, and think about these:

- Which way does each arrow run?
- What do the red arrows tell you about where the water comes from?
- Why might the same sea need a second map for another season?
