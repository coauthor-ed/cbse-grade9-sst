# A Budget in Four Steps

<!-- STEP -->
## Four steps

A budget is built in four steps, and the same four steps repeat every month:

1. **Identify your expenses**: list what the month has to pay for.
2. **Set a savings goal**: decide how much to keep aside before spending.
3. **Track your spending**: check each payment against the plan.
4. **Review and adjust**: at the end of the month, compare the plan with what happened.

## A goal you can check

"Save more" is only a wish. A goal answers four questions. Here are Meera's answers:

- **What** is the money for? A bicycle.
- **How much** will it cost? ₹3,000.
- **By when**? In six months.
- **What steps** will get her there? ₹500 kept aside every month.

Other goals work the same way: a school trip next month, sports equipment in six months, higher education, or money for emergencies.

## Review and adjust

The last step leads back to the first: what Meera learns this month changes next month's plan. A budget also stops money going on things nobody planned, such as eating out too often or buying on impulse. It helps a person live within their means, which means spending no more than comes in.
