# Sailors and the wind

<!-- STEP -->
## At the mercy of the wind

Long ago, sailors came to India in sailing ships with no engines, so they could only go where the wind took them. They were among the first to notice something strange: for months the wind blew one way, then for months it blew the other way.

<!-- STEP -->
## A word from the traders

Arab traders who sailed to India named this turning of the wind after their word *mausim*, which means season. That word became **monsoon**.

<!-- STEP -->
## It is about wind

Many people think monsoon just means rain. **Monsoon is the seasonal reversal in wind direction during a year.** The rain comes with it, but the wind turning around is the monsoon. Why does the wind turn?
