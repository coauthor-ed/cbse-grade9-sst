# Market Supply

<!-- STEP -->
## Three sellers

Seller A is not the only seller. Seller B and Seller C also have mango stalls in the same market. At ₹100 per kg, Seller A offers 2 kg, Seller B offers 4 kg and Seller C offers 6 kg. Together they offer 2 + 4 + 6 = 12 kg.

Tap a price on the picture to see what each seller puts out.

<!-- STEP -->
## The market supply schedule

**Market supply** is the sum of the supply of all the sellers at each price. Add the three sellers at every price, and you get the market supply schedule.

| Price per kg | Seller A | Seller B | Seller C | Market supply |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ₹50 | 1 kg | 3 kg | 2 kg | 6 kg |
| ₹100 | 2 kg | 4 kg | 6 kg | 12 kg |
| ₹150 | 3 kg | 7 kg | 8 kg | 18 kg |

<!-- STEP -->
## The market supply curve

Now plot the market supply at each price: 6 kg at ₹50, 12 kg at ₹100 and 18 kg at ₹150. Join the three points, and you get the **market supply curve**, SmSm'. Like Seller A's curve, it slopes up from left to right.
