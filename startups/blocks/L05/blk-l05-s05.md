## Investment

**Investment** means putting money or other resources into something, such as a machine, a project or a business, in the hope that it brings income, profit or a rise in value in future. Suppose ABC Bakery buys a second oven. You pay for it now, and it lets the bakery bake and sell more cakes in the months ahead.

<!-- STEP -->
## Paying for it with a loan

The money can come from your own savings or from a loan. A loan has to be paid back, and the bakery also pays **interest** on it: extra money for the use of the loan. Interest is an expense, and ABC Bakery's March account shows ₹10,000 of it. Finance also watches the cash flow: the money coming in and going out.
