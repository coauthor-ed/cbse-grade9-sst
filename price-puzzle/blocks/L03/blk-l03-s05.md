# Seller A and the Whole Market

<!-- STEP -->
## The same rise in price

Let the price rise from ₹50 to ₹150 per kg. Seller A offers 2 kg more: 3 kg in place of 1 kg. The whole market offers 12 kg more: 18 kg in place of 6 kg. Each seller adds a little, and together they add a lot.

<!-- STEP -->
## Two curves on one graph

Draw both supply curves on one graph with the same scales. Both slope up. Seller A's curve is steep, and the market supply curve is much flatter, just as the market demand curve was flatter than Srivalli's.
