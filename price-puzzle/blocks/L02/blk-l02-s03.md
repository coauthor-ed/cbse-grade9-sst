# Why the Market Curve Is Flatter

<!-- STEP -->
## The same fall in price

Let the price fall from ₹150 to ₹50 per kg. Srivalli buys 2 kg more: 3 kg in place of 1 kg. The whole market buys 12 kg more: 18 kg in place of 6 kg. Each buyer adds a little, and together they add a lot.

<!-- STEP -->
## Two curves on one graph

Draw both curves on one graph with the same scales. Srivalli's curve is steep. The market demand curve is much flatter.

**The same change in price brings a much bigger change in the amount the whole market buys, so the market curve is flatter.**
