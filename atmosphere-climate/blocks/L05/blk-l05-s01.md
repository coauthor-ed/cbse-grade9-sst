# A coast the size of a country

<!-- STEP -->
## The same idea, months long

On a coast, land and sea take turns at being warmer, day and night. India and the Indian Ocean take turns too, but over whole seasons.

<!-- STEP -->
## Land changes, sea stays

Through the year the land heats up and cools down much faster than the sea around it. Inland India reaches 32-40 °C in summer, and the north-west falls to 10-15 °C in winter. The line shows Delhi month by month, while the sea changes far less.
