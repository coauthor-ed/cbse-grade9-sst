# Choosing the Election Dates

<!-- STEP -->
## When Is an Election Due?

Two events start the work. The first is the end of the Legislature's term, which is five years. The second is the Legislature being dissolved before its term is complete.

**Either one is enough for the ECI to set the election machinery in motion.**

<!-- STEP -->
## Four Things to Plan Around

India is large and diverse, so a date that suits one place can be hard for another. The ECI must consider several factors, such as:

- weather conditions
- the agricultural cycle
- school and university examinations
- festivals

This is one example year for one place. Tap a factor to see when it matters.

## Polling in Phases

A general election does not happen on one day. Polling runs in phases over several weeks, because polling teams and security staff must reach every part of the country.

**So the ECI has to find several weeks that work, and every State has its own weather, harvests, examinations and festivals.**
