# Pack the School List

## ₹3,000 for the list

School reopens next week, and Meera's family gives her **₹3,000** for the school list. The shop's list has ten items with their prices. Some are needs and some are wants, and they are mixed up.

Put every need in the bag and keep the total within ₹3,000. Whatever is left is her savings. Nothing is timed.
