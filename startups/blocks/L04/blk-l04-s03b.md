## Finance

**Finance** is the management of money in a business: planning, getting and using money so the business can run and grow. For ABC Bakery you plan to pay costs such as rent, ovens, mixers, ingredients and packaging. You plan to invest in growth. And you manage profits and cash flow, the money coming in and going out.

<!-- STEP -->
## Accounting

**Accounting** is recording, organising and reporting the money a business takes in and pays out. You keep track of the money coming in, called **revenue**, and the money going out, called **expenses**, and check that every record is correct. The records show what the bakery owns and what it has borrowed, and they help it follow the country's tax laws.

<!-- STEP -->
## The profit and loss account

One important record is the **profit and loss account**. It shows whether the business made a profit or a loss over a time, such as a month or a year. This helps the owners decide what to spend and what to invest. The next lesson reads ABC Bakery's profit and loss account for one month.
