## chamoli-flood-2021.jpg

- Used by: `blocks/L07/blk-l07-s05.json` (`image.src`).
- Stands in for the source's `Fig. 2.20` (p.17 / book p.29). The
  textbook's printed photograph is **not** to be extracted.
- To produce: download a photograph of the **February 2021 Chamoli
  flood / rock-and-ice avalanche damage in the Rishi Ganga or Dhauli
  Ganga gorge, Chamoli district, Uttarakhand**, from Wikimedia Commons
  or another open-licence repository. Save it here and record below:
  source page URL, file name on the source, author or credited
  institution if any, and the exact licence (Commons photos are usually
  CC BY-SA or CC0 - check the file page, do not assume).
- What the slide needs, from `FIGURES.md` Fig. 2.20. Four details are
  load-bearing, and every one of them is a claim the prose makes:
  1. **The riverbed running as a torrent of grey silt and pulverised
     rock.** The slide's whole point is that the water arrived in one
     rush, and a wide shot of the loaded channel is what carries that.
     A picture of bare rock downstream does not.
  2. **The banks and walls scoured back to bare rock**, with no
     vegetation on them. This is the evidence that the water was far
     bigger than the ordinary stream in the frame.
  3. **Destroyed infrastructure still in place** - a broken bridge, a
     washed-out road, or the debris of a hydropower project. The
     chapter's damage list (buildings, roads, bridges, hydel projects,
     village connectivity) needs at least one of these visible or
     photographed; the block's `alt` text names them.
  4. **A steep-sided gorge**, so the reader can see that whatever came
     down came down a confined channel with nowhere to spread. A wide
     floodplain view would not support the slide.
- The date matters: this must be the **2021** event, not an earlier
  Uttarakhand flood and not a 2013 Kedarnath photograph. Kedarnath
  belongs to L10's `Fig. 2.29` and must not be substituted here - the
  two slides make different points (Chamoli is the suddenness from a
  single strike on the dam; Kedarnath is the town that was scoured
  out around a standing temple).
- A caption identifying the location, if the photograph itself has no
  visible signage, is welcome in the file name and in the block's `alt`
  text (already written); no text should be overlaid onto the image.
