# Ten Stations, One Country

## How much rain?

The table gives monthly temperature and rainfall for ten stations across India. Look at the rain for the whole year.

**Leh gets 8.5 cm and Shillong gets 225.3 cm**, more than 26 times as much.

<!-- STEP -->
## Nearest the equator first

Arrange the ten stations by their distance from the equator. Latitude tells you this, so **Thiruvananthapuram** comes first and **Leh** comes last.

Tap the sort buttons to reorder the rows yourself.

<!-- STEP -->
## Rain does not follow latitude

Read the rain in this new order. It does not rise or fall steadily.

Shillong, at 24°34' N, is the wettest station. Jodhpur, less than two degrees further north, gets only 36.6 cm. **Something other than latitude must explain this.**
