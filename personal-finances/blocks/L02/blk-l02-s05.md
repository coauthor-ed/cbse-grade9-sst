# Save First: a Family's Year

<!-- STEP -->
## ₹1,00,000 in a year

A family gets ₹1,00,000 in a year. It keeps **₹20,000** aside first, so the year's spending has to fit in the other ₹80,000. This is Meera's rule again: expenses = income - savings.

Move the savings share on the picture. Keep 30% aside and ₹70,000 is left to spend. Keep 5% and ₹95,000 is left to spend, but very little is kept for later.

<!-- STEP -->
## Can a family with a small income save?

Think of a family that earns ₹12,000 a month, where rent, food, school fees and travel take most of it. It can still save first, but in small amounts, such as ₹300 a month.

Small amounts add up over the months. Kept for emergencies, they also save the family from borrowing and paying interest later.
