# Where the Word Comes From

<!-- STEP -->
## Two old pieces

You already know what this word means in plain words - **fixing the number of seats, and fixing where one constituency ends**.

Now take the word itself apart. It is built from two pieces that are older than English:

- **de-** - a prefix you meet everywhere. It means *away from*, *undoing*, *the reverse of*.
- **limen** - Latin for *a limit or a threshold*. English **limit** is the same piece.

Set them together and the word means what it sounds like: **setting a limit.**

<!-- STEP -->
## And what gets the limit

The pieces and the job line up exactly:

- you **set a limit** on how many people one representative answers for - that is the number of seats,
- and you **set a limit** on where one constituency stops and the next begins - that is the line.

Number set, line set. The word is not a label somebody attached afterwards; it is the shortest description of the job there is.
