Media provenance - L05

Per `docs/copyright-and-sourcing.md`: the textbook's own photographs are
never extracted. Each photograph below is an openly-licensed photograph of
Indian home voting, standing in for the source's `Fig. 7.8` (p.11 / book
p.171).

Both are from one set of photographs made by the same photographer at one
home-voting visit in Bhopal, Madhya Pradesh, on 7 November 2023, and are
licensed CC BY-SA 4.0, which permits reuse with attribution.

Downloaded as 960px wide JPEG renditions of the Wikimedia Commons originals
(Commons `Special:FilePath` at width=960). The original file name and its
Commons file page are recorded below so either can be replaced at full
resolution without changing the block.

Used by `blocks/L05/blk-l05-s06.json` -> `image.src` and
`reveal.images.compartment.src`.

Note on eligibility: the source's eligibility criteria - senior citizens
above 85 years of age, and persons with disabilities with a benchmark
disability of 40% or higher - are NOT depicted or asserted by these
photographs. Both photographer file names say "above 80", which is an older
threshold than the chapter's and is a label on the file, not something the
photograph shows. The slide prose therefore states no age for either voter;
the criteria live on `blk-l05-s03`.

Caveat recorded for the reviewer: the two frames come from one household on
one morning, so this pair shows the measure in one setting. The source's own
`Fig. 7.8` pairs an urban frame with a remote one; no openly-licensed
photograph of remote or tribal-area home voting was located on Wikimedia
Commons during this stage, so the second frame is a different view of the
same visit rather than a different part of the country. This is logged as a
deviation in `GENERATION-LOG.md`.
