## Danger that comes quickly

Oceans and seas can give rise to sudden hazards. **Cyclones** are large rotating storms, and **tsunamis** are giant waves caused by earthquakes. People, communities and governments have to act in advance, because there is little time once the danger arrives.

<!-- STEP -->
## An alarm for the coast

Scientists have developed **Early Warning Systems**, which work like giant alarm systems for coastal areas. They detect the danger, predict what it will do and send warnings in time, so that people can evacuate and stay safe. We start with cyclones and come to tsunamis next.
