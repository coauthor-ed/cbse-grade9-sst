## Why rules matter

Without shared rules the oceans could become dangerous and chaotic, with more pollution, accidents, collisions and disputes over resources.

Common standards for all ships, whichever country they belong to, help keep the oceans safe, clean and open for trade and travel. They also let countries use marine resources without conflict while protecting marine life, ships, sailors and coastal communities.
