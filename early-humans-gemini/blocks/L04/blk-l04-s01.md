# Too Small to Hold

On a centimetre scale sit twelve tiny pieces of stone: trapezoids, crescents, triangles and slim blades. Each one is one to three centimetres long, thin, and sharp along one edge. They are called **microliths**, because *micro* means small and *lith* means stone.

<!-- STEP id="bone" -->
## Engraved Bone Points

Bone points come from layers of the same kind. Each one carries a repeating zigzag pattern, and the lines were cut on purpose, not worn in by use.

<!-- STEP id="beads" -->
## Beads From an Ostrich Egg

Round beads made from ostrich eggshell lie beside the blades. Each bead was shaped and pierced through the centre so that it could be strung.

## The Floor They Came From

A fieldworker brushes the floor of a rock shelter. Small finds like these come out of floors like this one.
