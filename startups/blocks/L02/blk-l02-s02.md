## Seven women on a rooftop

In 1959, seven homemakers from a **chawl** in Mumbai met on its rooftop. A chawl is an old building of many floors, with small one-room homes. They had borrowed equipment, a recipe for papads and ₹80. They had no factory, no investors and no business degrees.

**Predict:** with no factory, how did the seven women make their papads?
