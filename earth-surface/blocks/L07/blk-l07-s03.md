## From V to U

The valley ends with a wide floor and steep sides. Glaciers **widen and deepen** river valleys, and the ice reaches far up both sides. The result is a **U-shaped valley**.

Press **Let the ice flow**.

## The Valley Left Hanging

A smaller glacier joined this big one from the side. It cut down less deeply, so after the ice melts its valley ends high above the main floor. This is a **hanging valley**, and a stream may fall from its lip.

## When the Sea Comes In

Some glacial valleys reach the coast. When the sea floods one, it forms a deep, narrow inlet called a **fjord**. Norway and New Zealand have many. India has none.

Press **Let the sea in**.
