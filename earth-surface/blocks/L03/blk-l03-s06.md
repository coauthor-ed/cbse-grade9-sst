# Bhūkampa: An Early Account

## Shaking of the Earth

In early times too, earthquakes were known as *bhūkampa*, meaning the shaking of the Earth.

In the *Bṛihatsaṁhitā*, Varāhamihira gave a whole section to earthquakes. Sort each card by what he noted and how he explained it.

<!-- STEP -->
## What he noted, and how he explained it

He noted that changes in wind, rain, clouds, animal behaviour and planetary alignments could signal earthquakes.

He attributed earthquakes to four elemental forces: *Vāyu* (wind), *Agni* (fire), *Indra* (heaven or thunder) and *Varuṇa* (water). Each was linked to specific constellations and regions.

<!-- STEP -->
## An early attempt

His account was an early attempt in India to blend observations with cosmological reasoning and physical phenomena.

Today, scientists explain earthquakes by moving plates. They still cannot use such signs to predict one.
