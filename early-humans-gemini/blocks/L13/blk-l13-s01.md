# Who Built This?

A huge wall runs along the mountain ridges of northern China and disappears over the horizon. This is the Great Wall, shown here in a drawing, and most of what you see in pictures like this is the later stone wall.

<!-- STEP id="watchtower" -->
## Towers on the Heights

Square towers stand along the high ground. A lookout up here could see far across the valleys and pass on a warning.

<!-- STEP id="rampart-road" -->
## A Road on the Wall

A paved path runs along the top. Soldiers and messengers could move along it quickly, across land that would otherwise slow them down.

<!-- STEP id="ruined-section" -->
## An Older Wall

In other places, older parts lie half buried and worn down by rain. Many of these were never stone at all, but rammed earth, which is soil pressed hard between boards.

**Stone and earth give no name and no date. So who built this wall, and when?**
