# Mostly Nitrogen

<!-- STEP -->
## Nitrogen fills most of it

**Nitrogen** fills most of the jar: 78 out of every 100 parts of air.

Oxygen is a fair guess, because it is the gas we need to breathe. It comes second, with 21 parts out of 100.

<!-- STEP -->
## The whole jar in 100 squares

Each square is 1 per cent of dry air. Water vapour comes on top of these shares.

- **Nitrogen:** 78 per cent
- **Oxygen:** 21 per cent
- **Argon:** 0.93 per cent
- **Carbon dioxide:** 0.04 per cent
- **Others:** 0.03 per cent

Tap **Pie** to see the same shares as a pie diagram.

<!-- STEP -->
## Inside the last square

Together, the small shares fill just one square. Look through the magnifying glass.

Argon takes most of that square. Carbon dioxide is a thin sliver, yet it matters a great deal, as you will see later. The others are helium, neon, krypton, xenon, ozone and hydrogen.
