# Five Pillars of Personal Finance

<!-- STEP -->
## Five pillars

Personal finance stands on five pillars: **income**, **budgeting**, **saving**, **investing** and **protection** (insurance and careful borrowing). The chapter also asks where savings can go, how to protect them, and what income tax is. It starts with income, because without money coming in, nothing can be spent, saved or invested.

<!-- STEP -->
## Five kinds of income

**Income** is the money a person receives, regularly or now and then, for goods sold or work done.

A teacher earns a **salary**. A shopkeeper earns **business profit**. A person who lets out a room earns **rent**. Money kept in a bank earns **interest**. A person who owns shares in a company receives a **dividend**, a part of its profit.

## Earn while you learn

Some students earn while they study, through part-time work, internships or apprenticeships. These bring in some money and also teach skills and how a workplace runs. Before taking such work, think about three things: the skill you will gain, the time it takes from study and rest, and whether the work is safe and allowed at your age.
