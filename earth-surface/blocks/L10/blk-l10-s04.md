## Snow on a Steep Slope

An **avalanche** is a sudden slide of snow down a steep mountain slope. Heavy snowfall in a short time adds extra weight to the snowpack, and this is most dangerous when the new snow rests on a weak layer.

Press **Heavy snow** and see how the load builds on the weak layer.

<!-- STEP -->
## What Sets It Off

Three other things can make the snow unstable. Tap each one.

- **Warm spell:** a sudden rise in temperature melts some snow and reduces the friction that holds it together.
- **Strong wind:** it piles up snow unevenly and makes fragile layers.
- **Disturbance:** earthquakes and vibrations, or skiing, trekking and construction.

<!-- STEP -->
## Where, and What Helps

In India, avalanches are a danger in Jammu and Kashmir, Ladakh, Himachal Pradesh, Uttarakhand and Sikkim. In October 2022 an avalanche struck a mountaineering team on Draupadi ka Danda in Uttarakhand.

Forecasts warn people to avoid steep, loaded slopes after heavy snow. Snow fences hold snow in place, and tunnels keep roads out of its path.
