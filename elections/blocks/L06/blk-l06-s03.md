# Four Jobs of a Party

<!-- STEP -->
## Work at the Grassroots

You might think a party's work is only to put up candidates. Fig 7.9 of your textbook shows much more: political parties are essential to a democratic system, as they work directly at the **grassroots** level.

Grassroots means the streets, wards and villages where people live.

<!-- STEP -->
## Four Jobs

- **Organise public opinion:** gather what many people want into one clear demand.
- **Campaign on specific issues:** speak up for a named issue.
- **Offer voters meaningful choices:** give voters real differences to choose between.
- **Enable voters to make informed decisions during elections:** give them what they need to decide.

Tap a job to see it in one made-up town.

<!-- STEP -->
## What These Jobs Give Democracy

Parties help democracy to function by providing four things:

- **governance:** running the government
- **accountability:** answering to the people
- **representation:** speaking for the people
- **public engagement:** drawing people into public life
