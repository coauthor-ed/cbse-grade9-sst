## Old stories helped

Researchers suggest that Simeulue was spared partly because of local folklore. Stories passed down through generations, about a danger called *smong*, helped people recognise the signs of a tsunami and respond quickly. The Indian Ocean had no tsunami warning system in 2004.

<!-- STEP -->
## Minutes or hours

Water reached Simeulue in minutes, and Chennai in about two hours. Close to an earthquake there is no time to wait for a message, so people need to read the signs themselves.

<!-- STEP -->
## Signs you can read

Move at once if you feel strong shaking, hear a roar from the sea or see the sea pull far back. A bare sea bed may look safe, but the water is coming back. Go to high ground, and do not go to look.

Ask your elders about stories of natural disasters in your own area, and share them with your classmates. How can such stories help people stay safe?
