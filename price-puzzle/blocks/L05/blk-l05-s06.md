# Hotel Prices Change Every Day

<!-- STEP -->
## One hotel, three prices

Hotels do not charge the same price, called the tariff, for a room all year. Suppose a hotel in Goa has 100 rooms. On a Monday in July, when few tourists come, a room costs ₹1,500 a night. On a Saturday in December it costs ₹8,000. On New Year's Eve, when demand is very high, it costs ₹25,000.

<!-- STEP -->
## Prices can change overnight

If a group tour cancels its booking, rooms are suddenly empty. To fill them, the hotel may cut the tariff by 40 per cent overnight, for example from ₹8,000 to ₹4,800. Hotels may change the tariff several times a day to earn the most revenue: all the money earned from sales, before costs are taken out.

<!-- STEP -->
## What the tariff depends on

The tariff also depends on how fast rooms are getting booked and on the tariffs of nearby hotels. Festivals or events in the area, the weather forecast, the days left before arrival and past booking trends matter too. **Markets like this are dynamic: their prices keep changing as conditions change.**
