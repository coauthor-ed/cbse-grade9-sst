## A trawler off Chennai

Here is the coast around Chennai, drawn from Survey of India maps. A trawler from another country is fishing 100 nautical miles out from the city, which is 185 kilometres. A nautical mile is 1,852 metres. The Indian coast guard ship is much closer in, off the fishing villages, where it can be seen from the beach.

<!-- STEP -->
## How far is that

Set the coast guard ship going. It covers 20 nautical miles an hour, so 100 nautical miles is five hours of sailing. The trawler is fishing through every hour of it.

<!-- STEP -->
## The question

A foreign trawler is fishing 100 nautical miles off Chennai, and the coast guard ship is five hours away from it. Is it taking India's fish?

That is not a question about fishing. It is a question about who may fish where, and about how far a country's reach goes out into the sea.
