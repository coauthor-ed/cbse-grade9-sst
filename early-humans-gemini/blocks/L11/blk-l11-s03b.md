# Where the Idea of a Lasting Body May Have Come From

<!-- STEP id="pits" -->
## Graves in the Sand

Long before pyramids, the earliest Egyptians buried their dead in shallow pits in the hot desert sand.

<!-- STEP id="natural-drying" -->
## The Sand Dried the Body

Hot, dry sand pulls water out of a body quickly. Decay needs water, so a body in dry sand could stay recognisable for a very long time.

<!-- STEP id="looked-like-evidence" -->
## What People Saw

Imagine finding such a body years later, with skin and hair still in place, when nobody knew why. It could seem that the person had not fully gone. Historians think sights like this may have helped the belief grow.

<!-- STEP id="wealth-broke-it" -->
## Coffins Changed Things

One explanation goes further. Richer burials moved the body away from the sand, into coffins and tombs, and such bodies decayed.

**Historians think embalmers may have been trying to do on purpose what the desert had done by itself.**
