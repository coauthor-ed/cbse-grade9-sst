# A Fridge on EMI

<!-- STEP -->
## ₹3,000 a month

A family wants a refrigerator that costs **₹30,000**, but does not want to pay it all at once. It takes a loan and repays **₹3,000 a month for 12 months**.

This fixed monthly payment is an **EMI** (equated monthly instalment). Each EMI includes part of the loan (the **principal**) and the interest charged by the lender.

<!-- STEP -->
## ₹36,000 in all

Twelve payments of ₹3,000 come to ₹36,000. That is ₹6,000 more than the price, and the ₹6,000 is the interest.

Why would a family pay more? It gets the fridge now and uses it every day, and it does not need ₹30,000 in one go. Saving first would take months without the fridge.

<!-- STEP -->
## Too many EMIs

The picture adds a phone EMI and a scooter EMI to the same monthly income. Each EMI makes the savings and the emergency money smaller.

**Every EMI is paid out of next month's income, so too many EMIs leave nothing for a bad month.**
