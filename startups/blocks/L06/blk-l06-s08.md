## The Dandekar brothers

In 1931, two brothers, Digambar and Govind Dandekar, started a small business in South Mumbai selling ink powder. They had few resources, so they sold liquid ink in recycled glass bottles. At first they had money problems, and they struggled to make customers trust their products.

<!-- STEP -->
## Good quality at fair prices

The brothers did not give up. They focused on good-quality stationery at prices people could afford. As the business grew, they noticed that artists in India could not get good art materials, and they saw this as an opportunity.

<!-- STEP -->
## From a laboratory to a well-known brand

They travelled to Glasgow to study colour chemistry and improve their products. Back in India, they set up a laboratory to make colours and art supplies suited to Indian needs. Over time, the company made pencils, crayons, geometry boxes and paints, and became a well-known brand.
