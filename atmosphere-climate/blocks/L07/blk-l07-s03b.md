# Why Delhi Gets More Than Jodhpur

<!-- STEP -->
## Same height, different rain

Delhi gets nearly twice Jodhpur's rain. Height cannot explain it, because the two stand at 219 m and 224 m. Their latitudes differ by less than 3 degrees, too little to explain it.

So we look at the air that reaches each place.

<!-- STEP -->
## Two arms of the monsoon

Moist air from the Bay of Bengal turns up the Ganga plain to Delhi. Jodhpur gets air from the Arabian Sea branch.

One reason this air gives little rain is the Aravalli hills, which run along the wind, so the air is hardly lifted.

<!-- STEP -->
## A shorter stay as well

The monsoon also stays for less time at Jodhpur: under 11 weeks, against about 12 to 13 at Delhi. That helps, but a stay only a little shorter cannot nearly halve the rain. **The air and what lifts it matter more.**
