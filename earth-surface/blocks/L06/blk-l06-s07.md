## Read the Coast's Stage

The game hands you a coastline at one of five stages: an intact **headland**, a **sea cave**, a **sea arch**, a **sea stack**, or the **stump** a stack leaves behind.

Before anything moves you set two dials. The first is the **wave energy** - how hard the sea is working. The second is the **rock hardness** - how well the rock holds together when the sea works on it.

Then you claim a stage, and the coast runs the sequence with your settings. A high-energy sea on soft rock gets a long way in one run; a gentle sea on hard rock barely marks the headland at all.

Your claim is only right if the sequence your settings produced actually reaches that stage.

The tray will accept a claim that is wrong, and the feedback tells you the rule, not the answer. There is no clock and nothing counts down - take as long as you like, and rerun as often as you want.
