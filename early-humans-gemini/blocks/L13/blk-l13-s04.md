# What Else Is a Wall For?

The book asks what other reasons there could have been for building the Great Wall. Keeping raiders out is the reason it gives. Here are three more that historians suggest.

<!-- STEP id="frontier-gate" -->
## Gates and Taxes

A wall does not only stop people. It also pushes them towards a few gates. Guards at a gate could check who was passing and what they carried, and they could collect tax on goods.

<!-- STEP id="garrison-watchtower" -->
## People on the Frontier

Towers needed soldiers, and repairs needed builders. These people had to eat, so farmers were settled nearby. In this way, empty border land could become farmed land.

<!-- STEP id="frontier-settlement" -->
## A Line on the Land

A wall also shows clearly where a ruler's land ends. People inside it knew whose laws and taxes applied to them.

**A wall can guard, tax, settle and mark a border.** These are reasoned ideas, and the book asks you to explore more.
