# Challenge: Build the Dossier

<!-- STEP -->
## Four towns, and three cards each

Four towns are on the table. Each one is given two things and nothing else: **a latitude band**, and **whether it sits on the coast or inland**.

For each town you have to file three cards from the deck:

- the **thermal zone** it lies in - torrid, temperate or frigid
- the **temperature trend** that zone implies
- the **rainfall season** its position implies

Nothing is labelled on the towns. The latitude and the coast are the only evidence you get, and the cards have to be deduced from them.

<!-- STEP -->
## It is checked for consistency, not for luck

This is the part worth reading twice, because it is what makes the game a puzzle rather than a quiz.

A dossier is accepted only when the three cards on a town **agree with each other**. A set that puts a town in the frigid zone with a summer temperature peak and a summer rainfall will not close, no matter how confidently you placed the cards.

There is no combination of cards that looks reasonable and is still wrong in a way the game will accept. And there is no combination that is right by accident - if the zone is right, the other two follow from it.

<!-- STEP -->
## Take your time

No timer. No moves to protect. Unlimited retries, and the same four towns each time, so a retry is a second look rather than a new roll.

**If you can win by picking three cards that look like they go together, you have not used the latitude yet.**
