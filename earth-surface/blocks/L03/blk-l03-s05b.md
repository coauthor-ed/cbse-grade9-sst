# What Should You Do in an Earthquake?

<!-- STEP -->
## Indoors: Drop, Cover, Hold On

Stay inside until the shaking stops. Tap each step to see it.

- **Drop** to the floor.
- **Cover** your head under a strong table.
- **Hold On** to the table until the shaking stops.

Keep away from glass and heavy things that can fall. Do not use a lift. After strong shaking, expect aftershocks.

<!-- STEP -->
## Outdoors: go to open ground

Move to open ground, away from buildings, walls and electric poles. Stay there until the shaking stops.

<!-- STEP -->
## Before it happens

Precautions start long before an earthquake. Buildings made to resist shaking save lives. At home, fix tall shelves to the wall, and know where the strong table and the open ground are.
