# The Special Intensive Revision

<!-- STEP -->
## What the SIR Is

The ECI also conducts a **Special Intensive Revision (SIR)**, which involves updating, verifying and correcting the electoral rolls.

Through the SIR, it ensures that no eligible citizen is left out and no ineligible person is included in the roll.

<!-- STEP -->
## Who Is Added, and Who Is Deleted

The exercise ensures the addition of all voters, especially young voters who have just turned 18 and may be left out due to a lack of awareness or any other reason.

The SIR also deletes names on four grounds. Tap each one to see it on the roll:

- death of the voter
- change of residence
- duplicate enrolment
- being permanently untraceable

<!-- STEP -->
## Time to Object

The ECI gives time to raise **claims or objections** against the revised roll. A claim or objection is a formal request to correct an entry.

The ECI settles these before it publishes the final electoral roll.
