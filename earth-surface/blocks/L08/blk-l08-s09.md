## A Wall of Dust

Where long dry spells and thin plant cover leave the soil bare, strong wind can lift so much dust that it hides the sky. That is a **dust storm**, and we will come back to it in our last lesson on disasters.

## The Last Agent

You have now met four of the five agents that shape the surface: running water, waves, glaciers and wind. All of them work in the open.

The fifth works where we cannot see it. It moves through solid rock, and we go there now.
