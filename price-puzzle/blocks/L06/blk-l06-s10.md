# Sanitisers in 2020

<!-- STEP -->
## Hoarding and black marketing

During COVID-19 the demand for sanitisers rose very fast. Shops ran out and prices rose sharply.

Some shopkeepers kept large stocks hidden, hoping to sell later at a higher price. This is hoarding. Some sold sanitisers secretly, above the highest price the law allowed. This is black marketing.

<!-- STEP -->
## A highest price of ₹100

The government declared sanitisers an essential commodity under the Essential Commodities Act, 1955. It set a highest price of ₹100 for a 200 ml bottle. Meanwhile, many companies started making sanitisers, and soon they were easy to buy at fair prices.
