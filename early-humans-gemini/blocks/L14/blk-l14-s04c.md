# Could Bronze Have Mattered?

Mehrgarh had stored food and later copper, and it stayed a village. So something more was needed before cities and rulers could grow. One idea, which is not proven, points to bronze.

<!-- STEP id="notlocal" -->
## Tin Comes From Far Away

Bronze is copper mixed with a little tin. Few places have tin, so most cities had to get it through trade.

<!-- STEP id="requires-organisation" -->
## Trade Needs Organising

Someone had to send traders, store what came back and keep count of it. You have seen that scribes in Sumer kept exactly such records.

<!-- STEP id="tips-it" -->
## An Idea, Not a Proof

Historians suggest that this kind of trade may have added to the power of those who ran it. Metal alone was not enough: you saw that in China, where metal came around 2000 BCE and cities around 1600 BCE.

**Bronze did not make a state by itself. Organised trade may have helped.**
