# Early Sites in India

People have lived in the Indian subcontinent for about 2 million years. Two sites have especially early dates: **Attirampakkam** in Tamil Nadu, about 1.5 to 1.7 million years ago, and **Isampur** in Karnataka, 1.2 million years ago.

<!-- STEP id="palaeolithic" -->
## Palaeolithic Sites

Palaeolithic sites are found across the subcontinent, and Attirampakkam and Isampur are two of them. Here are some others:

- Hathnora, in the Narmada valley
- Didwana and Chirki-Nevasa, in the west
- Jwalapuram, in the south
- Riwat and Sanghao Cave, in the northwest

<!-- STEP id="mesolithic" -->
## Mesolithic Sites

Mesolithic sites include Bagor and Loteshwar in the west, the rock shelters of Bhimbetka in central India, and the Teri sites in the far south. Their tools are smaller and their dates are later.

<!-- STEP id="neolithic" -->
## Neolithic Sites

The Neolithic sites on this map are Mehrgarh, Burzahom, Lahuradewa, Daojali Hading and Sanganakallu. They belong to the age of farming, which we reach later in this chapter.

**People lived across the subcontinent long before anyone farmed.**
