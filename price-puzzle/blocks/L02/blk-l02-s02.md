# Add Across Each Price

<!-- STEP -->
## Add the three baskets

Together they want 12 kg: 2 + 4 + 6 = 12. We can do the same at every price. At ₹150 per kg, they buy 1 + 2 + 3 = 6 kg. At ₹50 per kg, they buy 3 + 6 + 9 = 18 kg.

Tap a price on the picture to fill the three baskets.

<!-- STEP -->
## Market demand

The total amount that all the buyers want at each price is called **market demand**. It is the sum of the individual demand of every buyer. Written as a table, it is the market demand schedule.

| Price per kg | Srivalli | Alex | Israt | Market demand |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ₹150 | 1 kg | 2 kg | 3 kg | 6 kg |
| ₹100 | 2 kg | 4 kg | 6 kg | 12 kg |
| ₹50 | 3 kg | 6 kg | 9 kg | 18 kg |

<!-- STEP -->
## The market demand curve

Now plot the market demand at each price. E is 6 kg at ₹150, F is 12 kg at ₹100 and G is 18 kg at ₹50. Join E, F and G, and you get the **market demand curve**, DmDm'. Like Srivalli's curve, it slopes down from left to right.
