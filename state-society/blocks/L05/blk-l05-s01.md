# An Order for a Far Village

<!-- STEP -->
## 500 kilometres and no phone

A king in his capital wants a village tank repaired. The village is 500 km away, and there is no phone and no post, so a messenger must walk for weeks to reach it.

You might think a strong king simply sends his word to every village. One man could not do that, because a large kingdom held far too many villages for him to watch.

Press **Send the order** and follow the messenger across the kingdom.

<!-- STEP -->
## The land is cut into smaller pieces

Early Indian kingdoms were generally divided into **provinces**, then **divisions** and **districts**, with **villages** at the bottom.

Press **Next level** and the map closes in, one level at a time. At each level an officer receives the order and passes it on.

**A far village could be governed because each level between it and the capital had its own officers.**

The first kingdom we will look at is in the Deccan.
