# Bhimbetka: Paintings on the Rock

Hundreds of painted rock shelters stand in the hills of Madhya Pradesh. This is Bhimbetka, a World Heritage Site, where people lived in Mesolithic and earlier times.

<!-- STEP id="hunt" -->
## Hunters in Red Paint

On the rock walls are red figures: hunters with spears and bows, and the animals they hunted.

<!-- STEP id="dance" -->
## The Dancing Groups

Other walls show groups of figures dancing, with their arms linked. Similar scenes appear in shelter after shelter.

<!-- STEP id="pigment" -->
## One Red Pigment

The paint was a pigment: a red-brown earth ground into powder. The book says people used pigments to decorate their bodies too.

<!-- STEP id="thought" -->
## What a Wall Can Tell Us

The first lesson drew a line: objects show what people did, and their thoughts are hard to reach. It is tempting to say that a painted wall crosses that line, but it does not.

The wall shows that people painted, what they chose to paint, and that they painted similar scenes again and again. It does not say why. A hunt on a wall could be a story, a lesson, a prayer or a memory of one good day. We have no way to check which.

**The wall shows what they painted. What it meant to them is a guess.**
