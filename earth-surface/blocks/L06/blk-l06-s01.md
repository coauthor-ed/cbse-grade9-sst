## Waves Build as Well as Break

You may think waves only destroy, yet many landforms on a shore are built by them.

On the Konkan coast, sandy bays lie between rocky cliffs. A **beach** is a landform of sand, pebbles or rocks along a shore, laid down by waves.

<!-- STEP -->
## Up and Back

A breaking wave rushes up the beach. This is the **swash**, and it carries sand with it. The water then runs back down as the **backwash**.

Press **Send a wave** a few times.

<!-- STEP -->
## Why Some Sand Stays

The swash is strong. Part of its water sinks into the sand, so the backwash is weaker and cannot take everything back. Some sand stays, and the beach can build up. Tides, currents and storms also move this sand, so a beach is always changing.

<!-- STEP -->
## What Beaches Give Us

Beaches bring tourists for swimming and rest, which helps the local economy. They provide fishing areas, and some communities collect sand and shells there.

A beach also acts as a natural barrier against strong waves and coastal erosion, which helps to protect settlements near the coast.
