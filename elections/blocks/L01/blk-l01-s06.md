# A Festival of Democracy

<!-- STEP -->
## Ink on a Finger

Indian elections are called **a festival of democracy**, and these photographs show why.

In the first two photographs, a young voter in Kerala holds up her voter identity card and her inked finger. Two friends in West Bengal take a photograph of the ink on their fingers. The ink shows that a person has voted.

<!-- STEP -->
## Cards and Queues

In the next two, women in a queue in Delhi show their photo voter cards. In a village schoolroom in West Bengal, a polling officer marks an elderly man's finger while others wait on benches.

<!-- STEP -->
## Every Voter Counts

In the last two, officials in Delhi check a voting machine before polling opens, and a voter in Chhattisgarh reaches the polling station in a wheelchair.

India had over 96.8 crore eligible voters in 2024. **Each one of them has one equal vote.**
