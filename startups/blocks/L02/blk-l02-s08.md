## Your turn

Discuss these questions from the book with your classmates. They do not have one right answer.

- What can you take away from the Jataka tale? Is it about spotting opportunities or working smart?
- What kind of traits do you think entrepreneurs need to have?
- What problem was Lijjat solving beyond just selling papads?
- What challenges could the Lijjat founders have faced at the start? How might they have overcome them?
- Can you think of a similar cooperative idea you could start in your community?
