## Sharing out the week's time

You and your friends have 10 hours a week to look after the four areas of ABC Bakery. Every area needs some of that time each week. Operations needs 4 hours, and finance, human resources and marketing need 2 hours each. These are made-up numbers.

<!-- STEP -->
## When the week is different

The game has four weeks. The first is a usual week. In the next three you find 2 extra hours, and a note tells you the news. Festival cake orders mean more baking: operations. A new helper needs training: human resources. A new bakery nearby means more customers to win: marketing and sales. Every other area keeps its usual hours.
