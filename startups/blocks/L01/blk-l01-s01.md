## A stall at the school festival

This chapter asks three big questions. How does entrepreneurship affect society and the economy? What are the main areas of a business? How do you make a business plan?

It begins at your school's annual festival. In the **Young Entrepreneurs Challenge**, you and your classmates set up a nimbu pani stall for the parents and children who visit.

<!-- STEP -->
## Choices before the first glass

Before you sell a single glass, you have to decide three things. How much will you spend on lemons and sugar? Where will you get the glasses? How much will you charge for each glass?

Tap each choice on the stall.

<!-- STEP -->
## The report at the end of the day

In the evening you write a report for your class teacher. It shows how much the stall spent, and whether it made a profit or a loss. You also count how many people liked the drink. A tasty drink is only one part of the report.
