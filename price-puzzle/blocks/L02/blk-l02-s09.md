# The Demand Side in Short

<!-- STEP -->
## Along the curve, or the whole curve

A change in the good's own price moves buyers along the demand curve. A change in anything else moves the whole curve: income, the price of a related good, taste, population, the season, or what buyers expect.

<!-- STEP -->
## Now the sellers

Demand is only one side of the market. Mangoes reach the stalls only if sellers bring them. In the next lesson, we look at how much sellers offer at each price. This is called supply.
