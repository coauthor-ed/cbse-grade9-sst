## The Thin Dark Layer

**Topsoil** is the dark top layer of a field, and crops get most of their food from it. You might think soil is lost only in great floods, but rain on bare ground takes a little every monsoon.

Drag **Monsoons** and watch the topsoil and the crop.

<!-- STEP -->
## Cover Changes the Loss

Now turn on **Plant cover** and drag **Monsoons** again. The rain and the slope are the same, but the soil stays much longer.

Leaves break the fall of the drops, and roots hold the soil together.

## Who Is Affected

Erosion changes the work people do.

- **Farmers** lose fertile topsoil to water and wind, so yields fall.
- **People near rivers and coasts** can lose land, houses and roads.
- **Construction and mining** become less safe.
- **Tourism and fishing** suffer when beaches and rivers are damaged.
