# Two Sets of Furrows

At **Kalibangan** in Rajasthan, excavators found a ploughed field from nearly 5,000 years ago. A furrow is the long, narrow line a plough cuts in the soil.

The furrows here run in two directions and cross at right angles. One set is spaced wide apart, and the other set is much closer together.

**Predict:** What do the crossing furrows most probably show?
