# Does air weigh anything?

<!-- STEP -->
## Air has weight

Air looks like nothing at all, yet it has weight. The pressure exerted by the weight of air on the Earth's surface is called **air pressure**, or atmospheric pressure.

<!-- STEP -->
## A column on your palm

Think of one square centimetre of your skin, about the size of a fingernail. The tall column of air above it presses down with about the weight of 1 kg.

Your palm is about 100 square centimetres, so about 100 kg of air presses on it. Why do you not feel it?
