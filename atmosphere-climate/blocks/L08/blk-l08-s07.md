# Four Words, Without the Gloss

<!-- STEP -->
## No pieces this time

Lesson two, lesson five and lesson six each gave you a board that took a word apart in front of you. This one runs the other way.

You get the plain meaning and you produce the name. There is no word bank and nothing to assemble - a miss gets one more go and then the answer appears.

**Four of them. If a word will not come, that is the useful part.**

<!-- STEP -->
## Where they came from

The four are the spine of the whole chapter, in the order the chapter needed them.

**Where the heat comes from.** The energy the Earth receives from the Sun, in the form in which it arrives.

**Why the wind turns twice a year.** The reversal of the wind over a continent that a difference in heating sets up, arriving with the summer and leaving with the winter.

**The gas that changes the rate.** The name for a gas that sits in the path of the heat leaving the surface and sends some of it back down.

**The total you are asked to reduce.** The name for everything one person or one organisation releases, added up.

**None of these four was waiting to be guessed. All of them had to be learned, and so did every other hard name in this chapter.**
