## Before we move on: ideas, plans and areas

- Entrepreneurship is turning ideas into action: finding opportunities, taking risks and creating value for society.
- A business plan is a roadmap. It helps entrepreneurs set goals, organise resources and make careful decisions.
- Every business has four functional areas that work together: management and operations, finance and accounting, human resources, and marketing and sales.

## Before we move on: money, failure and startups

- Knowing revenue, expenses, profit and cash flow helps a business see how it is doing and stay financially sustainable: here this means able to keep paying its costs over time.
- Challenges, risks and even failures are part of an entrepreneur's journey. They are chances to learn and improve.
- Startups and entrepreneurs bring new ideas, create jobs, solve real problems and help towards Viksit Bharat 2047.
