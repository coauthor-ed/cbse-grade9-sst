## Claim the Dune Before the Wind Draws It

The game gives you the **same two dials** from the dune slide: **`WIND`**, which sets the direction the sand arrives from, and **`SAND`**, which sets how much sand there is to build with.

Then it asks you something before it will show you anything.

**Claim the dune this pair will build** - **barchan**, **transverse**, **star** or **parabolic**. Only after you commit does the wind run, and the run draws the shape those two settings actually produce.

Later rounds add a fixed obstacle in the sand supply - something that stops the sand arriving at one end - and you claim again.

**A claim only scores if the shape that appears is the shape your two dials were going to make.** The game will tell you the rule when you are wrong, not the answer. There is no clock and nothing counts down - take as long as you like, and run as many rounds as you want.
