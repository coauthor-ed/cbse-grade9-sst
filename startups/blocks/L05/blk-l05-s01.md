## March at ABC Bakery

In March 2026, ABC Bakery sold bread, buns, pastries, cookies and cakes, and charged for deliveries. Customers paid **₹2,95,000** in all. The bakery also paid for flour and sugar, rent, salaries, electricity and more, and it has to pay tax to the government.

**Predict:** about how much of the ₹2,95,000 does the bakery keep as profit after tax, once all of this is paid?
