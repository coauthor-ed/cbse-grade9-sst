# Challenge: Set the Weather

<!-- STEP -->
## Places with a hidden temperature, and a forecast you have to keep

A grid of towns is laid out on a map. Over each one is a forecast that has been promised and not yet delivered: **clear skies** or **cloud and rain**.

Under each town is a temperature you cannot see. Reading it is the job.

Your job is to put the weather back. Place a **high** or a **low** over the towns and route the wind so that every town ends up under the sky it was promised.

<!-- STEP -->
## It scores the cells, not the towns

Here is the part that catches people, so read it before you start.

The game counts the **cells you place and whether each one is the right kind of cell**. It does not count satisfied towns.

A town only gets clear or wet sky when a **correctly-signed cell is genuinely sitting on top of it** - not near it, not next to it, not implied by it.

**You cannot win by reading the answer key backwards.** A map of the forecast is not a solution, because the forecast is an output. The temperature under each town is an input, and the cells are what you build from it.

No timer, no moves to protect, unlimited retries. Take as long as you like.

**A learner who only knows that rain means low pressure cannot get through this. You have to know why the low goes there.**
