# What Each Layer Does for Us

<!-- STEP -->
- **Troposphere:** gives us air to breathe, and our weather.
- **Stratosphere:** filters ultraviolet radiation, and gives aeroplanes clear air.
- **Mesosphere:** burns up most meteors.
- **Thermosphere:** absorbs X-rays and reflects radio waves.
- **Exosphere:** fades into space.
