## A Bicycle in Six Months

Meera gets **₹1,500** every month for her bus fare, stationery, lunch and snacks. She wants a bicycle that costs **₹3,000**, and she gives herself six months to save for it.

Her plan: spend on what she needs during the month, and save whatever is left at the end of it.

**Predict:** Will Meera have ₹3,000 at the end of the sixth month?
