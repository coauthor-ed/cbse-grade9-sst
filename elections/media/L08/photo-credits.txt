- Why this one: the source's `Fig. 7.5` is the framed pledge certificate with a
  cartoon vignette. No openly-licensed photograph or scan of that certificate
  graphic exists on Wikimedia Commons (searched `National Voters Day pledge
  India`, `voters pledge certificate India`, `National Voters' Day`, and the
  NVD category). This frame shows the pledge being administered at a national
  function on the date itself, on a stage whose front panel reads
  `NATIONAL VOTERS' DAY / January 25, 2012 / New Delhi`, and it carries the
  Commission's own logo - so the date the slide is about is legible in the
  image rather than only asserted beside it.
- Note on the people in the frame: this is a photograph of a real public
  function and real identifiable people, which is why it is an `image` and not
  a scene. The block's `alt` names no individual, and the slide prose names
  none either - the chapter names neither the President nor the event, so no
  name appears anywhere in the lesson.
- Note on the pledge's wording: the pledge is a public official text and the
  slide **paraphrases** it rather than transcribing it
  (`AGENTS.md` rule 4). The certificate's own official wording is not
  reproduced in prose, in the caption, or in `alt`.
