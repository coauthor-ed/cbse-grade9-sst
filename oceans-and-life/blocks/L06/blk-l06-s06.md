## A buoy on the open sea

Under an initiative of the Indian Tsunami Early Warning Centre (ITEWC), the Government of India set up a real-time network of tsunami detection buoys. They are designed to detect, measure and monitor tsunamis. This is our drawing of how such a buoy works, not a picture of a real one.

<!-- STEP -->
## Listening from the sea floor

A pressure recorder sits on the sea floor, far below the buoy. When a tsunami passes, more water presses on it for a while. The recorder notices this and sends a signal up to the buoy.

<!-- STEP -->
## Up to the sky, back to the centre

The buoy passes the reading to a satellite, and the satellite passes it to the warning centre. No instrument warns anybody by itself, because people at the centre read the reports and send the alert.
