## Route the Water Through the Plateau

The game hands you a cutaway of a limestone plateau with numbered sites on it - a **sinkhole** in the flat top, a **cave chamber** underground, a **fissure** running down through the rock, and the **cave mouth** where the water comes back out at the surface.

Your job is to put the water in at the sinkhole and draw its route through the sites, in the order the water actually travels.

Every route you draw is checked against the rock. A route that sends the water into a dead end loses the round, and the board tells you which stretch of rock cannot carry it.

One order is never available, however much you want it: the passages have to exist **before** the water gets there. Dissolving the rock open after the water has already passed is not allowed.

**Win the round only when the water goes in at the sinkhole, crosses the rock, and surfaces at the cave mouth.** There is no clock and nothing counts down - take as long as you like, and run as many rounds as you want.
