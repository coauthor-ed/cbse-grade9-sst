# Your Turn: Prices That Keep Changing

Work on these with a partner or in class.

1. Think of another real-life example, not hotels, where prices change often. Why do they keep changing?
2. Your textbook's Table 9.3 gives mango numbers at ₹40, ₹100 and ₹150. In your notebook, plot its demand curve and supply curve. Mark where there is excess demand and where there is excess supply. How could the market reach equilibrium from each of these prices?
3. Should we think only about short-term gains, or also about long-term sustainability? How could this change the market equilibrium?
