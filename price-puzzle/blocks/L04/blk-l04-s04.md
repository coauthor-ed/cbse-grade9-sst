# More Sellers, Fewer Sellers

<!-- STEP -->
## More sellers

At ₹100 per kg, our three sellers offer 12 kg, and buyers want 12 kg. Now three new sellers open stalls. As a made-up example, they offer 2 kg, 4 kg and 6 kg at ₹100. Market supply at ₹100 grows to 24 kg, but buyers still want only 12 kg.

<!-- STEP -->
## The price falls

Now there are more mangoes for sale than buyers want, and some would be left unsold. To sell them, sellers lower the price.

**More sellers: supply rises, and the price tends to fall.**

<!-- STEP -->
## Fewer sellers

Now go back to the three sellers, and let Seller C close her stall for the season. At ₹100, only Seller A and Seller B are left: 2 + 4 = 6 kg. Buyers still want 12 kg, so some go home without mangoes, and the price tends to rise.
