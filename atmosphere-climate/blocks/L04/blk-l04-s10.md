# From a coast to a country

<!-- STEP -->
## One coast, one day

You have seen land and sea take turns at being warmer. The air moved from the cooler side to the warmer side, and it turned around within a day.

<!-- STEP -->
## One country, one year

Now make the land all of India and the sea the Indian Ocean, and let summer and winter do the heating and cooling.

Will the same thing happen at the size of a country?
