## Why whole wheat bread

**Selling below cost loses money on every cake.** The big rival can keep low prices longer than a small bakery can.

**Other choices are open.** Some customers prefer healthier goods such as whole wheat bread. If the rival does not sell them, ABC Bakery can, and keep a fair price.

**The plan is changed, not thrown away.** The friends go back to the questions about needs and customers, and answer them again.
