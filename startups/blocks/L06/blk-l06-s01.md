## India's traditional business communities

Entrepreneurship in India has deep roots in traditional communities. In these communities, skills, occupations and knowledge about the work were passed down through generations. Long before modern startups, they practised self-employment, innovation and trade, and they formed the backbone of India's local economy.

## The Nattukottai Chettiars

The **Chettiar** (Nattukottai Chettiar) community from Tamil Nadu is a well-known traditional business community. In the past, Chettiars were traders, bankers and moneylenders. They worked across India, and also in Sri Lanka, Burma (now Myanmar), Malaysia and other parts of Southeast Asia.

<!-- STEP -->
## Salt, rice, textiles and money

The Chettiars built their wealth through trade in salt, rice and textiles, and through finance: lending money to others. They often did this without formal banks.
