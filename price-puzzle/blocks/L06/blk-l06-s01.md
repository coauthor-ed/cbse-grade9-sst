# The Government and the Market

<!-- STEP -->
## A market-based economy

Today, India is the fourth-largest economy in the world. It is a market-based, regulated economy: most prices depend on demand and supply, and the government makes rules for the market.

<!-- STEP -->
## Is the market always fair?

A market gives goods to those who are willing and able to pay. Suppose a medicine becomes very costly. Some families cannot buy it at all. For essential goods like medicines, fairness matters, most of all for poor and low-income families.

<!-- STEP -->
## What the government does

So the government acts in the market in a few ways. It stops unfair practices, for example by fixing a highest or a lowest price. It provides goods that everyone needs, like roads and parks. **But too much government action can also harm the market.**
