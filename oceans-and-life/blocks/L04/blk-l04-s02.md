## A stream of warm water

The sea off Norway stays open because warm water reaches it. The red arrow is the **Gulf Stream**, which starts in the warm Gulf of Mexico and flows towards Norway. Sea ice does not form easily there, even at 60 to 70 degrees north.

<!-- STEP -->
## A cold stream on the other side

The blue arrow is the **Labrador Current**, which flows along the east coast of Canada. It carries freezing water, so sea ice and icebergs form there even at a lower latitude. Latitude alone does not decide how cold a sea is.

## Streams in the sea

Both are **ocean currents**: continuous streams of water that flow across the ocean surface in definite directions. Water flowing from a warmer region makes a **warm current**, and water flowing from a colder region makes a **cold current**. Maps usually show warm currents in red and cold currents in blue.
