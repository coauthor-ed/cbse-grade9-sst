## Other Rivers, Other Cities

This was not the only river region to grow cities. In the same Bronze Age, three other civilisations grew up on river plains: Mesopotamia, Egypt and China.

These rivers grew their own cities. One of them, Mesopotamia, traded with the Harappans.

We start with Mesopotamia, because the book says the earliest city-based civilisation appeared there, and its plain had very little to build with.
