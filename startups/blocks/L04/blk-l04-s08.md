## Your turn: talk to business people

Interview two people near you who run a business, such as a tailor, a shopkeeper or a factory owner. Ask them about their journey of starting it:

- Why did you start the business?
- What challenges did you face?
- What risks did you take?
- What did you learn?

## Your turn: an entrepreneur's tasks

Write a short report on the tasks an entrepreneur performs. Use the five tasks: identifying a problem and solving it in a new way, taking risks with money and time, making key decisions, bringing together the factors of production, and adding to the welfare of society.

## Your turn: your own business plan

Think of your own business idea and write a business plan for it, using the template in the book. Now that you know the four functional areas, say how each one will work in your business: the operations, the money, the people, and how customers will hear about you.
