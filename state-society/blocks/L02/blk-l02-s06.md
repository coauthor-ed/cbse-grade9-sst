# What Was Changing on the Ground

## Two maps of settlements

Switch between the two periods. In the later one, more settlements are concentrated in the **Ganga region**.

<!-- STEP -->
## Vedic culture moves east

Early Vedic culture was most prominent in the north-west, in the Indus and Sarasvatī valleys. In the later Vedic period, the **Ganga plains** became the main centre.

<!-- STEP -->
## Iron, grain and pottery

Finds from the Ganga region show iron tools, a wider variety of grain and **Painted Grey Ware** (PGW) pottery.

In the second half of the first millennium BCE, the middle Ganga region adds a refined pottery called **NBPW**, Northern Black Polished Ware, with a smooth, glossy black surface.

<!-- STEP -->
## What the texts add

Texts of the period speak of expanding farming, more grain, more kinds of crop (paddy above all) and iron tools.

**Can you identify the crucial factors behind this change?** Weigh these: which came first, iron tools or more grain? What would more grain make possible? Why might people choose the Ganga plains?
