# Two Party Records

<!-- STEP -->
## Party P and Party Q

Here are the records of two made-up parties after one general election.

- **Party P** polled 5.8% of the country's valid votes, all in one State, which is 40% of that State's votes, and won 3 Lok Sabha seats.
- **Party Q** polled 2% of the country's valid votes and won 12 Lok Sabha seats, from four States.

<!-- STEP -->
## Which Looks Bigger?

You might say Party P, as it has almost three times the votes of Party Q. That is a fair first guess, because votes are the first thing most of us look at.

The ECI looks at something more than the size of the vote.
