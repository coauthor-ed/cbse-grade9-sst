# The breeze at the coast

<!-- STEP -->
## Land warms faster than water

Sunlight stays near the surface of land, so a thin layer gets hot quickly and cools quickly. In water the heat is mixed deep down, so the sea changes slowly.

<!-- STEP -->
## Day: the sea breeze

By afternoon the land is warmer than the sea. Air over the land rises, which forms low pressure there. **The wind blows from the sea to the land, and it is called a sea breeze.**

<!-- STEP -->
## Night: the land breeze

At night the land cools faster, so the water is now the warmer side. **The wind blows from the land to the sea, and it is called a land breeze.** The difference is small, so this wind is slow.

<!-- STEP -->
## Mild coasts

These two local winds keep the weather at the coast moderate. The sun heats land and sea unequally, and that difference sets the air moving.
