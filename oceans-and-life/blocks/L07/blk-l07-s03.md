## Far fewer than before

In the fifth year the village catches far fewer than 30,000 fish. In this model it catches none at all, because the sea does not give without limit.

Start with the old catch. The stock grows from 100,000 to 130,000, the village takes 30,000, and 100,000 are left. So the line stays flat, year after year.

<!-- STEP -->
## What doubling does

With a catch of 60,000, the stock grows to 130,000 and only 70,000 are left. The next year it grows to 91,000, and 31,000 are left.

In the third year it grows only to 40,300, and the boats take all of it. No fish are left to breed, so the fourth and fifth years bring nothing.

<!-- STEP -->
## What the model shows

Real fish do not grow by exactly 30% a year, and these numbers are made up. The rule is real all the same: a stock that is taken faster than it breeds back runs down.

That is why marine resources must be used with care, without disturbing the living world they come from.
