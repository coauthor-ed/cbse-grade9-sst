# Four Questions, Four Answers

<!-- STEP -->
## What the air is

**What is the air made of?** Mainly nitrogen and oxygen, with small amounts of other gases, water vapour and dust.

**How do its layers affect Earth?** They hold our weather, take in ultraviolet, burn up meteors and reflect radio waves.

<!-- STEP -->
## What moves it, and what we can do

**How does the monsoon work?** Land and sea heat unequally, so the winds reverse with the seasons.

**How can we reduce our carbon footprint?** Save energy, use renewable sources, plant trees and live in eco-friendly ways.
