## Four Questions, One Trench

A team opens a trench at a site with no writing of any kind: no tablets, no inscriptions, no marked pottery. They find stone tools, animal bones, a hearth full of ash, post holes (the marks left by wooden posts) and the base of a wall.

**Predict:** Which of these can they work out from that evidence alone: what these people ate, what they believed, who their ruler was, how they built?
