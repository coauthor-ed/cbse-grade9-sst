## A long tradition of books

Over several millennia, India produced a vast and diverse body of literature on subjects such as philosophy, grammar, medicine, astronomy, mathematics, culinary practices, agriculture and architecture.

Despite the rise and fall of kingdoms, many of these knowledge traditions persisted. Tap a shelf to open it.

<!-- STEP -->
## Grammar, smṛiti and medicine

- **Sanskrit grammar:** foundational texts, including Pāṇini's *Aṣhṭādhyāyī*.
- **Smṛiti literature:** texts on dharma, law and ethics. The word *smṛiti* means what is remembered.
- **Medicine:** classical works such as the *Charakasamhitā*.

<!-- STEP -->
## Poetry and Tamil literature

- **Sanskrit poetry and kāvya:** Kālidāsa's *Raghuvaṃśha* and *Kumārasambhava*.
- **Tamil literature:** Tiruvalluvar's *Tirukkural*, two epics and the vast body of Sangam texts.

Sangam texts cover many themes, including love (**akam**) and war or public life (**puram**), so they also tell us about political organisation and economic life.
