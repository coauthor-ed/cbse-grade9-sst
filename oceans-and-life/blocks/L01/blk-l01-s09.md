## The floor stays. The water does not.

Every part of the floor you have just walked across has been sitting where it is for a very long time. The shelf, the slope, the plain, the ridge - none of them has moved. That is the still part of the ocean.

The water above it is the opposite. Drop a coconut off the cliff at Kovalam in Keralam and it will never lie still on the surface. The sea surface is always moving.

<!-- STEP -->
## So what moves the sea?

There is a switch in the picture. Throw it one way and the surface lies flat and smooth. Throw it the other and the surface roughens, and the coconut on it bobs up and down and is never at rest.

The floor is quiet. The water on top of it never is.
