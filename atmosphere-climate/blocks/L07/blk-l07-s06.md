# Challenge: Diagnose the Station

<!-- STEP -->
## A station with its name taken away

The game hands you a station described in words, with **its name hidden**, and its two climograph outlines beside it - the temperature line and the twelve rainfall bars.

Three things have to be right before the round closes:

- **Put it on the map.** Tap the right position in the subcontinent.
- **Set its monsoon window.** Drag the bar so it runs from the date the front arrives to the date it withdraws.
- **Name the cause.** Altitude, distance from the sea, the windward side of a hill, or the interior.

<!-- STEP -->
## What actually gets scored

The three answers have to be **consistent with each other**, not individually reasonable.

Put a station at 3506 m and then name the interior as its cause, and the rainfall bars will not agree with the placement - the game will say so. Name the windward side of a hill for a station the scene has placed in the far interior, and the same thing happens.

A learner who can recognise a shape but cannot say **what makes it** does not close the round. That is deliberate.

<!-- STEP -->
## Two things that will not help you

**Matching a name to a shape is not enough on its own.** Half the answer scores nothing, and the other half is checked against the placement.

**The annual total is not the diagnosis either.** A station can carry a large yearly figure and still be the wrong shape entirely - the timing is what the bars and the line are showing.

<!-- STEP -->
## Take your time

No timer on this one, no moves to protect, unlimited retries, and the same stations each round. **There is no version of this that rewards guessing quickly.**
