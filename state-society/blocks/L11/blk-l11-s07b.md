## The Cave's Yearly Needs

Now you can try the arithmetic. You have 3000 kāhāpaṇas to place with two weavers' guilds, one paying 12 for each hundred a year and the other paying 9.

Each round sets the interest the cave needs. Use **+** and **-** to split the money until the year's interest matches.

The targets in this game are for practice, and only the first is from the inscription. There is no clock, and a wrong try costs nothing.
