# Chennai's Rain Comes Late

<!-- STEP -->
## The tall bars are at the end

Chennai's tall bars come after the rest of India's: **30.6 cm in October and 35.0 cm in November**. Those two months give just over half its year's rain.

<!-- STEP -->
## A different wind

From June to September, Chennai lies on the side of India away from the south-west wind. In October and November, north-east winds cross the Bay of Bengal and reach it wet.

Thiruvananthapuram gets this later rain too, 27.3 cm in October. These two are the stations most influenced by the retreating monsoon.

<!-- STEP -->
## What a total hides

Chennai's temperature changes by 8.5 °C across the year, far less than Delhi's 18.9 °C, because the sea is close.

**A yearly total of 128.6 cm tells you how much rain falls, not when.**
