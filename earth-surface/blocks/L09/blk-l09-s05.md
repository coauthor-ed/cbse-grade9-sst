## A Stream Disappears

On limestone land a stream can reach a sinkhole and simply vanish from the surface. Where does the water go? Press **Pour dye** to follow it.

<!-- STEP -->
## A River With a Roof

The dyed water runs through passages inside the hill. A river that flows through a cave system is an **underground river**.

Scientists use harmless dyes in this way to find where such water travels.

<!-- STEP -->
## Back Into Daylight

The water comes out at a cave mouth lower down the hill, often far from where it went in. The rain that fell on the top has crossed the hill inside the rock.
