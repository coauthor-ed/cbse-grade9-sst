# The One That Grew

<!-- STEP -->
## Magadha

Among the sixteen, **Magadha**, in present-day Bihar, gradually became the most powerful.

Why this one, out of sixteen?

<!-- STEP -->
## Three reasons

Historians give three reasons. Tap each one to see it on the map.

- its **strategic location**
- its **fertile plains**
- its **strong rulers**

<!-- STEP -->
## From kingdom to empire

These three let Magadha expand its control over neighbouring regions. This expansion led in time to the **Mauryan Empire**, one of the largest and most powerful empires in early Indian history.
