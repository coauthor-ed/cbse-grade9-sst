# What Holds a Large Group Together?

<!-- STEP -->
## A class of forty

Your class of 40 has a timetable and class rules, and nobody in the class voted on them. Most people follow them anyway, because the class could not run without them.

Whenever a large group of people lives together, rules and shared habits grow up over time.

<!-- STEP -->
## What a society is

A **society** is a group of people who share a territory, a culture and a sense of belonging. It is made of families and households, and of customs such as marriage.

**A society is ruled mostly by custom, not by formal law.**

<!-- STEP -->
## What a state is

A **state** is an organised political system based on rules and laws. It sets out the rights and duties of rulers and subjects, and it has institutions to enforce law and order.

States developed slowly, as societies became larger and more complex.

<!-- STEP -->
## A society comes first

A society can exist with no state. That is where this story begins: with people who had customs and leaders, long before there were kingdoms and offices.

We will now follow how one grew into the other, up to 1000 CE.
