## Three Questions, Three Answers

Now you can answer all three questions from the start of this lesson.

- Can Gurmat stay on as CR with no fresh election? **No.**
- Can a government continue past its term without the people's mandate? **No.**
- Can we exercise our choice if only one party is contesting? **No.**

In each case, the people need a real vote, and that vote must come again and again.
