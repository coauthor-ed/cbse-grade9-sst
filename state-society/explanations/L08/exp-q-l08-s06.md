# What Each Source Shows

The **Mandsaur Stone Inscription** (473 CE) shows a guild of silk weavers that migrated and was also proficient in archery and astrology.

The **Karitalai copper-plate inscriptions** (late 5th century CE) record brāhmaṇas as land managers.

The **Pattinappalai** describes many occupational groups interacting actively.

These sources support the idea of considerable social mobility. They are records of particular people and groups, so they do not show that everyone could move.
