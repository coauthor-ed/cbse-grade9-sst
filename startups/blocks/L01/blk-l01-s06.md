## Seeing opportunities

Entrepreneurs are curious people who see opportunities where others see only problems. One leads a business that makes drones to watch traffic. Another makes eco-friendly jute bags that can replace plastic bags. Both work hard to create something of value for society.

## Five tasks of an entrepreneur

An entrepreneur does five tasks. She identifies a problem and decides to solve it in a new way. She takes risks by putting in money and time. She makes the key decisions about how the business runs. She brings together the **factors of production**: the things a business needs, such as land, materials, machines and workers. And she adds to the welfare (well-being) of society.

## Five tasks in one jute bag business

Imagine a jute bag maker. The details here are made up. She sees plastic bags thrown on the streets: a problem. She puts her savings into the business: a risk. She decides the size and the price of the bags: decisions. She brings together jute cloth, sewing machines and tailors: the factors of production. Fewer plastic bags end up as litter: welfare.
