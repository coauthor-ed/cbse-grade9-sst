## The reinvest game

You run a trading firm, like a Chettiar firm, for three years; these are made-up numbers. The firm starts with ₹1,00,000 of trade money, and each year it earns ₹20 of profit for every ₹100. Each year you choose: put the profit back into trade, give it to temples, education and welfare, or split it half and half.

<!-- STEP -->
## The goal

At the end of the third year, the firm must have at least ₹1,30,000 of trade money. It must also have given at least ₹35,000 to temples, education and welfare. Money you put back into trade earns profit in the years after.
