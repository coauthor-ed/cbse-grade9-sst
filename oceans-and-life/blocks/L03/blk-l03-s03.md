## Full moon and new moon, both

The sea rises highest on full moon days and on new moon days. Brightness cannot be the reason, because the new moon does not shine in our sky at all.

On both days the Sun, the Earth and the Moon lie in a straight line, so the two pulls combine. This gives a **spring tide**: high tides of unusual height, and low tides that are unusually low.

<!-- STEP -->
## Half moon: the neap tide

At the first and third quarter the Moon stands at a right angle. The pulls of the Sun and the Moon now act in different directions, so the difference between high tide and low tide becomes smaller. This is a **neap tide**.

<!-- STEP -->
## Spring is not a season

The word "spring" here means that the water springs up. Spring tides come about every two weeks, all through the year, and neap tides come between them.
