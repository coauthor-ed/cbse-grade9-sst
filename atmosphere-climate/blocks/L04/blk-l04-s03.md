## The packet swells

The packet swells up tight. The air sealed inside still pushes outwards as before, but the air outside now pushes in less.

## Air pressure is the weight above you

At Leh, much of the atmosphere lies below you, so less air presses from above. **Air pressure is highest at sea level and falls rapidly as we go higher.**

At Leh it is about two thirds of the pressure at sea level. Ten kilometres up, where long-distance jets fly, it is about a quarter.
