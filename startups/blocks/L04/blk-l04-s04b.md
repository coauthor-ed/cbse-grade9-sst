## Marketing and sales

The **marketing and sales** team brings in customers and persuades them to buy. For a bakery this begins with a catchy name, promoting the bakery on social media, and offering discounts. You planned some of this as promotion. This area does that work every week.

## Five simple ways

The book lists some simple ways. Make special or seasonal items, and desserts made to order, such as a mango cake. Offer discounts, deals or special offers. Make it easy to order online and get a delivery. Share photos and videos on social media. Use packaging that can be recycled, and tell customers about it.
