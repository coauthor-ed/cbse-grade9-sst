## Where Water Is Scarce

Think of the Thar Desert in Rajasthan on a hot afternoon. Rain is rare here, so few plants grow and few roots hold the ground together.

That leaves the surface open to the wind. When strong winds pick up loose sand and soil and carry them away, we call it **wind erosion**.

<!-- STEP -->
## What the Wind Lifts

Try **Calm**, **Breeze** and **Strong** on the figure. A breeze lifts only the finest grains.

- **Dust** is so light that it rises high and can travel very far.
- **Sand** is heavier, so it hops along the ground. Most grains stay within about a metre of the surface.
- **Gravel** is too heavy for the wind to lift, so it stays where it is.

## What the Wind Leaves

After many storms the fine grains have gone from this patch, and the stones are left lying on the surface.

**Wind sorts the ground by size: it carries the small grains away and leaves the big ones behind.**

All of this is loose material. Next we ask about rock that is not loose at all.
