## A model answer

**1. Food and livelihood.** Fish are a major source of protein, and fishing and aquaculture support important industries and livelihoods for millions of people.

**2. Energy.** Offshore reserves give oil and natural gas, and tides and waves offer renewable blue energy.

**3. Trade and tourism.** About 90% of global trade by volume is carried by sea, so a country's harbours are part of how it earns its living. Coastal tourism is a multi-billion dollar industry.

**4. Culture.** The sacred śhaṅkha (conch) and kapardikā (cowrie shells), once used as currency, are regarded as divine treasures from the mythological Samudra Manthana.

**5. The condition.** Marine resources must be used responsibly, without disturbing marine ecosystems. A living resource taken faster than it breeds back runs down. This is the idea of the Blue Economy: use ocean resources for growth, livelihoods and jobs, while preserving the health of the marine ecosystem.
