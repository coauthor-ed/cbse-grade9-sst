# Your Turn: Rules and Public Goods

Work on these with a partner or in class.

1. Have you seen or heard of the government fixing a price or a wage, for example bus fares, medicines or the minimum wage? Share one example and say why you think it was done.
2. The government puts higher taxes on tobacco and alcohol to help people make healthier choices. Can you find other goods whose prices the government controls? Why does it control them?
3. List two goods or services near you that the government provides, for example roads, streetlights, parks or the police. Choose one. Who benefits from it? Why would it be hard for a private company to provide it? What problems would people face if the government stopped providing it?
