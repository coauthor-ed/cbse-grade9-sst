# Seven Digital Services

<!-- STEP -->
## Three Groups of Users

The ECI makes extensive use of technology. Its digital services are grouped by who uses them: candidates, election officials and voters.

Tap a group in the picture to see its services.

<!-- STEP -->
## For Candidates

**Suvidha** is an app for contesting candidates and political parties. They can get nomination forms and updates, apply for permissions for meetings, rallies, vehicles and loudspeakers, and check the status of each request.

<!-- STEP -->
## For Election Officials

**ERONET** handles form-processing and databases, and it maintains the E-Rolls. Each officer's access depends on their role.

**Sugam** manages vehicles taken from other departments for election work. It holds vehicle numbers, driver contacts and fuel allotment.

<!-- STEP -->
## For Voters

- **ETPBS**: service voters receive and cast postal ballots electronically
- **Voter Helpline App**: registration, transposition (moving your entry when you change address), verification, election news and updates
- **Saksham**: helps PwDs register and find their polling station
- **cVIGIL**: report a Model Code of Conduct violation within minutes; flying squads use auto location to reach the spot
