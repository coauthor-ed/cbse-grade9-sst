# A Script That Was Lost

Cuneiform went out of use by the end of the first millennium BCE, but scholars can read it again today. The Harappan script has still not been read.

## Egypt's Writing Was Lost Too

Egypt, the next civilisation in this chapter, wrote on temple walls, in tombs and on scrolls. Egyptians read this writing for thousands of years. Then, around the 400s CE, the last readers died, and nobody could read it for about 1,400 years.
