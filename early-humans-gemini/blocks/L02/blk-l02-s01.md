# Look Along the Row

Four skulls sit in one drawer, in a line. They are our ancestors, our close relatives and ourselves.

<!-- STEP id="habilis" -->
## The First Skull

This one has the smallest braincase of the four, the braincase being the part of the skull that holds the brain. The jaw juts forward, and a heavy shelf of bone hangs over the eyes.

<!-- STEP id="erectus" -->
## The Second Skull

The braincase is longer and larger. The bone over the eyes is still a thick shelf, but the face below it has begun to shorten.

<!-- STEP id="neanderthal" -->
## The Third Skull

This skull held a big brain, and it bulges out at the back. A curved ridge of bone sweeps over wide-set eyes.

<!-- STEP id="sapiens" -->
## The Fourth Skull

This one has a high, upright forehead and a rounded braincase. The face is flat, the brow is small, and the chin sticks out a little.

<!-- STEP id="trend" -->
## Oldest to Newest

Put them side by side, oldest to newest. From the first skull to the fourth, the face becomes straighter and the forehead higher.

The Neanderthal is a side branch, not a step on the way to us.
