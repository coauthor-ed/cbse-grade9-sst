# Reading a Climate Graph

<!-- STEP -->
## Two scales on one graph

A graph shows a year at a glance. The **line** gives temperature, read on the left scale in °C. The **bars** give rainfall, read on the right scale in cm. Both share the months along the bottom.

<!-- STEP -->
## What Delhi's graph shows

Delhi warms from 14.4 °C in January to 33.3 °C in May, and the bars stay tiny. Then July's bar jumps to 19.3 cm.

Rain and cloud cool the air, so July is cooler than May, at 30.0 °C.

<!-- STEP -->
## Read another station

Pick a station and see what shape its year makes. Try Leh, Mumbai and Chennai.

Two printed numbers look odd. Delhi's June is 33.3 °C in the table but about 30 °C on the book's graph. Jodhpur's September, 20.1 °C, may be a misprint.
