# Six Parties, Six Symbols

Question 7 at the end of your textbook chapter asks you to match six political parties with their symbols. It lists them in this order:

- Aam Aadmi Party (AAP)
- Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP)
- Bahujan Samaj Party (BSP)
- Communist Party of India (Marxist) [CPI (M)]
- Indian National Congress (INC)
- National People's Party (NPP)

Do this match with your textbook open, as this course draws no party's symbol.

Then visit the ECI website and find out how many State parties and Registered Unrecognised Political Parties there are.
