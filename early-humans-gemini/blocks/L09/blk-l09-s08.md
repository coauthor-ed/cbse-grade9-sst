# Does Reading a Script Change What We Know?

<!-- STEP id="unread" -->
## What an Unread Script Leaves Us

When a script is unread, like the Sindhu lipi, historians have only objects. Objects can show technology, trade and how a city was laid out. They do not tell us what people thought or why they did things.

<!-- STEP id="read" -->
## What a Read Script Adds

When a script is read, like cuneiform, it gives us names, dates, disputes and beliefs. Mesopotamian tablets even keep school exercises and complaints, which no pot could ever tell us.

<!-- STEP id="partial" -->
## Still Not the Whole Story

Most people could not write, so we mostly hear from kings, priests and scribes.

**A written record is still partial, because someone chose what was worth writing down.**
