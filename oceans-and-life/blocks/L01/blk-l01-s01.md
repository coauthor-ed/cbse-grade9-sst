## The sea we cannot see

Oceans cover most of our planet, and their water hides a whole landscape. In Indian culture the ocean is called **Samudra**, and it is treated as powerful and sacred in stories and rituals.

The ocean is also never still. It feeds people, it carries ships, and it can be dangerous.
