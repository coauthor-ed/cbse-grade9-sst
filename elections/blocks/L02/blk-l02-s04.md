# Votes and Seats

## Haritbhumi

Under FPTP, a party's share of seats can differ from its share of votes. On our made-up map, Party A has 40 per cent of the votes and **60 per cent of the seats**, because 15 of 25 is 60 per cent.

Party B has 35 per cent of votes and 28 per cent of seats. Party C has 25 and 12 per cent.

## Swarnalok

Under PR, a party's share of seats matches its share of votes. Tap Swarnalok to see each pair of bars come out equal: 40 and 40, 35 and 35, 15 and 15, 10 and 10.

## Each Rule Has Its Own Result

In Haritbhumi, Party A holds more than half of the seats, and it forms the government. In Swarnalok, Party M holds 40 of 100 seats. That is less than half, so power is shared.

Under PR, seats match votes closely. Under FPTP, one party can more easily hold most of the seats.

The book does not call one system better. **Each one turns the same kind of vote into a different kind of result.**
