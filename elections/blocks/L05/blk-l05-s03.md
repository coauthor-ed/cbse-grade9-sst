# Four Ways to Vote

<!-- STEP -->
## More Than One Way

Most people vote at the polling station, but some voters cannot get there. Others need help when they arrive. So there are four ways to vote:

- at the polling station
- from home
- by ETPBS, an electronic postal ballot
- with a companion

<!-- STEP -->
## From Home

Voting from home is for senior citizens above 85 years of age and for Persons with Disabilities with a benchmark disability of 40%. It was extended across India for the first time in the 2024 General Elections.

A polling team brings a ballot paper and a screen. The voter marks the paper in private, and it goes into a sealed cover.

<!-- STEP -->
## By ETPBS

**ETPBS** stands for Electronically Transmitted Postal Ballot System. It is for **service voters**, such as members of the armed forces who are posted far from their constituency.

They receive a postal ballot electronically and cast it from their preferred location, outside their constituency.

<!-- STEP -->
## With a Companion

A voter who cannot see, or who cannot use the machine because of a disability, may take a companion into the voting compartment. Tap a voter to see which ways are open to them.

The rules are exact. A voter aged 74 cannot vote from home, however hard the walk is.

<!-- STEP -->
## Two Cases in Custody

A person in prison, whether sentenced or awaiting trial, cannot vote. A person in **preventive detention** is held to stop a possible offence, and has not been sentenced. This person can vote by postal ballot.
