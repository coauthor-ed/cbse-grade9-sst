# Your Turn: Your Own Demand Schedule

Try these on paper. There are no right or wrong answers.

1. Make your own demand schedule for notebooks. Write how many notebooks you would buy in a month if one notebook cost ₹20, ₹50, ₹80 or ₹100. At which price would you buy the most? At which price would you stop buying? Why?
2. Ask three friends the same question, and add the four answers at each price. You have made a small market demand schedule. Draw it as a graph.
3. Ask your family: did they ever wait to buy something because they expected its price to fall, or buy it early because they expected its price to rise?
