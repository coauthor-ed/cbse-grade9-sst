# A year that depends on the wind

## A good monsoon

Most of India's agriculture depends on monsoon rainfall, because farmers rely on rain for sowing and growing crops. A good monsoon gives enough food and fills rivers, reservoirs and wells.

<!-- STEP -->
## Too much, too little

Excessive rainfall can cause floods, while weak monsoons can lead to droughts. Tap each case to see what changes in the village. The monsoon also shapes daily life, transport, festivals and employment, especially in rural areas.
