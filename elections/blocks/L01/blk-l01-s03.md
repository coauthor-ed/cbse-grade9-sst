# Why Elections Matter

<!-- STEP -->
## The Answer Is No, All Three Times

Gurmat answers to her class, and a fresh vote is how the class judges her work. A government must also seek the people's permission, called a **mandate**, again and again. If only one party is contesting, voters have no real choice.

Elected representatives are **accountable** to the people through periodic elections.

**An election is how we use our right to vote and fulfil our responsibility as citizens.**

<!-- STEP -->
## Accountability and Representation

This wheel shows why elections are important in a democracy. Here are its first two petals.

- **Accountability:** those elected must answer to the people.
- **Representation:** those elected speak and decide for the people who chose them.

An election gives people a way to act on both. They can keep a representative who speaks for them, or choose someone else.

<!-- STEP -->
## Equality and Legitimacy

Two more petals complete the part of the wheel that is already named.

- **Equality:** every citizen has one equal vote.
- **Legitimacy:** people accept a government's right to decide because they chose it.

A rich voter and a poor voter each put in one vote, and the same count decides who governs.
