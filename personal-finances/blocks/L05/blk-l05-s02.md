# Four Things to Weigh

<!-- STEP -->
## Risk and return

Aarav's money belongs in a fixed deposit or savings account. The fee is due on a fixed day, and the money must be there whatever the market does.

**Risk** is the chance of losing money. **Return** is the profit earned from an investment. In three months, shares can end above ₹50,000 or below it.

<!-- STEP -->
## Time horizon

**Time horizon** is the length of time the money can stay invested. Aarav's horizon is three months, so a fall has no time to recover.

A family saving for college in fifteen years has a long horizon. One bad year in between matters much less to them.

<!-- STEP -->
## Liquidity

**Liquidity** is how easily an asset can be turned into cash when needed. A savings account gives cash the same day. A bond or shares must be sold first, at that day's price.

**Before investing, weigh four things: risk, return, time horizon and liquidity.**
