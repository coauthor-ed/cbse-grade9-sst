## Two Cousins, the Same ₹1,000

Bhavisha and Dhruv are cousins. Each puts **₹1,000** at the end of every year into the same scheme, which pays **7% a year** on whatever is already in it.

Bhavisha starts at **25** and Dhruv starts at **35**. Both stop at **65**, so she pays in for 40 years and he pays in for 30.

**Predict:** At age 65, how much money does Dhruv have?
