## Kannauj

The **Gurjara-Pratihāras** ruled large parts of northern and western India from about the 8th to the 10th centuries CE. Their main capital was **Kannauj**, in modern Uttar Pradesh, and other kingdoms of the period wanted it too.

<!-- STEP -->
## The tripartite struggle

Two more powers joined the contest: the **Pālas** in the east and the **Rāṣhṭrakūṭas** in the Deccan.

The contest to control Kannauj later came to be known as the **tripartite struggle**, because three major powers were involved.

## Below the fighting

You might expect such a contest to change everything in these kingdoms. Yet their administrative systems followed the same pattern: a mix of monarchy and decentralised units of provinces, districts and villages.

In all three, the village remained the smallest unit and was largely self-reliant in infrastructure, social welfare and education.

**Kings fought over Kannauj, while villages in all three kingdoms kept the same place in the administration.**
