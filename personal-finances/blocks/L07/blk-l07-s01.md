# Taxes

<!-- STEP -->
## Who pays for the school?

A teacher in a government school and a doctor in a government hospital are paid every month. The money comes from **taxes**: compulsory payments made by people and businesses to the government, to pay for schools, hospitals, transport, defence and other services.

<!-- STEP -->
## Direct and indirect taxes

A **direct tax** is paid directly on a person's income or a business's profit. **Income tax** is a direct tax.

An **indirect tax** is added to the price of goods and services. The **Goods and Services Tax** (GST) on a notebook bill is an indirect tax.
