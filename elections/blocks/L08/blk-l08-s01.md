# The Road to the Booth

<!-- STEP -->
## A Huge Task

Conducting elections for over **96.8 crore** voters (in 2024), with thousands of polling stations and hundreds of political parties, is a challenging task. Fig 7.13 shows it as a road to a polling booth.

<!-- STEP -->
## Three Named Potholes

Three potholes on this road have names:

- **Intimidation:** frightening people so that they vote a certain way, or do not vote at all.
- **Misinformation:** false information that spreads, often without anyone checking it.
- **Fake news:** false reports made to look like real news.
