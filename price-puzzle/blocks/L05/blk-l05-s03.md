# Surplus, Shortage and Equilibrium

<!-- STEP -->
## Too many: a surplus

The price falls. At ₹150, sellers offer 18 kg but buyers want only 6 kg, so 12 kg is left unsold. When more is offered than buyers want, there is excess supply, also called a surplus. To sell the extra mangoes, sellers lower the price.

<!-- STEP -->
## Too few: a shortage

Now start at ₹50. Buyers want 18 kg, but sellers offer only 6 kg, so 12 kg is missing. When buyers want more than is offered, there is excess demand, also called a shortage. Some buyers are ready to pay more, so the price rises.

<!-- STEP -->
## Just right: equilibrium

At ₹100, buyers want 12 kg and sellers offer 12 kg. Nothing is left over, and no buyer goes without. **Market equilibrium is the price at which the quantity demanded equals the quantity supplied.** Here the equilibrium price is ₹100 and the equilibrium quantity is 12 kg.
