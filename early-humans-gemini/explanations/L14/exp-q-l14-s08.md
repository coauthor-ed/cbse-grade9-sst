# A Model Answer

A good answer has two halves, and it needs both.

- **Independently:** the book says these civilisations emerged separately. Their scripts and great buildings are different, and only some of them were in strong contact.
- **Common features:** fertile river plains, farming surplus, cities, social and administrative systems, and writing.

Give one example from a civilisation for each feature. For writing, say what its first job was: lists of goods in Sumer, questions on bone in China. The Harappan script is unread, so say that too.
