# Why you do not feel it

<!-- STEP -->
## Air presses from all sides

You might picture air pressing only from above, but it presses from every side: down on your head, up under your arms and in on your chest. Tap Outside to see it.

<!-- STEP -->
## Your body presses back

The pressure inside your body is equal to the atmospheric pressure, so it pushes out as hard as the air pushes in. Tap Inside, then Both.

**We do not feel the air's pressure because the pressure inside our bodies cancels the pressure from outside.**
