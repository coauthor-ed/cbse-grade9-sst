# Shares and the SENSEX

<!-- STEP -->
## A share

A **share** makes you a part-owner of a company. It can earn in two ways. One is a **dividend**, a part of the company's profit paid to its shareholders. The other is a rise in the share price.

Neither is fixed. Share prices go up and down with market conditions, how the company performs and changes in the economy.

<!-- STEP -->
## The SENSEX

The **SENSEX** is an index of the Bombay Stock Exchange. It follows **30 large companies** from different parts of the economy, so one number shows how the market as a whole is doing.

The chart shows it from 1998 to 2026. It ends many times higher, but it falls sharply in some years, such as 2008 and 2020.

## Buy one year, sell later

Pick a year to buy and a year to sell. Around the falls, a one-year window can end lower than it began. A family that needed its money that year would get back less than it put in. Over twenty years the same line ends higher.

**A rise over many years does not help money that is needed next year.** Why do you think the SENSEX goes up and down? Discuss with your classmates.
