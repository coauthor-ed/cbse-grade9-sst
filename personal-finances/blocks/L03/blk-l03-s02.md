# Less Than Half

<!-- STEP -->
## The two totals at 65

Bhavisha paid in **₹40,000** of her own money, and at 65 she has **₹1,99,635**.

Dhruv paid in **₹30,000**, and at 65 he has **₹94,461**. That is about **47%** of Bhavisha's amount: less than half, not three quarters.

<!-- STEP -->
## The first ten years

Move the age on the chart from 25 to 65. For the first ten years Bhavisha's total grows slowly. After ten years she has about ₹13,816, only a little more than the ₹10,000 she paid in.

The big growth comes later, which is why the slow start is easy to undervalue.

<!-- STEP -->
## The gap is not ₹10,000

Bhavisha paid in only ₹10,000 more than Dhruv. But at 65 the gap between them is **₹1,05,174**.

**Ten years of an early start are worth far more than ten extra deposits.** The next slide shows where the extra money comes from.
