# One Rock, Three Rulers

<!-- STEP -->
## The rock near Girnar

The **Junagadh Rock Inscription** near Girnar in Gujarat is unusual, because it carries inscriptions by three rulers from different periods. On the date strip below it, the gaps are true to scale.

<!-- STEP -->
## Aśhoka

The earliest belong to the Mauryan emperor **Aśhoka**, in the 3rd century BCE. His Major Rock Edicts, in **Prakrit**, spread the message of **Dhamma** and moral governance. Dhamma is the Pāli word for dharma, which Lesson 7 explains.

<!-- STEP -->
## Rudradaman I

About 400 years later, the Western Satrap ruler **Rudradaman I** (c. 150 CE) added a long **Sanskrit** inscription. It describes his military achievements and the repair of the **Sudarshana Lake**.

<!-- STEP -->
## Skandagupta

Still later, the Gupta emperor **Skandagupta** (5th century CE) engraved another record on the same rock. It notes the restoration of the lake after severe damage.

**One rock keeps the records of three dynasties, across about 700 years.**

<!-- STEP -->
## A question to think about

Why did different rulers record their words on the same stone, and not on a new one? Discuss these ideas.

- Was the rock already a well-known place?
- Did a later ruler want to be seen beside earlier ones?
- Did the lake nearby matter to the rulers who repaired it?
