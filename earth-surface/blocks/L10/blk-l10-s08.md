## One Valley, Four Measures, One Budget

The next slide is a map of one Himalayan valley, and it is the only place in this chapter where you get to do something about any of it.

The valley has a **village** on the floor, a **road** cut into the western slope, a **moraine lake** high above it, and a **farmed slope** on the eastern side.

You have a budget of **four measures** to place before the season:

- **Retain the forest** on the slide path.
- A **check dam** across the stream.
- A **warning system** for the valley below.
- **Vegetation binding** on the dune edge.

Drag each one somewhere on the map. A placed measure protects the thing it touches and nothing else.

**A measure in the wrong place does nothing at all - and there is no clock.**
