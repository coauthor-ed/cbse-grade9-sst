## Two texts on kingship

Two texts tell us much of what we know about what a king should do.

- The **Arthaśhāstra** of Kauṭilya is a treatise on statecraft.
- The **Śhānti Parva** of the Mahābhārata guides rulers on ethical conduct, on justice and on their duties to their subjects.

<!-- STEP -->
## A question to think about

Why was it important for rulers to protect people and ensure justice? What do these texts tell us about the relationship between rulers and subjects? Weigh these before you answer.

- People need safety before they can farm or trade.
- A duty runs from the ruler to the people, not only from the people to the ruler.
- These texts say what a king should do. They do not prove that every king did it.
