# A Hilltop in Karnataka

<!-- STEP id="hill" -->
## Sanganakallu

Far to the south of Mehrgarh stands a granite hill with a Neolithic site on top. This is **Sanganakallu** in Karnataka, and the book dates it to 2000 BCE.

<!-- STEP id="deccan" -->
## A Different Landscape

There is no great river valley here. The site is a hilltop of boulders and natural terraces, looking out over the dry Deccan plain.

<!-- STEP id="spread" -->
## Farming Across the Subcontinent

Farming villages appeared in many other places. By 2500 BCE, most of the Indian subcontinent was occupied by Neolithic farming communities. Some of them lived near Chalcolithic groups and interacted with them.

<!-- STEP id="solution" -->
## A Similar Way of Life

The land was different and so were some of the crops. The way of life was similar, though: people herded cattle, sheep and goats, and grew cereals, millets and pulses.

**Different land, a similar way of life.**

In the north-west, villages of this kind grew into something far larger. The next lesson finds cities there.
