## Near-complete control

In the territorial sea the government can make laws on customs, immigration and pollution control, just as it does on land. The control is near-complete and not total, because foreign ships keep one right here.

<!-- STEP -->
## Innocent passage

**Innocent passage** lets foreign ships pass through the territorial sea as long as they do not threaten the country's peace, good order or security.

Innocent does not mean that a ship looks harmless. A rusty fishing boat and a huge warship are judged the same way, by what they do. A ship must move continuously without stopping, except in an emergency or a breakdown.
