## The Scale of the Thing

Every ridge and hollow you have just watched being cut fits on one screen. A real yardang field does not.

This is what it actually looks like: **parallel flat-topped rock ridges running in the same direction for kilometres**, with wind-swept sandy corridors between them, on a bare hyper-arid plateau with no soil and no plants anywhere in the frame.

Look at how many ridges there are, and how none of them stops within the picture. A single yardang is a rock you could walk around. A field of them is a range.

**The scene cuts one ridge so you can see the mechanism. The desert builds thousands, all pointing the same way.**
