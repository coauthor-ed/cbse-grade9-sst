# Why Did They Leave Africa?

Your textbook asks why early humans left Africa. Nobody knows for certain, because the ground cannot show what anyone was thinking. Here are three reasons historians offer.

<!-- STEP id="drift" -->
## Small Moves, Not One Journey

The word "left" makes it sound like one decision, but it was probably nothing like that. Imagine each generation camping a few kilometres further on than the one before. Over thousands of generations, those small moves add up to a very long way.

<!-- STEP id="follow" -->
## Following Food and Water

Herds of animals moved, water sources changed, and grassland grew and shrank as the climate changed. People who lived by hunting and gathering probably moved with their food.

<!-- STEP id="corridor" -->
## When the Climate Allowed It

A way across that is open in a wet time can be closed in a dry time. So the climate probably did more than any plan to decide when people could move.

**These are reasons offered by historians, and each one is an inference.**

Bodies changed over millions of years, while tools changed faster. The next lesson opens on a puzzle about those tools: they became smaller, not bigger, the longer people made them.
