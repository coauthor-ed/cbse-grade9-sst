# What the Floods Did

<!-- STEP -->
## Effects on people and land

Many people lost their lives, and thousands moved to relief camps.

Farmland, crops like paddy, poultry and dairy farms, roads, bridges, border fences and public buildings were damaged.

Murky standing water spread waterborne diseases.

<!-- STEP -->
## Discuss in class

Talk about these four questions with your class.

1. How far did natural factors cause these floods, compared to human activities?
2. Could better planning have reduced the damage? How?
3. What are the guidelines of the National Disaster Management Authority (NDMA) for managing floods?
4. What can students and young people do to help with disaster preparedness?
