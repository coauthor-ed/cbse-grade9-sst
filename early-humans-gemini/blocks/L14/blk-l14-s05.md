# What Was Not Shared

The list is shared, but the great buildings are not. Each civilisation built with what it had, for its own reasons.

<!-- STEP id="ziggurat" -->
## Mesopotamia: The Temple Tower

The plain had little stone, so the Sumerians built ziggurats of mud brick, as you saw. A ziggurat was a temple and also a treasury.

<!-- STEP id="pyramid" -->
## Egypt: The Stone Tomb

Egypt had limestone cliffs. Pharaohs used this stone for pyramids, which were tombs built to protect the king's body and his ka. Robbers got into most of them all the same.

<!-- STEP id="harappan" -->
## The Harappans: Water and Streets

Harappan builders put their effort into drains, tanks and the dockyard at Lothal. No tomb or temple on the scale of the ziggurat or the pyramid has been found at their cities.

<!-- STEP id="greatwall" -->
## China: The Long Wall

China's great work was the wall along its northern frontier, built by many rulers against raids by nomadic tribes. It is not a single building at all.

<!-- STEP id="synthesis" -->
## The Scripts Differ Too

Scholars read cuneiform and hieroglyphs again with the help of old texts in known languages. The Sindhu lipi is still unread. Chinese characters never went out of use, so they run from the oracle bones to today.

**Each civilisation answered in its own way, with its own materials.**
