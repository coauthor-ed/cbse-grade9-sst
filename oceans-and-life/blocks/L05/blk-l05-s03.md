## Only the near boat

Only the boat 30 nautical miles out gets the warning. It is easy to think a satellite reaches everywhere, but at present the channel carries information only up to 50 nautical miles into the sea, about 93 km. A boat beyond the limit gets no message on GEMINI, so fishers check the forecast before they sail. Drag the far boat in until it is inside the ring.

## From the sky to the phone

The GEMINI device receives data from the GAGAN satellite and passes it to the mobile phone through Bluetooth. The INCOIS app then shows it in nine regional languages. GEMINI stands for GAGAN-Enabled Mariner's Instrument for Navigation and Information.

## A wider ring is planned

An upgraded version is expected to give two-way communication on a pilot basis, so that fishers can send feedback too. It will benefit fishers who travel up to 300 nautical miles in search of better fishing.
