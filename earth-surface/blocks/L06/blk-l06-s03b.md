## The Outer Leg Stands Alone

A tall pillar is left. The fallen roof does not make it: those blocks break up in the waves. The pillar is the **outer leg** of the arch, now cut off from the land. It is called a **sea stack**.

<!-- STEP -->
## Stack to Stump

Waves keep cutting the foot of the stack until it falls too. A low **stump** is left, which may show only at low tide.

Press **Waves work** to watch it.
