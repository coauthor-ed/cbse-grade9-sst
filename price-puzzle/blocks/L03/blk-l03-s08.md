## Along the curve

A change in the good's own price moves sellers along the supply curve. When the price of mangoes rises, sellers offer more. When it falls, they offer less. The curve itself stays where it is.

<!-- STEP -->
## What else changes supply

Sellers also look at other things. These include the price of other crops they could grow, new tools and machines, the number of sellers, and what they expect next. A change in any of these moves the whole supply curve. The next lesson looks at each of them.
