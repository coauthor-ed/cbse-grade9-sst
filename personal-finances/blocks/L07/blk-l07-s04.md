# A Small and a Large Income

<!-- STEP -->
## Sai pays nothing

Sai's taxable income is **₹3,80,000** in a year. All of it falls in the first slab, up to ₹4,00,000, where the rate is nil. So Sai pays no income tax.

<!-- STEP -->
## A raise across a slab line

Anil's taxable income rises from **₹16,00,000** to **₹16,10,000**. The extra ₹10,000 falls in the 20% slab.

The first ₹16,00,000 is taxed exactly as before: ₹1,20,000. Only the extra ₹10,000 is taxed at 20%, so his tax rises by **₹2,000**. He keeps ₹8,000 of the raise.
