# A Hospital Bill

<!-- STEP -->
## A good plan

The parent saving for college has a good plan. The money is in a mix that can wait out a bad year, because there are fifteen years to go.

In the third year, a family member falls ill. A hospital bill of **₹2,00,000** has to be paid now.

<!-- STEP -->
## What a plan cannot do

To pay the bill, the family takes the college savings out, even though the market is low that year. The fifteen-year plan has to start again.

An investment plan decides where money grows. It cannot stop a sudden large loss. **So what can protect a family from one?**
