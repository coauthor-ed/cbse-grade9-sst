## Two stalls, one drink

Two teams at the festival sell the same nimbu pani. Each glass costs **₹6** to make: lemons, sugar, ice and a paper cup. These are made-up numbers.

Stall A charges **₹5** a glass and sells **200 glasses**. Stall B charges **₹15** a glass and sells **80 glasses**.

**Predict:** which stall has more money left at the end of the day, after paying for what it used?
