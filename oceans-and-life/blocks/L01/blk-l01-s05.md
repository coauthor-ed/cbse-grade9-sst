## A ridge with a valley along its top

Far out in the ocean the floor rises into the **mid-ocean ridge**, an immense underwater mountain range formed by volcanic activity. A **rift valley** runs along its top, and magma rises from below there.

<!-- STEP -->
## Seamount and volcanic island

A volcano can also grow from the sea floor. If it does not reach the surface, it is a **seamount**. If it grows through the surface, it is a **volcanic island**.

<!-- STEP -->
## From island to guyot

When the volcano stops growing, waves wear its top flat over a very long time. Later the mountain slowly sinks below the sea. A flat-topped underwater mountain like this is a **guyot**.

<!-- STEP -->
## Three more shapes

**Ocean trenches** are the deepest parts of the ocean. A **submarine canyon** is a deep valley cut into the edge of a continent. **Abyssal hills** are low hills that rise from the deep floor.
