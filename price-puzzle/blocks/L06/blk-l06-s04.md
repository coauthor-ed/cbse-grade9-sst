# One Seller: a Monopoly

<!-- STEP -->
## Only one seller

Sometimes one seller controls the whole supply of a product that has no close substitute. This is a monopoly. With no other seller to compete with, a monopoly can charge a higher price, supply less, or sell goods of poorer quality.

<!-- STEP -->
## Keeping it in check

The government does not let a monopoly harm buyers. It makes rules that keep the monopoly's price and the quantity it supplies in check, closer to what a market with many sellers would give.

<!-- STEP -->
## Regulators

The government sets up regulators to watch different markets and keep them open and honest:
- **Reserve Bank of India (RBI):** banking
- **Central Consumer Protection Authority:** consumer rights and unfair trade
- **Telecom Regulatory Authority of India (TRAI):** telephone and internet services
- **Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI):** the share market
