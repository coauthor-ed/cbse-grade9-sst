# From Citizens to Two Paths

<!-- STEP -->
## Before the Vote

Fig 7.11 starts with **citizens**. **Political parties** organise public opinion among them. Parties then **contest elections**: they plan campaigns and prepare **manifestos**.

A manifesto is a party's written list of what it promises to do. It comes out before polling, so voters can read it and can later ask whether the promises were kept.

<!-- STEP -->
## After the Result

The result splits the parties into two paths.

- The **winning party** forms the government, then formulates and implements policies.
- The **opposition party** ensures accountability, and takes part in debates and committees.
