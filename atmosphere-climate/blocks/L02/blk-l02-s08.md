# Lights Near the Poles

<!-- STEP -->
## Auroras

The northern and southern lights, called **auroras**, occur in the thermosphere.

The Sun sends out charged particles, known as the **solar wind**. These are directed towards the Earth's magnetic poles. As they meet gases in the air, each gas glows with a particular colour.

<!-- STEP -->
## A name from the dawn

*Aurora* is Latin for dawn or morning light. It comes from the name of the Roman goddess of dawn.

- **Aurora Borealis** is seen in the Northern Hemisphere.
- **Aurora Australis** is seen in the Southern Hemisphere.
