# One More Payment

<!-- STEP -->
## Premiums and EMIs

A family's month now has two regular payments. A **premium** goes to an insurance company, to share risk with many others. An **EMI** goes to a lender, to repay a loan with interest.

The family chose both of them.

<!-- STEP -->
## Income tax

One more part of income goes out that the family does not choose: **income tax**, paid to the government. It pays for roads, schools, hospitals and defence.

**How is income tax worked out, and how much does each person pay?** The next lesson answers this.
