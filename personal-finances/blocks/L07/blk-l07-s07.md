## The five pillars

Personal finance rests on five pillars: **income**, **budgeting**, **saving**, **investing** and **protection**. Managing money wisely is not only about earning more. It is about careful choices at every stage: spending, saving, investing, protecting against risks, borrowing responsibly and paying taxes.

<!-- STEP -->
## Before we move on

- Making informed money decisions from a young age builds security, independence and confidence.
- Budgeting plans expenses, controls unnecessary spending and saves for future needs.
- Saving and investing build wealth, meet goals and protect money from inflation.
- Investment choices should match a person's goals and the risk they can bear.
- Insurance protects families from losses they cannot plan for.
- Income tax pays for public services; paying it honestly is a citizen's responsibility.
