# Duties Depend on Roles

<!-- STEP -->
## One word, many duties

Dharma is one word, and the duties it asks for depend on the role. A student has duties towards parents and teachers. A ruler has to protect the people and give justice. A candidate for the assembly should have honest earnings.

Everyone in society holds some role, so everyone has some duty.

<!-- STEP -->
## Who holds which role?

Now a new question follows. If duties depend on roles, how did people come to hold a role? Did birth decide what work a person would do, or could a person change?
