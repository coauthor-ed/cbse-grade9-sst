Media provenance - L01

Per `docs/copyright-and-sourcing.md`: the textbook's own photographs are
never extracted. Each photograph below is an openly-licensed photograph of
Indian voting, sourced from Wikimedia Commons, standing in for the source's
`Fig. 7.2` (p.3 / book p.163). All six are Election Commission of India
photographs released under the Government Open Data Licence - India
(GODL-India), which permits reuse with attribution.

Downloaded as 520-960px wide JPEG renditions of the Commons originals.
Thumbnails are served by Wikimedia's thumb host; the original file name and
its Commons file page are recorded below so any of these can be replaced at
full resolution without changing the block.

All six are used by `blocks/L01/blk-l01-s06.json` -> `photoDiagram.nodes[]`.
