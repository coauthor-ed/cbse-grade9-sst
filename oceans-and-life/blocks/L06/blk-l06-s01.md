# 26 December 2004

On that morning, an undersea earthquake off the coast of Sumatra moved the sea floor, and giant waves spread across the Indian Ocean. Around 2,30,000 people died in the many countries around that ocean.

<!-- STEP -->
## India's coasts

India was among the worst affected countries. Tamil Nadu, Puducherry, Andhra Pradesh, Keralam and the Andaman & Nicobar Islands suffered extensive damage, and areas of Chennai were among those affected. The disaster led many countries to build tsunami early warning systems.
