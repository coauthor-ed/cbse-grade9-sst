## A business plan

Before opening, you write a **business plan**: a written document that explains the goals of the business and the steps to reach them. It guides you from the first idea to starting and running the bakery. It also helps you stay organised and decide what matters most.

<!-- STEP -->
## Eight things to think about

The book lists eight things to explore in a business plan: needs and idea, potential customers, money and costs, location, human resources, pricing and profits, promotion, and challenges and risks. They are not steps in a fixed order. A good plan answers all eight, and each answer affects the others.

<!-- STEP -->
## Customers change the other answers

Knowing your customers helps you decide what to offer. For children, you might bake cakes and cupcakes and keep prices low. For young adults, savoury snacks. For families, everyday bread. A shop near the school suits children; one near homes suits families. So one change of customer changes many other answers.
