## What the Ice Carries

Glaciers carry rock, soil and debris called **till**. When the ice melts, this is left behind in heaps called **moraines**.

**Lateral moraines** form along the sides of a glacier.

<!-- STEP -->
## Two Glaciers Join

When two glaciers meet, their lateral moraines join in the middle. This line is a **medial moraine**, and while the ice lasts it lies on top of the glacier.

<!-- STEP -->
## The End of the Ice

A **terminal moraine** forms at the end of a glacier, so it marks how far the ice once reached.

Press **Melt the ice** to see what remains.

## Reading Bare Ground

The ice has gone, and the moraines remain as ridges of loose rock. This is how we can tell where a glacier once stood.
