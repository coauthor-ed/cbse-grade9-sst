# What the Key Opened

<!-- STEP id="plant" -->
## A Paper Made From a Plant

Now the papyrus scrolls could be read. Papyrus was made from a tall plant that grew along the Nile, by cutting the inner trunk into strips.

<!-- STEP id="strip" -->
## From Plant to Sheet

The strips were laid criss-cross, pressed, dried and polished into sheets. The Papyrus Ebers, named after its discoverer, lists more than 700 cures and spells of Egyptian medicine.

<!-- STEP id="scroll" -->
## Stories in Jars

Libraries dating back to 2000 BCE stored scrolls in labelled jars on shelves. One jar held the oldest version of the story of Sindbad the Sailor. There are animal fables that suggest a link to Aesop's Fables, tales of ghosts and miracles, and even the oldest known form of Cinderella.

**Some stories you know have very old forms in Egypt.**
