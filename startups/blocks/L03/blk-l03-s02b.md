## Money and costs

A bakery needs money before it sells a single cake. This first amount is the **initial investment**. It pays for equipment such as an oven, rent for the shop, salaries for the staff, ingredients such as flour and nuts, and packaging.

<!-- STEP -->
## Where the money comes from

The plan also says how the money will be arranged. It can come from your own savings, from a loan from a bank, or from friends, relatives or investors. You and your friends have to decide which of these you will use.

<!-- STEP -->
## Keeping track of the money

Once the bakery opens, you write down every rupee that comes in and every rupee that goes out. This is **accounting**: recording and tracking all the money related to the business, so the owner knows how it is performing.
