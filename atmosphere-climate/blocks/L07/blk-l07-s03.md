# Hills Lift the Wet Air

<!-- STEP -->
## Forced up the slope

The hills are the reason. Wet air from the Bay of Bengal blows over flat Bengal and has to rise over the Meghalaya hills.

Rising air expands and cools, so its water vapour condenses, and heavy rain falls on the slopes.

<!-- STEP -->
## Flat land lifts nothing

Kolkata stands on flat land, so the same air is not forced up there. It still gets plenty of rain, but less.

In June, Shillong gets **47.6 cm** and Kolkata gets **29.0 cm**.
