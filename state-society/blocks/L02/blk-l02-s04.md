# Sixteen on the Map

<!-- STEP -->
## Sixteen numbers

Each number on the map is a mahājanapada. Tap a number to see its name.

You do not need to learn all sixteen now. Look at where they sit.

<!-- STEP -->
## Where they cluster

Tap each region to light its mahājanapadas. **Most lie in the Ganga plain and the north-west.**

Only three lie south of the Yamuna: Chedi, Avanti and Aśhmaka. The kingdoms of the far south are not among the sixteen.
