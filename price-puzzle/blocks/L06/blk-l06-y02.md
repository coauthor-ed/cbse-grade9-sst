# Your Turn: Thinking as a Citizen

In the chapter on democracy, you read that a democratic government answers to the people and is expected to act in their interest. Discuss these with a partner or in class.

1. How should a democratic government decide when, and how much, it should act in markets to protect people's welfare?
2. Whose voices should it listen to before it decides: buyers, producers, workers, or others? Why?
3. Can too many government rules hurt a market? Give an example.
