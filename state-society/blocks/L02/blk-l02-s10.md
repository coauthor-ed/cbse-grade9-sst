# What Is a Ruler For?

## Magadha's reach

Magadha's control has spread over its neighbours, and a great deal of land is now ruled by one ruler.

## A large land and one ruler

A map can show how far a ruler's control reaches. It cannot show what he must do with that land.

An old text on governing, the **Arthaśhāstra**, gives one answer: "Only if a king is himself energetically active, do his officers follow him energetically."

Lesson 3 asks what a king was expected to do.
