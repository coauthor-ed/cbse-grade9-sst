## A storm shows in the sky

A cyclone is a storm in the air, so its spiral of cloud can be seen from above. Satellites follow it for days, and forecasts give people time to move.

<!-- STEP -->
## 26 December 2004

That morning, an earthquake moved the sea floor below the Indian Ocean. Nothing in the sky showed it, because no storm caused the danger. There was no cloud for anyone to see.
