# Where Did the Gift Go?

<!-- STEP -->
## A gift of ₹2,000

At Diwali, Meera's uncle gives her ₹2,000 in a gift cover. It feels like a lot of money, because nothing she usually buys costs more than a few hundred rupees.

Three weeks later the cover is empty, and Meera cannot name one big thing she bought with it.

<!-- STEP -->
## A week at a time

Tap **next week** on the picture. Each week, four small payments leave the pile: chaat with friends, a movie ticket, a gift for a friend, a phone cover. The biggest is only ₹250.

After three weeks, twelve small payments have used up the whole ₹2,000. None of them felt like spending a lot.

<!-- STEP -->
## Personal finance

Meera looked at her money only after it was spent. **Personal finance** means planning and managing your money to meet your needs and wants. It covers four decisions: how much to spend, how much to save, where to invest, and how to prepare for later needs, such as a house you want to buy in five years.
