# Supply Can Change When the Price Does Not

<!-- STEP -->
## When the whole curve moves

A change in the price of mangoes moves sellers along the supply curve. But supply can also change while the price stays the same. Then the whole supply curve moves. It moves right if sellers offer more at every price, and left if they offer less.

<!-- STEP -->
## What moves the supply curve

This lesson looks at four of them. They are the prices of other goods the seller could make, the number of sellers, technology, and what sellers expect. Costs, weather and disasters move it too.
