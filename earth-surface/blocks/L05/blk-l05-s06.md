## The Sundarbans

The Ganga and Brahmaputra meet the Bay of Bengal in the largest delta in the world. Its sea edge is the **Sundarbans**, in India and Bangladesh. Other Indian deltas include those of the Godavari, Krishna, Kaveri and Mahanadi.

<!-- STEP -->
## Why People Visit

Tourists come for the mangrove forest, a World Heritage Site, and for its home to the Bengal tiger. Mangroves grow in salty water, and tides run through hundreds of winding channels, so boats are the usual way to explore it.
