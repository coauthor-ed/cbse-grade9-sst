## Your turn: spot a problem

Over the next week, watch your daily routine at school and at home. Find two or three problems that you or others face. For each one, make a table that answers: Who faces it? How often does it happen? Why has it not been solved yet? What business idea could solve it? Would people pay for it?

## Your turn: find out more

Visit a factory or a production unit. Ask the owner or manager what it makes, who buys it, how it started and what challenges it faced. Note what holds the business back and suggest solutions in a short report. Also find out about other communities in India's regions who have done well as entrepreneurs.
