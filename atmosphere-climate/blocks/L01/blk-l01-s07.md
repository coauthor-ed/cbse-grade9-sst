# Challenge: Seal the Canister

<!-- STEP -->
## Three canisters, three readings

A canister will not seal until what is inside it is fit for the job printed on the card above it.

- **A room** a person can breathe in.
- **A tank** that can hold a flame.
- **A store** a seed can be kept in until it is time to plant.

Each card is a different reading of the same question - what has to be true about the mixture. The three do not have the same answer.

<!-- STEP -->
## Fill it, and it refuses to close

Choose a gas, press **Fill**, and the canister takes that share. Keep going until the total reaches 100.

Fill it wrongly and the lid will not shut. There is no clock here, no score to protect and no limit on how many times you can start over - the canister will simply stay open until the mixture is right.

**You cannot seal a canister with a mixture you guessed at. You have to reason about which gas each reading actually depends on.**
