## What Glaciers Leave for Us

Glacial landforms are useful to people. Tap each one.

- **U-shaped valleys and cirques:** trekking, skiing and mountaineering
- **Fjords:** harbours and fishing
- **Glacial soil:** farming in some valleys
- **Moraines:** fertile soil, and natural dams and lakes for water supply, irrigation and sometimes hydroelectric power
- **Glaciers:** fresh water for rivers
