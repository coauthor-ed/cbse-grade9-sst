## Where the bakery stands

Location means two things: where the goods will be made, and where they will be sold. ABC Bakery can bake in its own kitchen and sell at a shop counter. It can also sell online.

<!-- STEP -->
## A busy area or a quiet lane

A shop in a busy area, near schools, colleges or markets, can bring in more customers, because more people walk past it. In real life, a shop in a busy area usually costs more rent than one in a quiet lane. The plan has to weigh more customers against the higher rent.

<!-- STEP -->
## Selling online

Selling online, or taking orders through mobile apps, can help you reach more people. Families who live far from the shop can order a cake and have it delivered. So even a bakery in a quiet lane can reach customers beyond its own street.
