# When Government Action Goes Too Far

<!-- STEP -->
## Prices set too low

Suppose the government sets a highest price for wheat of ₹20 per kg, while the market price is ₹30 per kg. Farmers now earn less than they would in a free market. They may grow less wheat, and there may be a shortage.

<!-- STEP -->
## Too many permissions

A small restaurant may need many permissions: for food safety, fire safety, pollution control and local clearances. The time and money this takes can stop small business owners from starting or growing. Ease of doing business means how simple it is to start, run and close a business.

<!-- STEP -->
## Less new investment

If farmers cannot earn enough, they will not spend on better seeds, irrigation or new technology. Over the years, they grow less than they could have. **Government action helps when markets fail, but too much of it can harm the market.**
