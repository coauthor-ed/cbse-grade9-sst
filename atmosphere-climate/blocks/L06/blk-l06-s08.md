# Challenge: Draw the Isocrones

<!-- STEP -->
## A blank map, and the dates you have just been shown

The map in the scene is the subcontinent with nothing on it - no front, no lines, no dates.

You have two tools and a row of dated isochrones to place: **the day the front reaches each part of the country, and the line it has drawn on that day.**

<!-- STEP -->
## What actually gets scored

A set of lines is right only if each line sits where the leading edge is on the date written on it.

Put 30 June along the far south and it will not close, whatever else is right. Draw the north-west line early and it will not close either. **The win condition is the front's position on a given date** - so the two sweeps have to be in your head at once: coast first and inland behind it on the way in, north-west first and south-east behind it on the way out.

The diagonals are the whole task. A learner who has only the advance in mind draws every line in the same direction and gets nowhere.

<!-- STEP -->
## Two things that will not help you

**Order does not rescue a wrong diagonal.** The lines are checked against the front's position, not against each other, so a set of lines in a tidy sequence that slopes the wrong way still fails.

**Neither sweep is a straight line across the map.** They follow the coast first and the interior behind, and if you draw a neat even band from one edge to the other, the ends will be wrong.

<!-- STEP -->
## Take your time

No timer, no moves to protect, unlimited retries, the same map each time. **There is no version of this that rewards guessing quickly.**
