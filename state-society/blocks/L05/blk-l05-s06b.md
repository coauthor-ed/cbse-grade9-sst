## Who Does This Work?

You have now met officers at several levels, and the committees of a village. Here are eight matters from daily life in a kingdom.

**Tap a matter, then tap who deals with it.** Then press Check my answer.

- Village assemblies had small committees, the **variyams**, for tasks like irrigation, gardens and temple management.
- A **district governor** had judicial and administrative work.
- A **city governor** collected revenue and kept law and order.
- The **minister of peace and war** was a new Gupta post.
