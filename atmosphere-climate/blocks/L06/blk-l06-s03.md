# Five Weeks to Cross the Country

<!-- STEP -->
## About five weeks

It takes about five weeks, from **1 June** over Kerala to **8 July** over far western Rajasthan.

The hottest land does not get the wind first. The desert comes last, because the moist wind has to cross most of the country to reach it. **The monsoon spreads slowly.**

<!-- STEP -->
## Reading the lines

Each line joins places that usually get the monsoon on the same date. Such a line is called an **isochrone**, which means "same time".

These are **normal dates**: in a real year the rain can come earlier or later. Tap a place to read its date.

<!-- STEP -->
## It starts at sea, in two arms

The first lines, 22 May and 26 May, lie over the sea. Then the monsoon moves in from two seas.

The Arabian Sea branch runs up the west coast. The Bay of Bengal branch reaches the north-east early, then turns up the Ganga plain. So Shillong gets the monsoon in early June, and Delhi around the end of June.
