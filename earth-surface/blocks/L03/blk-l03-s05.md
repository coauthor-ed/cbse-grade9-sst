# Does India Have a Risk of Earthquakes?

<!-- STEP -->
## Yes

India has experienced some major earthquakes, resulting in thousands of deaths. The areas of highest risk are the Himalayan arc, the North-East, Kachchh, and the Andaman and Nicobar Islands.

<!-- STEP -->
## Near plate edges

India's plate pushes north into Asia, so the Himalayan arc and the North-East lie on an active boundary.

The Andaman and Nicobar Islands lie where the Indian plate sinks under the plate to the east.

<!-- STEP -->
## Kachchh: inside the plate

Earthquakes do not happen only at plate edges. Kachchh lies inside the plate, yet old faults there still break as the plate pushes north.

A major earthquake struck Gujarat on 26 January 2001, centred near Bhuj in Kachchh, and caused extensive damage.

<!-- STEP -->
## Why lives are at risk

India is densely populated, so a large earthquake can cause severe damage to life and the environment.

In towns, most harm comes when weak buildings fall, so the way we build matters.
