## When the Land Turns Dangerous

The land gives us valleys, rivers and snow, and it can also turn dangerous. Four disasters come with particular landforms: **landslides**, **avalanches**, **glacial lake outburst floods** and **dust storms**.

We begin with a hillside.

<!-- STEP -->
## What Rain Does to a Slope

Heavy, continuous rain is one of the main natural causes of landslides. Water seeps into soil and rock, which makes the slope heavier. It also reduces **friction**, the grip that holds the pieces in place.

Try **Dry**, **Light** and **Heavy** and read both bars.

<!-- STEP -->
## Shaking and Loose Rock

Rain is not the only cause. Earthquakes and volcanic eruptions can trigger landslides by shaking the ground, so press **Shake**.

Steep slopes and loose or weathered rocks add to the risk.
