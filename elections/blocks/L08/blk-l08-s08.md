## The Four Big Questions

- **Why are elections essential to a democratic system?** They give every citizen an equal voice.
- **How do electoral systems and rules shape representation and fairness?** The same votes can give different seats.
- **What role do institutions and laws play?** They keep the process free and fair.
- **What challenges do elections face in practice?** Fake news and intimidation can reduce an election's democratic value.

<!-- STEP -->
## Before We Move On

- Casting a vote is a constitutional right as well as a duty of every citizen.
- Elections give every citizen an equal voice in forming a government.
- Laws and bodies like the Delimitation Commission and the ECI work to keep the process free, fair and transparent.
- Political parties present diverse viewpoints, helping voters make informed choices.
- Despite many challenges, the ECI tries to ensure that elections are carried out impartially.
