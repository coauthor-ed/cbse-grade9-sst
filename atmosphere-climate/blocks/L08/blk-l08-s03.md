# A Small Share Can Matter

<!-- STEP -->
## Yes, it can

Yes, it can. Heat rising from the ground passes straight through nitrogen and oxygen. It is caught by small shares of other gases, such as carbon dioxide and water vapour.

A pinch of chilli is a tiny part of a pot of dal, yet you can taste it. **What matters is which gas holds heat.**

<!-- STEP -->
## One and a half times as much

In the 1800s carbon dioxide was under 0.03 per cent of the air. Today it is about 0.04 per cent, **about one and a half times as much**, mainly from burning coal, oil and gas.

The Earth has warmed by a little over 1 °C.
