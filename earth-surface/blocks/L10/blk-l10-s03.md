## Natural and Human Causes

Tap **Natural** and **Human** to light each set of causes on the slope.

- **Natural:** heavy rain, earthquakes and eruptions, steep slopes, loose or weathered rock.
- **Human:** cutting trees, mining, road building, unplanned building on hillsides, poor drainage.

## Where, and What Helps

In India, landslides are common in the Himalaya, the Western Ghats and the North-East. In July 2024, landslides struck Wayanad in Kerala after heavy rain.

People can lower the risk. Add a **Retaining wall**, a **Clear drain** and **Trees** to the slope.
