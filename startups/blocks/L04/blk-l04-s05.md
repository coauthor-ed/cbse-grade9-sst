## Oberoi's hotels and the four areas

Mohan Singh Oberoi began as a hotel desk clerk in Shimla and built a hotel chain. His story is about more than a good idea. He hired and trained skilled staff, and kept improving hotels and guests' stays. He managed money carefully for growth. His hotels were renovated and run efficiently. His company built a trusted brand, and customer satisfaction came first.

<!-- STEP -->
## Each action in its area

Hiring and training skilled staff is human resources. Managing money for growth is finance and accounting. Renovating hotels and running them efficiently to keep high standards is management and operations. Building a trusted brand for luxury and good service is marketing and sales. Customer satisfaction was the top priority in all the hotels' operations.

<!-- STEP -->
## What improved the quality

The book asks which area helped improve quality. One answer: management and operations kept high standards in every hotel, and human resources trained the staff who gave excellent service. The other two areas worked with them. Finance managed the money to support growth and expansion, and marketing and sales built the trusted brand.
