## Income tax return

Every year, a person with taxable income files an **income tax return** (ITR). It states how much they earned and how much tax is due for the **financial year**, which runs from 1 April to 31 March.

Paying taxes honestly and on time is a responsibility of every citizen. It helps the government provide public services and develop the country.
