## Priya's Income Tax

Priya, a business analyst, earns **₹14,00,000** a year. Before tax is worked out, a fixed amount called the **standard deduction**, ₹75,000, comes off her salary. So her **taxable income** is **₹13,25,000**.

The top part of that income falls in the slab taxed at **15%**.

**Predict:** How much income tax does Priya pay for the year?
