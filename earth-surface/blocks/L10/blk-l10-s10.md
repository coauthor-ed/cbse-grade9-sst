## Forces From Inside

What shapes the Earth's surface? The heat inside the Earth drives the plates. **Internal forces**, such as earthquakes, volcanic eruptions, folding and faulting, build mountains, valleys and ocean basins.

<!-- STEP -->
## Forces on the Surface

**External forces** wear the land down and move it. Weathering breaks rock, and erosion and deposition carry it away and lay it down again.

Five agents do this work: running water, waves and tides, glaciers, wind and underground water. Each one makes its own landforms.

<!-- STEP -->
## People Live on the Result

Landforms shape our climate, resources, settlements and cultures. They also bring dangers.

You might think a disaster is only nature's doing. A slope can fall far from anyone and harm no one. It becomes a disaster when it reaches people, and our roads, trees, drains and buildings change how likely that is.

**Understanding the land helps us prepare wisely.**

## Before We Move On

- The Earth is made up of layers: crust, mantle and core.
- Interior forces (earthquakes, volcanoes, folding and faulting) move the crust.
- External forces like weathering and erosion carve smaller landforms, and these affect human life.
- Running water, waves and tides, glaciers, wind and underground water carve the surface.
- Disasters like landslides, avalanches, glacial lake outflows (outburst floods) and sandstorms (dust storms) go with specific landforms.

## The Big Questions

1. **What shapes the Earth's surface?** Forces from inside the Earth build it up, and forces on the surface wear it down and move it.
2. **What is plate tectonics? What are the effects of plate movement?** It is the theory of how the plates of the outer shell move. Their movement causes earthquakes and volcanoes and builds mountains.
3. **How are landforms formed and how are they classified?** Landforms are grouped by what made them: internal forces, or an agent (running water, waves, glaciers, wind, underground water).
4. **How are humans and other living beings connected to these landforms?** Landforms shape our climate, resources, settlements and cultures, and living things depend on them.
5. **How do disasters associated with different landforms impact human lives?** They harm people, homes and work, and how we use the land changes the risk.
