# Your Turn: ₹1,00,000

<!-- STEP -->
## Plan the family's money

Imagine your family receives ₹1,00,000 from farming, a small business, livestock sales and savings. Help decide how to use it.

Share it among savings, education, household expenses, a health and emergency fund, farm or business investment, home improvement, festivals and recreation, and other. Write each amount and its percentage, so the total is ₹1,00,000 and 100%.

<!-- STEP -->
## Check your plan

Mark each expense as a need or a want, and give a reason. Try these: school fees, a new mobile phone, better seeds or feed, festival celebrations, a water storage tank.

Save first: if you keep ₹20,000 aside, ₹80,000 is left to spend. What percentage did you save, and how does it make the family more secure? Draw a pie chart of your plan.
