# What Prices Tell Us

<!-- STEP -->
## The lesson in short

Buyers and sellers together set the price. Above the equilibrium price there is a surplus, and the price falls. Below it there is a shortage, and the price rises. In real markets demand and supply keep changing, so the equilibrium keeps moving.

<!-- STEP -->
## Is the market price always fair?

A market can settle at a price that some people cannot pay. When medicines or food cost too much for poor families, many people ask whether the government should act. The next lesson looks at the role of the government in the economy.
