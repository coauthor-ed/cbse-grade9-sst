## What is missing

This picture shows no classroom. This place is a **gurukula**, and its centre was the teacher's home.

Students lived here, and each student was treated as a member of the teacher's family. They followed a disciplined life marked by self-control, obedience and devotion. So what was such a life meant to do for a student?
