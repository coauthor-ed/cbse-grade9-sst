## Not a bowl

The ground does not sink evenly. It runs out as a long shallow ledge, drops steeply, lies flat and deep, and then rises into mountains. A bowl is a fair first guess, because we never see the bottom.

Just as land has mountains, valleys and plains, so does the ocean floor. Together these landforms are called **submarine relief** or **ocean relief**.

<!-- STEP -->
## A ledge, then a drop

The shallow ledge next to the coast is the **continental shelf**. At its outer edge the ground falls away down the **continental slope**.

<!-- STEP -->
## A rise, a plain, a ridge

Below the slope the ground eases into the gentler **continental rise**, and then flattens into the **abyssal plain**. Further along this line stands a **mid-ocean ridge**, a range of mountains under the sea.
