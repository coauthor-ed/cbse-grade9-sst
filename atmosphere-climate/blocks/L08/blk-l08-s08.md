# Challenge: Spend the Budget

<!-- STEP -->
## A town with a fixed allowance

The game hands your town a **carbon budget** for the year - a total it is allowed to spend, in tonnes, and the total does not move no matter what you decide.

Five dials. **Power. Transport. Heating. Waste. Food.** Each one runs from a low setting to a high one, and each setting costs tonnes. That is the whole of the model.

**You cannot spend nothing.** Every one of the five dials has a floor below which the town stops functioning, and the floor is shown on the dial.

<!-- STEP -->
## What it is actually scoring

Two conditions, and both have to hold at once.

- **The total has to finish under the line.** Over budget is not a loss - it is not solved.
- **Every category has to stay above its floor.** Shutting one dial all the way down to buy room in another is not a solution either, and the town will tell you which dial you starved.

There is no timer, no score to protect and no limit on attempts. **A round that fails tells you which condition broke, and nothing else.**

<!-- STEP -->
## Why this is the concept and not a quiz about it

A carbon footprint is not one habit. It is a **sum across categories**, and the two things that make a sum hard are the two things this game will not let you skip.

You cannot win by being excellent in one place. A town with wind farms and no buses still has to cook, heat and dispose of something, and the floors are what stop that being an answer.

And you cannot win by being moderate everywhere without checking the total, because "a little in every category" is exactly how a real budget gets exceeded by people who each did something reasonable.

**Reading a sum correctly is the whole skill. The game is that skill and nothing else.**
