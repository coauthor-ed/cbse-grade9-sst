# Water Vapour and Dust

<!-- STEP -->
## One share keeps changing

The air also holds **water vapour**, which is water in the form of a gas. You cannot see it.

Its amount varies, but it generally ranges from **0.1 to 0.4 per cent** of the air. Tap **Dry day** and **Wet day** to compare the two ends.

<!-- STEP -->
## From vapour to cloud

Water evaporates from the sea, rivers and wet ground, and the vapour rises with warm air. High up, it cools and turns into tiny drops of water.

Those drops form clouds, and clouds give us rain. **This small share of the air brings all our rain.**

<!-- STEP -->
## Tiny dust particles

The air also carries **tiny dust particles**. They are solid, so they are not gases. You can see them floating in a beam of sunlight in a dark room.

Water vapour turns into drops around specks like these. So dust helps clouds to form.
