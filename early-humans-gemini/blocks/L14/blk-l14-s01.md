# Four Rivers, One List

Four early civilisations grew up in river plains, thousands of kilometres apart. People then had no fast way to travel between them, so you might expect four very different stories.

<!-- STEP id="southasia" -->
## The Sindhu-Sarasvati and Mesopotamia

In South Asia, the Harappan civilisation grew along the Sindhu and Sarasvati rivers. To the west, Mesopotamia grew around the Euphrates and the Tigris.

<!-- STEP id="egyptchina" -->
## The Nile and the Huang He

In North Africa, the Egyptian civilisation grew along the Nile. Far to the east, the Chinese civilisation grew along the Huang He and the Yangtze.

<!-- STEP id="checklist" -->
## What They Share

Yet the book finds the same features in all four:

- fertile river plains
- farming that gave a surplus, more food than the farmers needed
- cities
- social and administrative systems
- writing
