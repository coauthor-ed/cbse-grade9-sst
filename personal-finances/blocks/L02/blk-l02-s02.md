## A Bag for Next Year

Meera's school bag will wear out next year. A new one costs **₹1,000** today. She has exactly ₹1,000, but she does not need the bag until next year, so she keeps the money in the gullak.

Prices in the shops are rising by about **6% a year**, and the shop will not hold today's price for her.

**Predict:** One year later, Meera breaks the gullak and goes back to the shop. What happens?
