## The territorial sea

Now compare what a country may do in each band. In the first 12 nautical miles it has near-complete control. It makes laws on customs, immigration and pollution, and foreign ships have only innocent passage.

<!-- STEP -->
## The contiguous zone

From 12 out to 24 nautical miles, the country has limited control. Customs means checking goods brought in, fiscal matters means money matters such as taxes, and illegal immigration means people entering without permission.

<!-- STEP -->
## The EEZ

The EEZ reaches 200 nautical miles from the baseline, so it takes in the waters beyond the contiguous zone. Out to 200 nautical miles from the baseline the country has special rights to use the resources, such as fish, oil and gas.

Ships from other countries may still pass through peacefully, and the country does not fully own or control the water. In the game you patrol a made-up coast and act by these rights.
