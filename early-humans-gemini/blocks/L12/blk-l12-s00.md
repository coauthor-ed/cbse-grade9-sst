# The Fourth Civilisation

You have met three civilisations so far: the Harappans, Mesopotamia and Egypt. Each grew up beside rivers, and each has left a different kind of evidence.

<!-- STEP id="turn" -->
## China

The fourth lies far to the east, along two rivers, the Huang He and the Yangtze. The book says there is little solid evidence of direct contact between China and the Harappans.

The oldest writing in Sumer was mostly lists of goods. The earliest known writing in China is different: it is mostly questions, cut into bone.
