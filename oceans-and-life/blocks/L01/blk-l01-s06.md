## Records under the sea

The **Mariana Trench** in the Pacific Ocean is the deepest place on Earth, about 11 km down. The **Mid-Ocean Ridge** is the world's longest mountain range, about 65,000 km long, and it is hidden beneath the ocean.

<!-- STEP -->
## Taller than Everest?

Mount Everest stands 8,849 m above sea level. Mauna Kea in Hawai‘i stands only about 4,200 m above sea level, but most of the mountain is under water. From its base on the sea floor it is about 10,000 m tall.

So Mauna Kea is the taller mountain from foot to top, and Everest is the highest above the sea. Switch the measuring line to see both.
