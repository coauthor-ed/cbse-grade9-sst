# The Bicycle, and a New Habit

<!-- STEP -->
## The bicycle

At the end of month six Meera has ₹3,000, on time, and she buys the bicycle. Saving first worked, so she keeps the habit. Every month she drops ₹100 into the family **gullak**, a clay piggy bank on the kitchen shelf. Coins go in through a slit and stay in until the pot is broken.

The next lesson checks what money kept in a gullak can still buy a year later.

<!-- STEP -->
## Your turn

Make your own budget for one week or one month.

Write down the money that comes in, such as pocket money and gifts. List your expenses and mark each one as a need or a want. Set one savings goal with an amount and a date, and keep that amount aside first. At the end, compare what happened with your plan.
