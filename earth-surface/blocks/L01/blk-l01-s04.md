# Where Does the Heat Come From?

<!-- STEP -->
## The energy is heat from inside

Moving a plate takes energy, and that energy comes from heat inside the Earth. Part of this heat is left from when the Earth formed. Part is still being made, because radioactive rock slowly gives off heat.

The heat moves outward, from the core towards the surface.

<!-- STEP -->
## Four shares of the heat

The Earth's total interior heat flow can be split into four shares.

- **Crust:** 24 per cent
- **Upper mantle:** 22 per cent
- **Lower mantle:** 32 per cent
- **Core:** 22 per cent

The lower mantle has the largest share, though the thin crust is close behind.

<!-- STEP -->
## Three ways heat moves

- **Conduction:** heat passes through solid rock, and the rock stays where it is. A steel spoon in hot chai warms the same way.
- **Convection:** hot material rises and cooler material sinks, in a loop. This happens in the mantle and in the outer core.
- **Advection:** hot material such as magma moves upward and carries its heat with it.
