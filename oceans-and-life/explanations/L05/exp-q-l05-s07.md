# Four parts, four tasks

**Watching.** Cyclone Warning Services and Marine Weather Services monitor storms along both the eastern and western coasts, continuously.

**Preparing.** The National Cyclone Risk Mitigation Project (NCRMP) builds cyclone shelters, strengthens warning systems and improves emergency response.

**Forecasting the water.** INCOIS with IMD set up the Storm Surge Early Warning System (SSEWS), which forecasts storm surges and how far the water will spread.

**Reaching the boats.** GEMINI receives data from the GAGAN satellite and passes it to a phone by Bluetooth. At present this reaches up to 50 nautical miles into the sea.
