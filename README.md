# CBSE Grade 9 Social Science

Courses for [CoAuthor](https://coauthor-ed.github.io/coauthor/), an offline-capable learning app.

## Courses
- Democracy (`democracy`)
- Early Humans and Beginning of Civilisation (`early-humans-gemini`)
- Elections (`elections`)
- The Blanket: Air, Pressure and the Monsoon (`atmosphere-climate`)
- Shaping of the Earth's Surface (`earth-surface`)
- Oceans and Life (`oceans-and-life`)
- State and Society up to 1000 CE (`state-society`)

## Add these courses
1. Open the app: https://coauthor-ed.github.io/coauthor/
2. Open the Parent area, then Settings, then Course sources.
3. Add this address: https://coauthor-ed.github.io/cbse-grade9-sst/bundle-manifest.json

Once the Discover list is live, you can also find these courses in Discover.
