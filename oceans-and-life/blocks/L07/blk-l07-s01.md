## What counts as a marine resource?

Eight things lie on the sand. Some of them are marine resources and some are not. Put each one in a basket, going by what you think the words mean.

<!-- STEP -->
## Materials and features

Six of the eight belong in the marine resource basket. **Marine resources** are the materials and features found in oceans that have intrinsic or economic value. Intrinsic value means a thing is worth keeping for itself, even if nobody sells it.

Many people think a resource must be something you carry out of the sea, so the reef and the tide feel wrong here. Both stay where they are and both count, because visitors come to see the reef and the tide can make electricity.
