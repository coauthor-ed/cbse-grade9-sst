# Three temperature zones

<!-- STEP -->
## Five lines on the globe

Since insolation falls from the equator to the poles, the Earth can be divided into belts. Five lines of latitude mark them:

- Equator, 0°
- Tropic of Cancer, 23½°N, and Tropic of Capricorn, 23½°S
- Arctic Circle, 66½°N, and Antarctic Circle, 66½°S

<!-- STEP -->
## Torrid, temperate, frigid

Sunlight strikes most directly in the **Torrid Zone**, between the two tropics. Between each tropic and its polar circle lies a **Temperate Zone**. Beyond the polar circles, where sunlight comes in at a low angle, are the **Frigid Zones**.

Tap a zone. The Tropic of Cancer runs through the middle of India, so which zones does India lie in?

It lies in two: the Torrid Zone and the North Temperate Zone.
