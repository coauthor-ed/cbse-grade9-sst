## What maritime law does

To keep activities at sea safe, fair and peaceful, countries follow international rules and agreements called **maritime law**. These laws:

- regulate how ships operate and move
- protect the marine environment
- address piracy, terrorism, natural disasters and accidents
- safeguard trade routes
- help settle disputes between countries

## Let's explore: maritime disputes

A maritime dispute is a conflict between countries over territory, resources or rights at sea. Find out about one such dispute from a newspaper or an atlas, and ask these questions. There is no single right answer.

- **What is it about?** Is it about the sea itself, or about the fish, oil or gas in it?
- **How could it be settled?** Talks and courts are two ways. What does each need from the countries?
- **Who else is affected?** Think of the fishers who work in that sea.
