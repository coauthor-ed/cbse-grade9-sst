## Setting the price

The price of a cake has to do three things at once. It must cover what the cake costs to make. It must leave some profit. And it must stay affordable for customers, or they will buy somewhere else.

<!-- STEP -->
## One cake, two prices

Say one cake costs ₹150 to make: flour, butter, electricity and the baker's time. These are made-up numbers. At ₹120, each cake sold loses ₹30. At ₹220, each cake leaves ₹70, and the price is still one many families can pay.

<!-- STEP -->
## A range of prices

A bakery does not have to sell only one thing at one price. Small, cheaper mini cake jars, say at ₹50, sit next to full cakes at ₹350. A child can buy a jar with pocket money, and a parent can order a full cake for a birthday. **A range of prices brings in a wider set of customers.**
