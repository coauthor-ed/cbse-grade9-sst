## Look Around You

Every landform has an agent behind it. Look at the land around your school or home and try to name the agent that made each thing you see.

- Is there a valley or a bank? Think of running water.
- Is there loose sand or dust? Think of wind.
- Is there a hollow where water vanishes? Think of underground water.

<!-- STEP -->
## Make a Model

Try making a model of what underground water creates. Clay can be the rock, and thin rolls of clay can be the stalactites and stalagmites.

- Where will the water enter your model?
- Which parts show rock taken away, and which show rock put down?

We have now seen what shapes the land. Next we ask what happens when that land turns dangerous for the people living on it.
