# The Voters' Pledge

<!-- STEP -->
## 25 January

Every year, 25 January is celebrated as **National Voters' Day**, and there is a voters' pledge that goes as follows:

> "We, the citizens of India, having abiding faith in democracy, hereby pledge to uphold the democratic traditions of our country and the dignity of free, fair and peaceful elections, and to vote in every election fearlessly and without being influenced by considerations of religion, race, caste, community, language or any inducement."

An **inducement** is a gift or reward offered to influence someone.

<!-- STEP -->
## The Same Date

The ECI was established on 25 January 1950, so National Voters' Day falls on the ECI's own date. The picture shows the pledge being taken at a National Voters' Day function in 2012.

**This pledge is one way a citizen takes up the work of keeping elections free and fair.**
