## Human resources

People are the heart of every business. **Human resources** are the people the entrepreneur employs, who add to the business. At ABC Bakery, human resources means hiring bakers, helpers, cleaners and a cashier, and making sure each one has the skills the work needs.

<!-- STEP -->
## Training and a good workplace

Human resources also trains the staff in hygiene and in the bakery's baking standards. It looks after their benefits, settles quarrels at work, and keeps the bakery a comfortable place where people want to work well. In this way it builds a strong team that helps the business grow.
