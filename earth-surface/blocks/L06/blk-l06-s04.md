## The Cliff Moves Back

Waves cut a notch at the foot of the cliff, near the **high-tide level**. The rock above loses its support and falls, and the waves clear it away. So the cliff slowly moves back from the sea.

Drag **Years** to see it.

<!-- STEP -->
## The Platform Left Behind

The waves wear the rock at their own height, not higher up. As the cliff retreats, a flat area of rock is left at its foot. This is a **shore platform**, also called a wave-cut platform.

Tap the labels to find each part.
