# The Monsoon Leaves From the North-West

<!-- STEP -->
## First out of the north-west

The monsoon also leaves a little at a time. The first line of its retreat, **17 September**, lies in the far north-west. The last, **15 October**, crosses the south of the country and runs out over the Bay of Bengal.

<!-- STEP -->
## The last place reached is the first to lose it

The far north-west gets the monsoon last and loses it first. Jodhpur has it for about 11 weeks and Kolkata for about 17.

As the land cools, the low pressure over the north-west weakens first, so the wind eases there first.

<!-- STEP -->
## After the monsoon has gone

In north-western India, October and November are usually cloudless. This is the **post-monsoon** season, or the retreating monsoon.

South India typically receives more rainfall during this time.
