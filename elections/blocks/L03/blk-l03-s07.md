## Laws Need People

A law on paper cannot run an election. People and bodies have to apply it, and each of them has a part to play. These are the **stakeholders**:

- the Election Commission of India (ECI)
- political parties
- civil society
- the media
- the voters

Tap each one to see its part.

## Who Runs It All?

Our Constitution and laws provide for the functions and powers of all these stakeholders.
