# The Stratosphere

<!-- STEP -->
## Clear air for flying

The stratosphere is ideal for flying aeroplanes because it is free of clouds and other weather disturbances.

Long-distance jets cruise at about 10 to 12 km, close to the boundary between the troposphere and the stratosphere and above most clouds.

<!-- STEP -->
## The ozone layer

The stratosphere contains a layer of **ozone** gas. It filters the Sun's harmful radiation, including ultraviolet, so much less of it reaches the ground.

Ozone is one of the smallest shares of the air, yet all of us depend on it.

<!-- STEP -->
## Why this layer gets warmer

When ozone takes in ultraviolet rays, it warms the air around it. This is why the temperature rises as you go up through the stratosphere.
