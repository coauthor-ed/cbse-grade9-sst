# Four Places for Savings

<!-- STEP -->
## Four common options

A family has ₹10,000 that it will not need for ten years. The book names four common places to invest it:

- a **fixed deposit** with a bank or post office
- a **bond**, which lends the money to a government or a company
- **shares** (also called stocks or equity), which make you a part-owner of a company
- a **mutual fund**, which pools money from many people

<!-- STEP -->
## How they differ

They differ in three ways: how safe the money is, how much it may earn, and how soon it can be taken out.

A fixed deposit pays a rate fixed in advance. The price of a share goes up and down, and over many years shares have often earned more. So why do many careful families still choose fixed deposits?
