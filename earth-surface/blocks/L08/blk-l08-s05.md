## Crescents From a Little Sand

**Dunes** are hills or ridges of sand made by the wind, in deserts and along sandy coasts. Their shape depends on the wind, the amount of sand, and any plants.

With little sand and one wind direction, a crescent forms. This is a **barchan**. Its two horns point **downwind**, the way the wind is going.

<!-- STEP -->
## Long Ridges, Two Ways

Sand can also lie in long ridges, and the ridges can run two ways. Press **Across** and **Along** to compare them.

- **Transverse dunes** run across the wind, where there is plenty of sand.
- **Longitudinal dunes** run along the wind, parallel to it.

The Thar has barchans and longitudinal dunes.

<!-- STEP -->
## Winds From Many Sides

When winds come from different directions, sand is pushed back and forth around one high point. The dune grows several arms, like a star. This is a **star dune**.

<!-- STEP -->
## Plants Hold the Arms

A **parabolic dune** is U-shaped and is held by plants. It is easy to mistake for a barchan, but it points the other way.

Plants hold the two arms, while the bare nose in the middle moves on downwind. So the arms trail behind and point **upwind**. Press **Blow** to see it.

## All Five Together

Tap a name to light its dune.

| Dune | What it needs | What you see |
|---|---|---|
| Barchan | one wind, little sand | crescent, horns downwind |
| Transverse | one wind, plenty of sand | ridges across the wind |
| Longitudinal | one main wind | ridges along the wind |
| Star | winds from many sides | arms around a high point |
| Parabolic | one wind, plants | U shape, arms upwind |
