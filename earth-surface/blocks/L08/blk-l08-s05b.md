## How a Dune Moves

A dune has two slopes. Wind pushes sand up the long, gentle **windward slope**. At the top the grains tumble over and slide down the steep **slip face**.

Sand leaves the back of the dune and lands on the front, so the whole dune moves downwind. Press **Blow** to watch it.

## Why Dunes Matter

Dunes are useful to people in several ways.

- They act as natural barriers against desertification and wind erosion.
- Along some coasts they protect settlements from strong sea winds and waves.
- They bring tourists and adventure sports, as at Sam near Jaisalmer.
- Their sand is sometimes used for building.
