# The Thermosphere

<!-- STEP -->
## Heated by the Sun's rays

The thermosphere extends from 80 km to 700 km. Temperature rises very rapidly here, because gas molecules in this layer absorb the Sun's **X-rays** and **short-wave ultraviolet radiation**.

<!-- STEP -->
## Radio waves come back

Part of the thermosphere is called the **ionosphere**. It reflects radio waves sent up from the Earth back towards it, and this helps radio transmission.
