## Three small starts

The young man in the Jataka tale began with a dead mouse. The seven Lijjat founders began with ₹80, borrowed equipment and a recipe. Mohan Singh Oberoi began as a desk clerk earning ₹50 a month, and later risked his savings on one small hotel.

<!-- STEP -->
## What each one built

The young man became very wealthy and helped communities in his city. Lijjat grew to over 45,000 women members and a turnover of ₹1,600 crore a year. Oberoi built one of India's largest hotel chains.

**None of the three began with much money, and each grew one step at a time.**
