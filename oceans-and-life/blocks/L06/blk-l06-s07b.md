## Five steps for your family

If a tsunami or cyclone alert came to your phone right now, would your family know what to do? These five steps are a good start:

1. Read the whole alert calmly and tell everyone at home.
2. Follow what officials say, and leave at once if you are told to.
3. For a tsunami, go to high ground, or the upper floors of a strong building, only if high ground is too far to reach. For a cyclone, go to a shelter or a strong building before the wind grows.
4. Take only a small bag of water, medicines, papers and a phone, and help those who move slowly.
5. Stay there until officials say it is safe, even if the danger seems to be over.

Talk with your family about where your nearest safe place is.
