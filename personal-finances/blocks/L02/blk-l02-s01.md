# The Family Gullak

<!-- STEP -->
## A year of coins

Meera's family keeps a gullak on the kitchen shelf. All year, Meera drops in ₹100 a month, her mother adds the change from the vegetable market, and her younger brother adds coins he is given at a wedding.

At the end of the year they break the pot and count: Meera ₹1,200, her mother ₹2,000 and her brother ₹400. That makes **₹3,600**, and not one coin is missing.

<!-- STEP -->
## Saving

What the family did is **saving**: keeping aside a part of your income for later use. Savings build up over time, as the gullak did.

People save because life is uncertain and an emergency can come at any time, to live more comfortably, and to invest later. The next question is whether the ₹3,600 still buys as much as it did a year ago.
