## Ground That Is Ready to Lift

A **dust storm** starts with the ground, before the wind arrives. Long drought and low rainfall dry out the soil. Press **Long drought** and watch the field.

Sparse plant cover, often caused by cutting trees, overgrazing or poor farming, leaves the land exposed.

<!-- STEP -->
## The Wind Arrives

Press **Strong wind**. It lifts large amounts of loose, dry soil and sand into the air.

Climate change and extreme weather can make dust storms happen more often and grow stronger.

<!-- STEP -->
## Where, and What Helps

Dust storms are common in desert and semi-arid regions. In India they strike Rajasthan, Delhi and north-west India in summer. In May 2018, dust storms hit Rajasthan and Uttar Pradesh.

Belts of trees slow the wind, and ground cover holds the soil. In a dust storm, stay indoors, close the windows, and cover your mouth and nose.
