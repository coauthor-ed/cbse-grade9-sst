## India's startups today

With consistent hard work, India today is the **third-largest startup ecosystem** in the world. An ecosystem here means a country's startups and everything around them that helps them grow. It is powered by India's young and talented population. In 2025, India had around 120 **unicorns**: startups valued at over 1 billion dollars.

## Fields where startups work

The startup world is growing fast, driven by creativity and help from technology. Startups work in fields such as e-commerce (online shopping), fin-tech (digital payments), health-tech (online healthcare) and agri-tech (farming technology).

## Startup hubs

Cities like Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Mumbai and Delhi NCR have become major startup hubs: places where many startups work. These startups are creating jobs, supporting sustainability and driving economic growth. Sustainability means meeting today's needs without harming the needs of people in the future.
