## What a fisher can read

The app gives important information in the fisher's own language:

- alerts about high waves and strong winds
- cyclone and tsunami warnings
- advisories on potential fishing zones
- current speeds
- bathymetry data, which is the mapped depth of the sea
- mapping and risk management information

## Help to buy a device

Under the Pradhan Mantri Matsya Sampada Yojana (PMMSY), the government gives financial support for GEMINI devices. This improves the safety of coastal communities while they are at sea.
