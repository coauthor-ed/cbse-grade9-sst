## Do currents change the temperature of a coast?

Yes, and you have already seen two cases. The warm Gulf Stream keeps the sea off Norway relatively warm, and the cold Labrador Current brings sea ice to Canada's coast. Tap a name to light that current on the map.

<!-- STEP -->
## Warm and cold side by side

Japan has both. The warm Kuroshio comes up from warmer seas to the south, while the cold Oyashio comes down from the north. They flow towards each other along the east side of the islands.

<!-- STEP -->
## Not every current brings warmth

You might expect any current to warm a coast, but the Humboldt (Peru) Current does the opposite. It carries cold water north along the west coast of South America, so it cools the coast beside it.

**A current tends to carry the warmth or cold of the place it came from.**
