# Meera's Month

<!-- STEP -->
## One month on the board

Meera's ₹1,500 comes in, and the savings row is filled first: ₹500. The rest pays for the month: bus ₹300, stationery ₹200, lunch ₹300 and snacks ₹200. Bus fare, stationery and lunch are **needs**, because the school day does not work without them. Snacks are a **want**, because the day works without them.

<!-- STEP -->
## Month 4: the bus fare rises

In month 4 the bus fare rises to ₹350, so the board is ₹50 over the ₹1,500 that comes in. The ₹50 must come out of another row. Savings stays, because the bicycle is due in month six, so snacks drop from ₹200 to ₹150.

**When a cut is needed, a want gives way before a need or the savings goal.**

<!-- STEP -->
## The racket breaks

The strings of Meera's badminton racket break, and a restring costs ₹300. She has three ways to pay.

She can cut back, with no snacks for two months. She can use her savings, and the bicycle comes later. Or she can borrow from her parents and pay them back next month. Tap each card: every way has a cost.
